Suggestion box

This is where suggestions for making the biz-OC-doll site easier to use are collected.

Accepted

  • Things that were inconvenient while using the site
  • Features that would make things easier for everyone
  • Suggestions about how events are run

Not accepted

  • Suggestions about characters' settings, stories, or relationships
  • New character ideas or design requests
  • Posts arguing over which take on a character is right
  • Posts that single out a specific Holder or work
  • Insults, put-downs, or attacks on other people
  • Complaints or protests with no alternative offered

Posts like these are not published.

What gets adopted

Things that keep running on their own once built are taken on. It doesn't matter whether it's a small fix or a new feature.

  • Fixes like a button's position, a line of wording, or one awkward behavior
  • Features for the character list, like grouping, filtering, searching, or sorting

Things the official side would have to keep tending after they're built are not taken on.

  • Sections that need new content on a regular basis
  • Features where the official side has to check or pick each new entry
  • Events where requests are taken and handled one by one
  • Fandom housekeeping, like lists of Holder names, collections of works, or tag cleanup

This project keeps its operation light as a rule. Not every suggestion on the accepted side gets adopted.

Goes elsewhere

  • TITLE HOLDER name changes, payments, refunds: Ko-fi message
  • Company and collaboration proposals: business email
  • Registering serials and commercial works: the registration form

Do not write personal information here, such as real names, contact details, or payment records.

How the official side takes part

  • Posts are read from time to time.
  • No replies are written. Results are shown only through status marks.
  • Good replies to a suggestion may get a mark.
  • Agree counts are for reference only. A high count doesn't mean a suggestion gets adopted.

Status marks

Read · Under review · Adopted · Not adopted

Other

  • This suggestion box has no translation feature. Write in any language.
  • Posts that collect reports are folded automatically.
  • The official side may freely refer to and adopt posted suggestions. No reward or name credit is given when one is adopted.
  • The suggestion box may be closed or cleared depending on how things are going.
