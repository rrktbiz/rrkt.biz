How to Play
How it works

biz-OC-doll is an open character project: anyone can take its characters and use them.
Once you've picked one you like, here's how to enjoy it.

Become a biz-OC-doll's Holder!

What's a Holder?
A biz-OC-doll starts out with nothing but a code. The moment you give one a name, you become that biz-OC-doll's Holder. The character you named is entirely yours.

What biz-OC-doll Official gives you
  • A unique code
  • A character sheet
  • A few expressions
What the Holder decides
  • Name · Personality
  • Way of talking · Story
  • Age · Height
  • Everything else
For example

Say you give No. 042 the name "A." From that moment on, the character is A.

Even with the same No. 042, every Holder can choose a completely different name and a completely different take on the character. All the characters born this way count as the same No. 042.

Once A has a name, you can make A the lead of your web novel, cast A in the game you're making, put out a comic or zine starring A and sell it at a convention, or stream as a VTuber with A's look. Where and how you use A is all up to you, the Holder.

One thing, though: a named character belongs to its Holder. If you'd like to use another Holder's character, please ask that Holder for permission first.

Bonus
Still No. 042, even with a new look

Maybe you want to put A in your own work, but A's eye color is the same as your main character's, and that gives you pause. In that case, feel free to change A's eye color. Whether it's to fit your work or just your taste, you can freely customize A with a new outfit, a different age, a different species, or recoloring parts like the eyes.

My fave, my canon

You may meet Holders who enjoy the same code in a completely different way. Don't let it shake you. No one is right or wrong here, and we hope this becomes a place where everyone loves their own character and has more fun than anyone else. biz-OC-doll Official won't interfere with how you use a character that has a name, either. You don't have to draw, and you don't have to write. All a biz-OC-doll needs is a Holder's love. In fandom, confidence is everything, so go all in.

Guideline
The ground rules
OK
  • Naming · Creating settings
  • Art · Writing · Video · Games
  • Making and selling your own merch
  • Publishing paid posts
  • Serializing on platforms · Releasing commercial games
  • Featuring in other works
  • VTuber activities
NG
  • Real-world political propaganda · Promoting hate · Aiming to stir up conflictCharacters' identities and relationships are yours to set
  • Use in real-world religious outreach materialsReligious settings within a story are fine
  • Use that makes people mistake it for official or original workWebcomics, merch, and pop-ups with your own name and take are welcome

biz-OC-doll Official does not ask for a share of profits. The rights to a character a Holder has named, and to the works made with it, belong to that Holder. biz-OC-doll Official will not use them without permission or claim ownership of them. We may reach out first if we'd like to collaborate, but we'll never push it.

We don't review works one by one. Just steer clear of the three NG items above.

We don't restrict the use of AI as a creative tool. The same goes for going further, as long as it doesn't hit the three NG items. Since the official side won't be the one judging, that call is yours as the creator.

If the rules ever change, the changes won't apply to names given or works made before then.

Content rating is the creator's responsibility. Please handle age-restriction labels and viewing limits yourself. The official side plans to keep things SFW, so works with mature content won't be used for merch or game tie-ins.

Politics & religion: what's allowed and what isn't
Allowed Freely setting a character's identity, such as gender, sexual orientation, race, or nationality, and relationships, such as romance, family, or friendship. Political settings in a fictional world (kingdoms, elections, wars, and so on) are also allowed.
Not allowed Using a character as promotional material or a propaganda tool for a real-world political party, candidate, or campaign.
Allowed Having a character appear with a religious background (a priest, a nun, a Buddhist, and so on) or wear related attire within any kind of content or story (web novels, webcomics, illustrations, videos, games, and more). Works built on top of those are allowed too.
Not allowed Using a character without permission in outreach materials (posters, flyers, and so on) for a real-world religion. Turning a character into a symbol of a specific religion and using it as a propaganda tool is not allowed.
