▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 9

At Aoi's answer, Eunho and Miko were left speechless for a while. Eunho was struck by a shock that turned his mind white.

'He...... forgave? Everything......?'

Eunho realized.
This man had never intended to take 'revenge' in the first place.

Swish—Aoi rose from his seat.

"I take it there's no further business. You may go."

Aoi gave an order to the girl waiting outside the door.

"Yuka. See our guests out."

Eunho and Miko were flustered.

"Wait!"

At Eunho's cry, Aoi stopped.
Eunho spoke resolutely.

"I know that not everything can be resolved simply. But we want to carry on Yoshiyuki's will."

Beside him, Miko, too, backed Eunho's words with a firm expression. Aoi had his back turned, so his face could not be seen.

Eunho kept going.

"......I was planning to tell Kuro of the Kagehira family's existence before long. And together—"
"...!"

In that instant, the color drained rapidly from Miko's face, and she cut in.

"It is I who will inherit the headship!"
"!?"

It happened in an instant.
Eunho looked at Miko, and his face twisted.

"...?"

The expression on Aoi's face, as he slowly turned his head, was ice cold.
But Miko did not stop.

"I am Ryuo Miko. As the daughter of Ryuo Ren, I want to take responsibility! The Ryuo family's businesses are in my hands. I excel both as a head and as a manager. I will keep the Ryuo family from losing its essence and rotting, and I will take it to the very top and make it a family of influence."

Miko held forth in a heated voice.
Eunho broke into a cold sweat and screamed inwardly.

"And with that power, I want to give the Kagehira family as much support as I can, and make amends!"

Miko finished.
In a state of extreme tension, Eunho studied Aoi's expression.

"......................."

Aoi's reaction went beyond cold; one could feel murderous intent in it.

"Hmm... responsibility, is it."

At Aoi's frighteningly low voice, Eunho and Miko trembled with fear.

*

Aoi sat back down.

"Very well. Listen."

Covering his mouth with his fan once more, he began his story.

"After Ryuo Ren died, the Kagehira family's reputation was cracked, an' the employers who wanted us dwindled. Unable to support its numbers, the Kagehira family... rotted through, completely."

Having thrown out these words, Aoi deliberately let a pause fall in the conversation and fixed his gaze on Miko.

Miko, though breaking into a cold sweat under that gaze, darted her eyes about, desperately trying to read the situation. He could see her struggling to hold her expression in place, betraying a childlike determination 'not to lose this battle of words,' whatever it took.

Next, Aoi's gaze turned to Eunho. Eunho seemed to have already grasped the weight of the word 'rotted' and the horror hidden behind it. He bit down on his lip and lowered his head, despair in his eyes.

Only after reading that expression on Eunho's face did Aoi at last feel the conversation was worth having, and went on.

"The former head died, an' the moment I took over that seat, the Kagehira family returned to its original way."
"Its original way...?"

When Miko asked back, Aoi explained 'the original way.'

[ "Those who kill by curses must not fear their own death. If the only power you possess is your own life, accept your sacrifice for the family. This is a place where those nearer to death than to life have gathered. To place value on the way of dying one has chosen—that is the pride of this family." ]

"That is the original way of the Kagehira family. The destiny of those who use forbidden arts, you might say; our family, for whom a life is all we have to offer, can't stand on equal footing with other families. There was the former head's example, too. I learned it well."

Aoi wore a bitter expression.

"So for seven years, the Kagehira family joined hands with no one, an' kept itself goin' on commissions alone. After all that effort, we won back the trust of the other families an' became the largest power among the shadow families."

And Aoi added the cold reality.

"....But no matter how hard I tried, erasin' what the former head left behind was impossible. Those who remain number thirty thousand."

Eunho and Miko were aghast.
There was no way a single family could have such numbers.
Aoi went on as though he had expected that reaction.

"Before joinin' hands with Ren, the Kagehira family was a house carried on by a small bloodline. But after it did, the family's very identity changed completely. The Kagehira of today ain't bound by blood; it's a mass of people drawn together by blind doctrine. No different from a giant religion."

Aoi's gaze turned to Miko.
It was no sneer, only a cold, objective look at reality.

"The Ryuo family, with its blood-centered family business, wouldn't understand how things are built over here.... but castin' them out would've been the same as throwin' tens of thousands of people to their deaths."

Aoi's eyes narrowed.

"......As luck would have it, this yoke ain't easily slipped. The Kagehira family will never disappear."

He stood his fan upright on the table with a sharp 'tack!'
Leaning forward, Aoi brought his face close to Miko's and continued.

"To take 'responsibility' for a Kagehira family like that, an' 'make amends.' It's come to mean havin' to answer even for other people's sins."


***

Miko's mind went blank.

Over the past eight years, it was not only the Ryuo family that had changed.

Miko's mind spun rapidly in search of a clear answer. First, she thought of the business expansion that a number like thirty thousand would make possible. To Miko, whose heart was set on management, they had, before she knew it, come to look like an enormous 'resource.'

But the Kagehira family before her was a group moved not by capital but purely by 'doctrine.' A group bound together by such fierce conviction lay outside the realm of what Miko could calculate.

'That number isn't something the Ryuo family's strength can resolve. Far from the Ryuo family rising,'

Miko felt the terror of having sunk into a swamp, with someone's hands entwining her feet.

The enormous karma of thirty thousand people. It was a trigger that Ryuo Ren had pulled in the past, but with the passing years, it had become a vast darkness, swollen from absorbing the sins of countless people.

Ren was already dead. And so there was no knowing even who the evil was that must be eliminated for these people's sake. Along with her terror, Miko felt the injustice of it. So then, just as Aoi said, to take responsibility for such a Kagehira family and make amends would be......

'That would be, all too plainly, self-destruction.'


*

"Scared?"
"!"

At Aoi's quiet question, Miko, pulled back to reality, jerked her head up.

"That man always looked down on us from above. As if this swamp were no place for the likes of him."

Over Miko's figure, Aoi saw the afterimage of Ren.

"The daughter of Ryuo Ren, Ryuo Miko."

Having already finished his background check, Aoi began to recite Miko's information indifferently.

"A born elite, a prodigy known as the 'Crimson Shrine Maiden,' the talk of all the sorcery families.... and 'Yoshiyuki's disciple.'"
"...!"
".....It seems a teacher's lessons are no match for the blood of one's own father."
"...!!!!!!"

Miko pushed back at once.

"......!! I am...... nothing like my father!!"

Aoi answered at once, coolly.

"I'm sure. I'm sure you've struggled to live differently. For the sake of a transparent Ryuo family, unsullied from here on, in the more rational, tidier form of 'responsibility' an' 'amends.' Did you want to shake off the guilt your father left you an' restore your honor? An' that's why you 'have to' become the head of the Ryuo family, isn't it?"

And finally, Aoi struck at Miko's core.

"An' what lies at the end of that?"

Miko could not answer.

The family's recognition, the revival of the Ryuo family, setting right its rotten spirit and carrying on a healthy family business. But the end of all of it came down, ultimately, to 'recognition of me.'

Miko had always been a girl brimming with certainty. To Eunho or Kuro, she would have stated this truth with pride. Isn't everyone like this, she would have said.

But in this situation, she could not say that naked truth to Aoi. She could have dressed it up with all sorts of logic, and yet her lips simply would not part.

'......'

The sensation of herself growing endlessly small was so unpleasant that her thoughts leapt elsewhere. The Aoi before her had seen Yoshiyuki only once, and yet he spoke as though he knew the whole of Yoshiyuki's will. When she was the one who was Yoshiyuki's disciple. The words that had raked Miko so badly—'the father's blood runs thicker than the teacher'—were like a death sentence declaring that the entire road she had walked was wrong. Once again she had been met not with recognition, but with denial.

'What do you know.'

In a flash of anger, Miko clenched her fist.
But then she thought of her teacher.

—Would the end her teacher, Master Yoshiyuki, reached have been different from hers? Even if so, Miko could not escape herself. No, she must not escape. By the reckoning of Miko's clever mind, that would plainly be 'the wrong answer.'

Kuro came to mind. There was no way the mentality of that weak, foolish boy could be right. Kuro was a lump of wrong answers. So there was no way she could be wrong. No way—and yet before this man in front of her, of all people, her mouth would not open.

*

"......"

Aoi, without so much as the sound of a breath, stared piercingly at Miko with a chill expression.

He pressed her, giving her no time even to breathe.
As if to leave a mark she would never forget.

"......"

A heavy silence hung in the room for a long time.
Miko's breathing grew ragged. Eunho sensed it.

'At this rate, Miko's spirit will break..!!'

Eunho mustered his courage and broke the silence.

*

"Forgive our rudeness!!"

Eunho bowed his head deeply and went on.

"You're right. This child has been scorned by the Ryuo family all her life for the sins of her parents. She is a child who, simply to prove herself and be recognized, to protect the Ryuo family, has been desperate for nothing but to grow strong ever since her teacher Yoshiyuki passed on."

Miko looked at Eunho with a contorted expression.
She resented Eunho for laying bare her shame, and at the same time, the fact that he was trying to protect her put a crack in her pride.

"....."

Silence fell.

"....."

Miko lowered her head without a word.
Somehow, the sight was familiar to Aoi.

'A child.. who can't even hide her own sharpness..'

Aoi remembered.

His past self, wailing, 'I'm gonna get out! Outta this cursed house!!'

"....."

Aoi rose from his seat.

"......I'm the one who got too emotional."

The mood settled into calm.

Aoi walked toward the door, preparing to see the two off himself, and said evenly.

".....There's none of the Kagehira blood left here. They all died young. Now only those who were brought here remain. Myself included."
"!!"

Miko's and Eunho's eyes widened.

One who did not share the Kagehira blood
had appointed himself the gatekeeper of this hell.

"I always wanted to give them a new life. But most of 'em come back here again an' wait for a peaceful death. Because to them, this place is hell an' at the same time their only haven."

Laying a hand on the sliding door, Aoi left his final words.

"So I have to stay here until I die. As head."

Slam.

The door of the Kagehira house closed firmly.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 9]
