▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 12

Before the grave of Yoshiyuki and his wife.
Eunho sat there, staring into space.
Miko came walking up behind Eunho, who was lost in deep thought.

"To think we had the same idea. Gross."

At Miko's barbed words, Eunho let out a sigh. "Haah."

***

The two sat side by side before the grave.
Before they knew it, the sun was setting.

"...It's already been eight years since Master Yoshiyuki passed away."
"....."

The two were lost in deep thought for a while.
At length, Miko spoke.

"You know, I don't think I was all that wrong. Shouldering even the sins other people committed—what is that, are you stupid? Normally, no one would—"

Miko stopped, then went on.

"Haah. Kuro. He just went and took Master Yoshiyuki's path, like it was the most natural thing in the world."

Eunho listened to Miko in silence.
Miko's gaze turned to the empty air.

"...And somewhere along the way, I became a disciple walking a different path from her teacher."

Silence.
And Miko admitted, weakly, in a dejected voice.

"...The truth is, I might not even remember him anymore. Master Yoshiyuki."

Only ten years old. Her time of being taught had ended far too soon.
And Miko, simply because she was clever, had looked squarely at the world rather than at those teachings.

Eunho raised his head.

"Miko."
"?"
"Come back to the Ryuo family."

A cool wind blew beneath the red evening glow.
Leaves whirled in disarray, and backlight fell on the figures of the two sitting side by side before the grave.


***

By now, a good many of the Kagehira family's people had moved into the Ryuo house. Every day they were making an effort to break down the boundary between the families and blend into one another's territory.

Herbaeken, too, moved into the Ryuo house at Kuro's suggestion.
There were plenty of empty rooms in the house, after all.

Grandpa pouted, as if the changed atmosphere of the family was not to his liking.

"Do you have some complaint?"

Just then, the murderous face of Lackness appeared.
Grandpa jumped out of his skin and bowed his head low.

"N-no, not at all!!!! That you would so much as spare the life of this sinful old man is more than I deserve!!!!!!"

Lackness: an estimated ten thousand years old.
Being older than Grandpa, he was, in a reversal, receiving the respect due to one's elders.

Those watching the scene were thoroughly cowed.
Shiro in particular, now that his brother had grown scarier, began walking on eggshells all the more. The fear that 'I'll die too if I do wrong' had been carved into him.

Herbaeken whispered to Eunho.

"Is that really Kuro? He's so firm.."
"Kuro's always been like that..."

Just then, a familiar voice came from afar.

"Now that is refreshing~"

Miko's entrance. Yuka stood behind her.
Miko showed a satisfied smile at the way Kuro handled things.

***

Meanwhile, evening at the Ryuo house.
While a hot pot party, that cliché of Japanese anime, was underway,
Miko began laying out the Ryuo family's situation.

"The Ryuo family's assets—I've finished transferring all of them over to our side. And the matters that need an adult have all been handled through Aoi."

Miko looked across the table and asked.

"And your side?"

Aoi and Yuka, seated across from her.
Aoi went into a recollection.

"The rumor goin' 'round among the shadow families 'bout the Ryuo family an' the Kagehira family.
'The new head of the Ryuo family, outta guilt, is lettin' his fool of a family get used by the Kagehira family'—is how it goes."

At those words, Eunho and Kuro simultaneously made an "Ugh...." face.

It wasn't exactly wrong, either. The current course of dividing its assets with the Kagehira family to cleanse the Ryuo family's karma might well look, to others, like nothing so much as being 'a sucker.'

"The Kagehira family's rare move did draw suspicion, since we've always drawn a hard line between ourselves an' other families. But the Kagehira family holds a fierce hatred an' thirst for revenge toward the Ryuo family over what happened in the past, so it'll strip the Ryuo family of everythin' an' then throw it away. We're movin' so that's what they'll think."

Aoi added.

"The Ryuo family ain't all that strong a family right now anyway, so it's not drawin' any special wariness."
"......."

That last remark stung Miko.
Yuka's calm voice followed.

"In addition, there has been no progress on 'finding the mastermind within the Ryuo family.' It appears it will take more time to get a grasp on it."

Finding the mastermind who had killed Yoshiyuki. A number of the Kagehira family's people had concealed their identities and worked their way into every one of the Ryuo family's business sites. It was infiltration on a colossal scale—like living CCTV.

Aoi went on, as if enjoying himself.

"No matter how ya manage things, traitors always crop up, y'see~ Ya gotta catch 'em early, before the rot spreads... but that sorta thing's the Kagehira family's specialty, so I'm confident~♪"

When Aoi gave a chilling smile, no one asked any further.
Somehow, it seemed that if they asked more, some horrifying story of torture might come popping out.


*

"All right. We leave the back end to the Kagehira family. It's the Ryuo family's turn to move forward."

The mood flipped.
All eyes focused on Miko.

"I haven't given up on the Ryuo family rising to the top, you know? Whatever the circumstances, I'll make it happen—!!!"

Miko laughed a mad laugh, like a villain.
With eyes blazing, her fists tightened.

Ryuo Miko was tenacious. She had already overcome the brief collapse of her spirit not long ago, and had hauled up an ambition even greater than before. ૮( ᵒ̌皿ᵒ̌ )ა GUOOOH!!

"...So we're really doing it.."

Eunho went pale.

"Eh, what? What is it?"

Flustered, Kuro's eyes darted this way and that.

"Kuro.. there's one more... power you have to accept."

At Eunho's words, Kuro's eyes went round.

"Eh? I've got something else besides Lackness's power..?"

Eunho slowly walked up to Kuro and tapped his glasses with a finger. In that instant, with a 'ting-', the spell came undone.

"—and there, no more spirit effect."
"Eh?"


***

May.
Unlike usual, the front of the Ryuo shrine was bustling with crowds.

It was the 'Ryuo Family Revival Festival,' planned to rebuild the family and announce the inauguration of its new head far and wide.

On stage, the Ryuo family's 'twin Dragon Gods' were dancing to cute music, and cheers erupted from the audience.

"Ehh, what, what, twin Dragon Gods? They're super cute~!"
"Little Shiro's idol power is amazing~"
"The older brother's super embarrassed, look, ehh~!!! My heart's on fire~~!!"

Shiro was enjoying the stage two hundred percent, showing off his flair, while Kuro, embarrassed as he was, danced earnestly without getting a single move wrong. Below the stage, Miko bustled about as director, and Yuka as staff.

"Did the Ryuo family always do stuff like this? I thought they were stuffy, but this is fresh!!"
"Rumor has it the young ones took over and are running it."

Perceptions of the Ryuo family were beginning to change.
In people's hands was not the tacky pamphlet of the past, but a stylish one.

Its contents: along with the line 'These twins have real Dragon Gods inside them!', the 'Dragon God profiles' of Lackness and Lumensia.

[Before transformation: Kuro - glasses -> After transformation: Lackness - side hair grows 20cm and glasses vanish.]
[Before transformation: Shiro - eyepatch -> After transformation: Lumensia - side hair grows 20cm and eyepatch vanishes.]

Appearance meta-information, helpfully comparing even the before-and-after differences. At the bottom of the pamphlet were printed the Ryuo family's dedicated messenger ID and a QR code for the official site. What's more, over at the shrine's merchandise shop, all kinds of 'twin dragon goods' were flying off the shelves.

Click.

The lights and music cut out, and before the puzzled audience, the MC spoke.

"Now, thank you for waiting! At last, it is time to welcome the star of this Revival Festival, the guardian deity our Ryuo family enshrines!!! The Dragon God!"

The audience buzzed with anticipation.

A moment later, the intro of a song began to play. It was a so-called 'metal-flavored' track, blending the traditional Japanese koto with a powerful beat. To it, the two dragons showed off flawless, razor-sharp synchronized choreography. As the song reached its climax, the two dragons breathed out flames and even put on a spectacular fire show.

"Waaaaah—!!!"

At the flood of positive reactions to the Ryuo family's special stage, the corners of Miko's mouth lifted. Dreaming up all manner of events and merchandise strategies, Miko laughed like a villain.

'Full speed ahead!!! At this rate, we're aiming for the summer festival!!!'


*

Watching the scene, Eunho was appalled.
Beside him, Herbaeken just looked on with a "heeh—".
Eunho sighed and spoke.

"Kuro's hated being the center of attention ever since he was little, so.. I put a spell on his glasses to make him look or-di-nary. The only ones who'd notice his real face are people with spiritual power strong enough to see through the spell....."

Kuro's real face was that of a strikingly cute, pretty boy.
At Eunho's explanation, it clicked for Herbaeken.

"No wonder! They're identical twins, and yet something felt oddly different, so I thought it was strange."

Just then, someone whispered in Eunho's ear.

"'I wanted li'l Kuro's cuteness to be mine alone to know'—too bad~♪"

Eunho got goosebumps and turned around.
It was Aoi.

"Ain't that right, Lord Spirit?"
"I-I never thought anything of the sort..!"

Eunho was flustered by Aoi's words and scrunched up his face.
With a troubled expression, Eunho looked at the stage again.
The twin Dragon Gods, who had become the Ryuo family's mascots.

Having launched its new branding, the Ryuo family had chosen the image of a 'friendly guardian deity' over 'worship of the Dragon God tinged with awe.' Friendly, yet showing the Dragon God's majesty at the finish—in other words, a 'gap moe' strategy.

Kuro truly hated standing in front of people, but he gritted his teeth and took the stage. Foreseeing all the tiresome things Kuro would go through from here on, Eunho felt a pang.. but..

".....But whatever it takes, the Ryuo family has to give it everything from now on."

Miko shouted toward them.

"This season was way too dark, and there wasn't enough 'moe'!!!! Fix it!!!!!!!!!!!"
"....What is she even talking about...;;"

Eunho fired a jab at Miko's meta remark.
But moe was, undeniably, important.


***

Late one evening.
Kuro was giving it his all as head of the Ryuo family.

"It's about time for bed, Kuro."

At the words of Eunho, who had appeared in the doorway, Kuro checked the clock.

"Ah~.. okay."


*

Washed up and lying in bed, Kuro, drunk with sleep, spoke half-dreaming, in a stream of consciousness.

"How does Miko manage to do all of this.."

Eunho, tidying up beside him, answered.

"Don't compare yourself to Miko.. That girl's seriously something else.."

Kuro shifted in bed and brought up something from a little deeper down.

"...Hey, Eunho.. how long do you mean to stay by my side? Even if it's Dad's request.. a spirit is.. supposed to protect the town from the forest, isn't he."

Eunho answered as if it were nothing.

"I guarded it for over a thousand years. I'm allowed this much vacation~"
"Eh, what's that supposed to mean..."

Kuro mumbled, looking as though he might fall asleep any second.

"If that's so, I'm glad, but.. hogging everyone's Fox Spirit all to myself.. that feels really unfair....."

Having said that, Kuro fell right asleep.

After making sure Kuro was asleep, Eunho quietly gazed at him. And he recalled what Lackness had said to him.

[ "What have you done to me?" ]

Eunho's eyes narrowed softly.
They were the eyes of the Fox Spirit.

'....That's what I should be saying. Kuro.'


***

Deep in the night,
'That Being,' whom Herbaeken had encountered in the cathedral, stood far off on high, contemplating the Ryuo family.

< "If you have decided what to believe in and what to fight for, let us proceed to the next play. Lackness." >

Meanwhile,
Herbaeken held the materials on the dragon legend.

< "Once more, to take hold of this one (眞ノ鍵)." >

*眞ノ鍵 (Shin no Kagi) - the True Key

The succession of the dragon clan, drawn in the materials,
and the picture, partly torn away,
of the dragon clan holding something aloft.

In that instant.

Lackness, lying in bed, snapped his eyes open.
