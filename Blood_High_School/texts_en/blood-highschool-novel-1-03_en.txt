▶Ep. 3 The Jewel of the Abandoned Holy Site

The athletic grounds of St. Saints Academy.

Eunho bounced a basketball.
Three defenders blocked his way, only to crumble before his dazzling dribble.
Seizing the opening, Eunho slipped through and tossed up an easy shot.

Swish—!

It traced a clean arc, straight through the hoop.
At the same moment, the girls in the gym erupted in shrieks—kyaa!

"Nice one, Eunho—!!"
"Yaay~!"

Eunho, high-fiving his teammates.

Usually he was too busy slyly teasing Kuro, but in this moment alone he was a refreshing, cheerful sports boy... a scene straight out of the springtime of youth.

"Isn't Eunho, like, kind of cool?"

Hearing the girls' fuss, Kuro's eyes went round.

'Huh?'

Kuro was merciless, without the slightest flutter of the heart for his childhood friend.

After the game, as Eunho was wiping off his sweat, a group of big, mean-looking upperclassmen came up to him.

"Oi! You there, boy!"

Wiping his sweat with a towel, Eunho answered without flinching.

"? Yup."

At that instant.

"Please!! Come join the basketball club!"
"No, he's joining our soccer club!"
"No, the volleyball club—!!"

They turned out to be the captains of the sports clubs.

'Uh-oh~!'

With a look that said he'd been caught by the wrong crowd, Eunho clapped his hands together toward the captains and answered.

"Sorry, sorry! I'm in the go-home club, so—!"
"Aw, come on~! At least hear us out..~!"

Eunho shook off the captains and bolted.


***

"I should really quit filling in as a sub~ It's such a pain turning them down every time~."

Eunho grumbled as he straightened his clothes.

"Why don't you join any club when you've got talent like that? What a waste.."

When Kuro said it with genuine regret, Eunho flashed a grin and answered playfully.

"Hmm.. 'cause Kuro's in the go-home club? Just kidding☆"

Eunho threw him a wink.

"Geez~ quit messing around~ Why is it my fault~?"
"lol"

Naive Kuro couldn't even pick up on the affection in Eunho's teasing. Eunho laughed, because he found that adorable.

Just when the mood was at its best.

"If you're both in the go-home club, then you can both just join the Legend Research Club!"

It was Herbaeken. A thick stack of paper was tucked under his arm. This looked ominous.
A vein popping on his forehead, Eunho smiled through gritted teeth and asked.

"......Did this academy even have a club like that~?"
"I made it~ Now recruiting members~!"

With the air of someone who would never back down, Herbaeken thrust out a crude, hand-drawn poster of a dragon.

[Legend Research Club] Let's all research legends together. Wow! A dragon!

Kuro and Eunho froze on the spot.

"Ah~ we're in the go-home club, y'know~ We've got a mountain of stuff to do when we get home~"

Eunho grabbed Kuro and beat a hasty retreat.

"I'm begging you~ Kuro, Eunho~!!!"

Behind them, Herbaeken wailed "uwaaah" and launched into a flashback scene entirely on his own.

Herbaeken in a dragon mascot costume, handing out recruitment flyers. And the students who wouldn't take them.

"I've been trying so hard for days now.. wearing a costume I made myself.."
"What the heck are you wearing, man.."
"And before long, I came to realize."

Unfazed by Eunho's jab, Herbaeken saw his flashback through to the end and roared.

"Nobody's ever gonna join a nerd club like this~!"
"So you DO know what the problem is, you little!!!"

Undaunted even by Eunho's fierce jab, Herbaeken latched onto Eunho's pant leg and clung on.

"An academy this full of legends, and nobody cares~! Please~! Join the club, Eunho~! Kuro~!"

Watching this, Kuro thought long and hard before speaking.

"Hmm.. it'd be sad if the club couldn't even get started.. so shall we at least join? Though we'll probably end up as ghost members.."
"!"

At kind-hearted Kuro's consent, Herbaeken stopped crying.
Eunho, too, backed down.

"Tch.. if Kuro says so, guess there's no helping it."
"Eunho!"
"I mean, after what happened a few days ago, I'm kinda worried about leaving you on your own."

At the concern Eunho tossed out so offhandedly, Herbaeken answered brightly.

"Thank you both~! I'll go submit the club application, then!"

Herbaeken headed for the faculty office. And once the two were out of sight, he turned cold.

"Those two. So soft."


***

A few days later, after school.

"Welcome, new members!"

In front of the club room.
Herbaeken began the proceedings as president of the Legend Research Club.
Before him, Eunho and Kuro sat side by side, listening attentively.

"The Legend Research Club is a no.ble. club that explores and researches the legends handed down at this academy! I ask that all of you approach it with the mindset of a researcher!"

Kuro watched the full-blown briefing with fascination, while Eunho just munched away on the German snacks Herbaeken had laid out, savoring them—'German snacks are pretty good.'

"For your research topic, please choose from this book."
"Ah, the one you're always carrying around.."

It was the hardbound book Herbaeken always carried with such care.

"Yep, this book is my treasure~ Ever since I was little, I've loved legends~! It's a book of info I've been compiling since back then. This academy's got all kinds of legends gathered in one place, so I can see and study them firsthand—that's why I came all the way from Germany to Japan, you know? Me, I want to check out every last legend at this academy, at least before I graduate!"

"Wooow..."

Kuro was moved by Herbaeken's grand ambition.

"Coming all the way to another country to study what you love! That's kind of wonderful!"

Kuro's heart filled to the brim with a single resolve: 'I want to help Herbaeken.'

"All right, guess I'll give it my best too!!"

Kuro ambitiously flipped open a page, and there was a drawing of a Horn-Bearded Goblin.

The description read [Reeks of armpit when approached, and drools. Bedbugs can be found nearby.]

"I can't........"

His motivation crushed within one second, Kuro slumped face-down on the desk.

"Kuro, it's not all scary legends, you know? There are cute spirits and monsters that are friendly to humans too~ Look!"

The page Herbaeken opened showed a very cute monster. The Rubellite Sphinx Cat. A cat-like monster with a jewel at the tip of its tail and wings sprouting from its back.

"You're right. So cute~ I might be okay with the animal types~"
"!"

In that instant, Eunho flinched.

"Then shall we start by looking for this one today?"

Herbaeken wrapped things up as club president.

"The Legend Research Club's first activity, is, star,ting~!!"

(Herbaeken was imitating the 'ha,jima,ru yo~!' from the YuruYuri intro.)


***

The hill behind the academy, deep in the forest.
Here lay a holy site of unknown origin.
Moss-covered statues of divine figures stood here and there, and around them, nameless flowers bloomed in profusion.

A quiet yet devout atmosphere hung in the air, as if one were looking upon the traces of an ancient age.

And the Legend Research Club trio was cutting right through the middle of it on their expedition.

"The Rubellite Sphinx Cat! It's a monster straight out of mythology! In a place with high sacred power, like the holy site behind this academy...!"

Herbaeken went on explaining as they explored.

"What kind of academy was this place, anyway.... -_-;;"

Eunho grumbled, looking all around.
This darn oddball of an academy.
Vampires, marionettes, and now a holy site on top of it all—it was pure chaos.

"It feels like it's been left abandoned for a long time.."

A place untouched by human hands, where time seemed to have stopped.
Tense in the unfamiliar air, Kuro kept a wary eye on his surroundings.


*

On a flat patch of grass.
Two pickled-plum rice balls sat all by themselves on a leaf.
The trio hid behind a low bush, waiting for the monster to appear.

"Think it'll show up?"
"Why pickled-plum rice balls..? There's no way it'd come out for somethi—"

Just as Kuro's worry and Eunho's jabs were flying back and forth.

Rustle—.

The shadow of a small figure slipped into view.

'It came out!!!'

The trio screamed inwardly, all at once.

"Monsters really do exist!"
"S-so what do we do next?"

Kuro marveled, while Eunho, more worked up than anyone, pestered Herbaeken. Herbaeken, on the other hand, was surprisingly calm.

"For now, let's watch how things go..."
"It's eating!"

Before Herbaeken could even finish, Eunho gave the play-by-play.
The monster sniffed at the rice balls, about to eat.

Then, out of nowhere, a flock of crows swooped in and attacked the monster.
The monster just took it, helpless.

"It's a monster, so why is it that weak!?"

Eunho and Kuro sprang up and ran to the monster.

The two pulled off their uniform jackets and swung them around, driving off the crows.

"Are you okay!?"

Once things had calmed down, Kuro carefully held out a hand to the monster.

"Grrrrrrrr~!"

The monster eyed Kuro with all the wariness it had, then chomped down on one of his hands, all the way up to the wrist.

Crunch!

"Ow!"
"Kuro!!"

Eunho and Herbaeken's worried cries.
But Kuro did not pull back.

"Ow, ow... It's okay, I won't hurt you... there, there."

He stroked the monster with the hand that wasn't being bitten.
At his warm touch, the monster's growling gradually died down.

After a while, the monster looked up at Kuro and began to lap at his hand.

"Phew~"

Only then did sighs of relief escape the trio.


*

"It must not have been able to find any food all this time.."

The monster, wolfing down the rice balls.
Kuro murmured with pity.

Just then, Herbaeken, who had been studying the monster's tail closely, noticed something. The jewel at the tip of its tail was cracked.

"! Now that I look closely, it looks different from a normal Rubellite Sphinx Cat."
"Luu......."

As if to hide its flaw, the monster—whisk—tucked its tail away against its body.

"This kind of monster normally lives in packs.. but it looks like it got cast out because of how it looks. If we leave it alone in the forest like this, it could be in danger.."
"...."

Kuro looked at the monster with worried eyes.
Eunho, too, watched in silence, lost in thought.
At last, Kuro clenched his fists as if he'd made up his mind.

"Let's take care of it in our club room!"
"Lu!!"

As if it had been waiting for just that, the monster hopped right into Kuro's arms.

"What the heck, Kuro! Stealing the good scene~ I was actually thinking the same thing, y'know~"
"......."

Herbaeken watched the two of them for a moment, then wrapped things up as club president.

"…...Haha! The Legend Research Club has a new member! It's getting late, so shall we call it a day and head back!"

Before they knew it, the sky was dyed with the orange glow of sunset.
On the way back, Kuro and Eunho gave the monster a name.

"Anyway, we found a legend, so I'd call that a successful first club activity~"
"What should we name it?"
"'Rubellite Sphinx Cat'.. is way too long, so let's go with Lubell."
"I like it!"

Kuro and Eunho, walking ahead, chatting cozily.
And the gaze of Herbaeken, walking behind them, was filled with nothing but suspicion.

[End of Ep. 3]
