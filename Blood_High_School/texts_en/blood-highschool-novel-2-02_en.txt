▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 2

Rustle.

Kuro opened the refrigerator and took out a plastic bag with something in it.
Then he put on his shoes and got ready to go out.

"Kuro, where are you off to?"

Eunho asked, carrying a load of laundry.

"To see the Fox Spirit!"
"Ah~, okay~, have fun~."

Clack.

No sooner had Kuro shut the door behind him than Eunho burst into motion.
He threw the laundry at Shiro, who was lazing around nearby.

"Shiro, hang these up for me!!"

Dumping the housework on Shiro, Eunho dashed out the door in a mad flurry. He looped around by a side path so as not to cross paths with Kuro, and headed for the Fox Spirit's shrine.


***

"He's here!"

Arriving at the shrine, Kuro was as delighted as a child.

"He wasn't here last time!"

Beside the shrine sat the Fox Spirit, striking a pose, though somehow looking rather ragged and out of breath.


*

The two sat side by side on a nearby rock, sharing the offerings from the plastic bag.

"—So for the time being, I'm going to go around with Grandpa on observation visits, to learn the head's work by watching."

Kuro was filling the Fox Spirit in on how he had been doing.

"I still don't really understand what it means to be head, but...... I.. I won't let the Ryuo family use me!"

With his angry-Pomeranian face, Kuro spoke in a resolute tone that didn't suit it at all.
The Fox Spirit was a little surprised to hear it.

'So it has been weighing on him after all.......'

Kuro had not let what Miko once told him go in one ear and out the other; he remembered it. Those painful words: 'They'd rather prop up dim-witted Kuro as head and push him around as they please, wouldn't they?'

Kuro, pouring out to the Fox Spirit every little thing he could never bring himself to say to his childhood friend Eunho. Setting his mixed feelings aside, the Fox Spirit answered gently.

"....I see. Just keep learning like that, one step at a time. You will do well."

Kuro, who had been gazing steadily at the Fox Spirit, murmured softly.

"Eunho.."

Flinch!

"—and you talk kind of alike~ Your faces are alike too. It's so strange."

The Fox Spirit's heart pounded.

"Your vibe is totally different, though, so there's no way you're Eunho."

At the conviction in Kuro's next words, the Fox Spirit's expression turned hard to read.

"Thank you for cheering me on, Lord Fox Spirit."
"....."

That Kuro had failed to notice he was that very Eunho brought the Fox Spirit relief and, at the same time, a wave of bitterness.


*

Shhhh.......

A breeze came with the silence.
The leaves swayed, and the warm night air of spring brushed past.

"Kuro."
"?"

The Fox Spirit spoke softly, gazing into the air.

"You are.. truly kind. And the kindness you have is a strength that comes from trust. As you sit in the seat of the head of the Ryuo family, a moment will come when that trust wavers, all too fragile. And when that happens, you may lose that trust, and change."

At these baffling words, Kuro asked with an uneasy expression.

"...I don't really understand, but.. what do I do then?"

The Fox Spirit's expression softened, and he gave a clear answer.

"That trust is carved deep within you, Kuro. Call it back to mind, and you will return to who you truly are."

Kuro did not fully understand the Fox Spirit's words. But the air the spirit gave off now overlapped with the air of their first meeting, when he had told Kuro of the destiny he could not escape. In that heavy air, Kuro felt a vague unease.

The spirit drew close to Kuro and met his eyes.

"Do not worry.... When a trial too hard to bear comes to you, Kuro, I will be with you. Because I am always watching over you."

For an instant, Kuro blushed at a feeling he couldn't name and looked away from the Fox Spirit's eyes. The Fox Spirit was speaking in complete earnest, for Kuro's sake, and yet Kuro kept squirming. The Fox Spirit tilted his head, as if unable to make sense of that reaction.


***

Beneath a clear sky.
At the elite girls' academy Miko attended, a kendo friendly match was in full swing.

Miko glared and took her stance.
And she remembered. Those unpleasant words she had heard from behind the corner on the night of the succession ceremony.

[ "You have no idea what a relief it is that Miko didn't become head~" ]
[ "Ren nearly brought the family down—how could we raise his spitting image of a daughter to head? The power of the dragon or whatever, frankly, who cares~" ]
[ "Kuro is far better than having Miko as head~ He's docile." ]

The gossip her own family had spouted popped into her head even mid-match and grated on her nerves.

"That's it! Miko wins!"

At the referee's call, Miko steadied her stance, breathing hard. Her schoolmates swarmed around her, cheering.

"Kyaa~! Lady Miko, you're so cool~!"
"Lady Miko, please use this towel!"

Miko, who a moment ago had been radiating murderous intent, switched to saint mode in an instant. She accepted the towel with the gentlest smile of anyone.

"My, thank you~"

At Miko's kindness, the girls shyly mustered their courage.

"If you'd like, would you care to go to a dessert shop after this?"

Amid the flood of attention, Miko answered briefly and firmly, yet gently.

"I'm sorry, I have a prior engagement."
"Ehh~ T_T"


***

Clack.

Miko closed her shoe locker door.

'Sorry, but I don't have time to deal one by one with people who are of no use to me. Because my goal is "the top."'

What she had told her schoolmates was a lie.
She had no particular engagement, but that didn't mean she wanted to spend her precious time on them.

Ding—

"?"

On her way out of the academy, her phone rang in her pocket.
When she checked the screen, Miko's face soured.


***

Inside a burger joint downtown.

Through the boisterous noise, Eunho, seated across the table, cautiously began.

"...Uh.. how's life? Are you short on living expenses at all?"
"Get to the point. I don't have time."
";;"

At Miko's cold reply, Eunho looked down at the food in front of him and spoke heavily.

"...Miko. Do you happen to know of the 'Kagehira family'?"
"I know of them. They're a 'shadow family,' aren't they?"

Miko's answer came without a hitch.

"When they take a commission, they use forbidden arts at the cost of their own lives and take care of it without leaving a shred of evidence... A peculiar family. Most people who study sorcery know of them. Because getting tangled up with them the wrong way is dangerous."

Eunho's voice dropped low.

"The Ryuo family's past is tied up with that family."

Miko's eyes widened.

"! Tell me the details."


*

Brow furrowed, Eunho slowly began to speak.

[ "'Ryuo Ren,' who is called the worst head in the history of the Ryuo family. This is the story of the Ryuo family when that man was head." ]

Time went back to the past.

[ "...That man was a born villain. The kind of person who is only satisfied with everything beneath his feet." ]

A Ryuo family business site.
The road he walked was lined with people bowing their heads. A city hall inspector stepped into the path of the arrogantly striding Ren.

"Sir, this is a protected zone... Legally, I don't have the authority to let you through."

The voice of the low-ranking official who had blocked his way.
Instead of answering, Ren slowly twitched an eyebrow.

[ "For the sake of his ambition, he did not hesitate even to kill those who stood in his way." ]

After that day, the TV news reported that the head of the construction bureau had taken his own life. Ren smiled, as if satisfied.

[ "That was when he became involved with the 'Kagehira family.' At first the deal was to remove a few obstacles... but when the Ryuo family was actively doing charity work, the number of people following him grew rapidly." ]

Ren deceived the world with thoroughly calculated staging. He poured enormous donations into a crumbling orphanage, handed out food to starving children with his own hands, and smiled like a benevolent father. The photograph of him wiping away tears as he lifted an abandoned animal into his arms graced the front page of the newspaper, and people called him 'a living saint' and followed the Ryuo family blindly.

[ "Taking advantage of that, he sent the blind believers who followed him, and orphaned children, to the Kagehira family, had them taught forbidden arts, and made them into sacrifices." ]

The deal was clear.

[ "That man needed the Kagehira's power to climb higher, and the Kagehira family needed a major employer for the stability of their house." ]

[ "With these two families joining hands, the Ryuo family grew, and with the Ryuo family at the center, several other families joined in, forming a vast organization that amassed enormous wealth." ]

[ "But it did not last. Swallowed by desire and not knowing when to stop, that man dug up sacred land that should never have been touched, received the divine punishment of the land gods, and died an empty death." ]

On the hill behind the town, under the pretext of a 'large-scale development project,' Ren brought in heavy excavators and tore centuries-old sacred trees out by the roots. Watching the scene, Ren was drunk on a sense of omnipotence, as though he could bend everything in the world to his will.

But a few days later, together with the workers he had mobilized, divine retribution struck: he was taken by a disease that burned his whole body black, and died. Such was the wretched end of the villain Ryuo Ren.

[ "When the pillar of the organization collapsed, all the families that had taken part pulled out to escape responsibility, and the Kagehira family was abandoned. As a result, the Kagehira family was left saddled with more sacrifices than it could bear, lost the trust of its employers, and saw its commissions dwindle." ]

The face of the Kagehira head, filled with rage and betrayal.

[ "Yoshiyuki, who had been living apart from the Ryuo family, learned of this only belatedly, after inheriting the headship, and set out for the Kagehira family to take responsibility for the Ryuo family's sins." ]

That was the last Eunho ever saw of Yoshiyuki's back.

[ "But that day, Yoshiyuki did not come back." ]

Miko was shaken.

"!!!!!...Then Master Yoshiyuki's death wasn't an accident, but—!"
"...There is no way to know. I tried to dig into Yoshiyuki's death, but the Kagehira family had already vanished without a trace."

At Eunho's calm answer, Miko's agitation subsided.

***

"—That is the Ryuo family's dark history."

Inside the burger joint, people's laughter sounded alien. Miko's stomach churned.

"...To do things like that and never once get caught. The Ryuo family."

At Miko's trembling voice, Eunho spoke heavily.

"I'm going to tell Kuro everything too, before long. It's the family's sin, and as head he has to shoulder it."
"....."

Eunho's words carried weight.
Just then, Miko, who had been hanging her head, murmured low.

"Don't tell him. Not Kuro."
"What?"
"...Hey, do you seriously think Kuro is fit to be head?"

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 2]
