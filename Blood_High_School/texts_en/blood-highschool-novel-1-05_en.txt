▶Ep. 5 Crossed Succession and Unwanted Power

Tweet, tweet—

A peaceful weekend morning at the Ryuo house.
Kuro slowly opened his eyes to the warm sunlight streaming in between the curtains.

*

"Morning...."

Drawn by the delicious smell wafting from the kitchen, Kuro came down the stairs mumbling sleepily.

"Oh, you're up?"
"Good morning to you~"

The Ryuo family kitchen.
There stood Eunho, an apron tied on as naturally as could be, tasting the soup for seasoning.
Grandpa, on the other hand, sat plopped in the seat of honor without lifting a finger to help.

"It's the weekend, you could've slept in."
"Ah, mm... I know, but.."

Just then, Grandpa cut in.

"That won't do at all! Look at me! As head of the Ryuo family, rising early is the very basics!"

Really, he just needed less sleep because he was old, but Grandpa swaggered as if it were some great feat of his own. Eunho, with a look that said he was sick of it, simply went on setting the table, while Kuro's face died a little at the pointless nagging that had started first thing in the morning.

"My grandson will one day be the head who leads the Ryuo family, after all~ Now that the Dragon God dwells in you, you must carry yourself all the more—"
"Dragon God?"

Kuro tilted his head.

"Ah~ here we go again, first thing in the morning. Just eat your food!!!"
"Guoh!! What do you think you're doing, you rascal!!"

Eunho shut Grandpa up by stuffing rice into his mouth.
Kuro didn't miss his chance and wolfed down his meal.

Thanks to Eunho squabbling with Grandpa like a comedy duo, Kuro faded into the background and focused on nothing but eating.

"You rascal! Coming to the Ryuo house day after day just to eat us out of house and home! Go back to your own house!"
"No way~ This place is huge, and you're loaded~ Plenty of spare rooms, too."

Even at Grandpa's scolding, Eunho answered with easy slyness.

"Ah~ maybe I'll just live here for the rest of my life~ How about it, Kuro?"
"Eh? Fine by me!"
"Hyaah!! Absolutely not~!!!!"

Just as Grandpa's shriek rang out.

Swish—.

The split curtain at the kitchen entrance was roughly swept aside.

"......?"

All three pairs of eyes turned to the entrance at once.

"My, my~ good morning, everyone~"

A morning greeting from a beautiful girl.
It was Miko, Kuro's cousin of the same age.


***

The formal reception room of the Ryuo house.
A spacious tatami room that conveyed the full dignity of the family.

Grandpa sat in the seat of honor, and Miko sat across from him. Off to the side between them, Kuro and Eunho sat side by side, watching the vicious war of words.

"Like.I.said!! I cannot accept the way you do things, old man!"

Miko's sharp voice rang through the room.

"To begin with, I was the child of the family head, wasn't I!? So why does the head's authority pass to Kuro, the second son's boy!?"
"Enough! After your father passed in that accident, it was Yoshiyuki who led this family!"
"And now that Master Yoshiyuki has passed away as well, shouldn't it come back around to me, the direct line!?"

Neither Miko nor Grandpa gave an inch.
Beside them, kneeling formally alongside Eunho and listening, Kuro hung his head, lost in thought.

'Miko's right. Originally, Miko's side was supposed to inherit the headship, but Miko's family died in an accident.... so Dad took over as head.. And then Dad passed away, and I became next in line..'
'I told them I'm not confident about becoming head.. but Grandpa's always been so stubborn..'

Then Miko struck at the heart of the matter.

"—The Ryuo family's little scheme is plain as day."
"?"

Miko snorted. Grandpa just blinked, wondering what on earth she was about to come out with.

"I've always been above Kuro in everything, ever since we were little. Unlike that coward Kuro, I was strong and smart—naturally, I'm the one fit to be head."

The three of them had grown up together since they were seven.
Back then, Kuro always lost to Miko.
At kendo, at test scores, even at arguments.
Miko glared at Grandpa with venom in her eyes.

"But the people of the Ryuo family would rather prop up dim-witted Kuro as head and push him around as they please than have me, a perfect elite—isn't that right?"
"......!"

Hit right where it hurt, Grandpa visibly flinched.
Eunho glanced at Kuro's face.
Kuro already looked hurt.

"Miko!! Say what you will, the one who inherited the power of the dragon is Kuro! That settles who is head of the Ryuo family!"

Sweating bullets, Grandpa insisted all the louder.
Miko ground her teeth.

"The power of the dragon, is it. Fine. Then I will duel Lackness."
"!?"

Eunho was thrown by those words.
Gripping the wooden sword she had brought, Miko slowly rose.

"Eh-wha!?"

Grandpa flapped about at the unexpected turn of events.

"If he's such a high and mighty Dragon God, there's no way he'd lose to me, right? That is how I'll make you all acknowledge my right to be head."

The sight of Miko declaring it so boldly was dazzlingly beautiful..... and handsome.


***

The wide front yard of the Ryuo house.
Amid the tension, the two stood facing each other at a distance.
Each gripping a wooden sword.

"...Um, what's all this about a Dragon God and the power of the dragon you keep talking about? I don't have anything like that..."
"A power you're not even aware of—how truly pathetic!"

Miko's brow furrowed at Kuro's clueless question.

"Here I come!"

Fwap—!

Miko scattered talismans into the air, and shikigami foxes were summoned.

'That's the shikigami technique they say only a shrine maiden who's gone through grueling training can use......! Miko, just how much have you trained?'

Eunho, watching from the side, was astounded.

The shikigami foxes pounced on Kuro all at once.

Boom—!

With a tremendous roar, a cloud of dust rose.

Lackness slowly emerged from within it, without so much as a scratch. Her pride wounded by the sight, Miko tightened her grip on her wooden sword and charged at him.

'I won't accept it....... I, I—!'

At the same time, Miko remembered.
Her memories of 'Yoshiyuki'—Kuro's father, and her own teacher.

[ "Miko, you are a natural." ]
[ "Intellect, martial arts, spiritual power—you are lacking in none of them." ]
[ "Just as one would expect of a child of the Ryuo family." ]

Her teacher Yoshiyuki, who had taken seven-year-old Miko under his wing.

[ "Won't you become my disciple?" ]

Little Miko, beaming with joy at those words. And all the years she had spent training with Yoshiyuki flashed before her eyes.

'I am Ryuo Miko.'
'The disciple who inherited everything from Master Yoshiyuki.'
'And so—'

Backlight shone on Miko's resolute figure, seen from behind.

'The one who will inherit Master Yoshiyuki's place is me!'

Boom—! Bang—! Boom—! Thud—!

Back to the battlefield of the present.

Miko unleashed every bit of spiritual power and martial skill she had, without holding back. The dozens of talismans she scattered surrounded Lackness in an instant, forming a dense wall and caging him in.

But that was all.

The blood-red flames that poured from Lackness evaporated everything into nothing. The talismans burned up in an instant, and Miko, charging in, was flung back, unable to withstand the pressure of the flames.

Lackness had not moved even once.
He only silently defended against her attacks, and never once attacked Miko.

Meanwhile, Grandpa was aghast, watching his own front yard get wrecked.

"Good grief! My front yaaaard~~!!"

Eunho, on the other hand, watched the scene tensely, certain in his heart.

'But the outcome is already decided...'


***

"Hah, hah, hah......."

At the sight of Miko, tattered and sunk to the ground, both Eunho and Grandpa lowered their heads bitterly. Lackness, on the other hand, completely unharmed, looked down at Miko with emotionless eyes.

Head lowered to hide her face, Miko shook with fury.

"Why..... Why did the power of the dragon come to dwell in someone like Kuro?"

A voice thick with resentment and rage.
When Miko made no further attack, Lackness closed his eyes to return to Kuro.

"Wait!"

Miko shouted, looking up at Lackness.

"...Why doesn't Kuro remember anything!? Is that your doing..?"
"!"

At Miko's question, Eunho's gaze sharpened.
It was a question Eunho had too, so he held his breath and listened closely.

Lackness was silent for a long while before slowly opening his mouth.

".....Kuro is rejecting me (the power) of his own will."

At those words, Miko and Eunho froze as though they had received a great shock. Only Grandpa, unable to grasp the situation, went "Hm?" with a blank look on his face.

"......Just as I thought. So Kuro is running away."

Lackness answered Miko firmly.

"It makes no difference. Any hostility aimed at Kuro, I will face in his place. Does that answer your question?"

Miko asked no more. In the silence, Lackness closed his eyes, and a moment later he was Kuro again.

"Huh.. Miko?"

Shocked at the sight of Miko in tatters, Kuro ran to her side.

"What happened? Why are you hurt like this.. Don't tell me I..?"
"As if that could ever happen!"
"Eh...eh?"

Leaving the bewildered Kuro behind, Miko got to her feet.
On her way into the house, Miko brushed past Eunho.

"......Miko."
"Don't get the wrong idea. I didn't lose to Kuro!"

Miko snapped at Eunho and walked off on her way, head held high.


***

Evening, the sun setting.
Ready to leave, Miko was putting on her shoes at the front door.

"I'm going home. Staying here is just a waste of time."
"Eh? You came all this way after so long, you should stay a while longer.."

Kuro's wistful voice lasted only a moment before Grandpa butted in obnoxiously.

"Miko! What do you intend to do from here on!"
"From here on? Isn't it obvious. Unlike Kuro, I am going to graduate from an elite high school and an elite university~ inherit this and that business of the Ryuo family, and achieve great success with my brilliant mind~"

Miko declared it with a dazzlingly beautiful smile. Then she smoothly pulled out a single document, something like a transfer of property shares, and held it out to Kuro.

"So hand over your shares, Kuro."
"Whaaat—!?"

Grandpa shrieked, but Kuro, as if bewitched, slowly brought his finger toward the paper.

"Miko! So this was what you were after all along!"
"Uwaah! Don't just put your seal on it that easily, Kuro!!!"

Grandpa dangled from Kuro's arm, straining and whining, and Eunho, panicking too, grabbed Kuro by the shoulders.

"Tch."

Miko clicked her tongue as though it were a real shame.

Once the commotion had passed, Kuro said his last goodbye to Miko.

"I'm rooting for you, Miko! You're amazing, so I'm sure you can do anything!"

Even though she was the one who had been cornering him only moments ago, there was not a trace of ill feeling in Kuro.

"And come visit often. Let's hang out again, the three of us, like old times."

The power of the family, and the true successor to carry on their teacher's will. The two of them stood atop that fierce conflict, and yet they were as good as siblings who had grown up together in the Ryuo house. Like the photograph in an old frame sitting somewhere in the house, Kuro put that bond first.

Reading his heart, Eunho gazed at Kuro warmly, while Miko furrowed her brow, as if that attitude of his did not sit well with her.


***

On the train home.
Looking out the window, Miko grumbled, her expression still tinged with discontent.

"....I tried to attack you, so what are you smiling for?"

Before long, Miko's eyes turned simply bitter.

"...Do you hate fighting that much? Kuro. That part of you—I truly hate it."


***

Back at the Ryuo house.

Perched on the windowsill, Eunho looked down at Kuro squirming before Grandpa and turned Lackness's words over in his mind.

'Kuro is rejecting me of his own will.'

Beneath the night sky.
Eunho steeled his resolve.

"I have to tell him. Everything."

[End of Ep. 5]
