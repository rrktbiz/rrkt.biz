▶Ep. 8 A Legend Overturned Is Still Just a Legend

The day of the St. Saints school trip.
A big tour bus was running along a wide-open coastal highway with Tokyo Tower in view.

"Wooow~ It's Tokyo Tower~!"

Herbaeken, pressed up against the bus window, gazed outside in excitement. In the seat behind him, Eunho was chatting with the boys who were roaming up and down the bus, goofing around.

The boys suggested sneaking over to the girls' lodging at night, but Eunho was smoothly turning them down: "Eh~ I've got no interest in the girls' lodging~lol"

Next to him, in the window seat, Kuro stared blankly out the window without a thought in his head.


*

The bus stopped by the seaside, the students got off, and the teachers began herding them along. The homeroom teacher of Class 1-B, Mr. Murasaki, was so excited that he was wearing a loud Hawaiian shirt.

"Everyone, be sure to be back here by two o'clock~"
"If you're late, we're leaving you behind, gero!"
"Yes~"


*

The seashore, under blazing sunlight.
The principal's speech followed.

Beach-version Shota stood under a parasol, in a bizarre full-body swimsuit reminiscent of a prison uniform, and goggles. Even the stuffed rabbit he carried around every day wore a dainty little straw hat.

"Everyone~ have fun on the school trip~!! Our Ferel will keep everyone safe~!!"

Appearing beside him, Ferel, the student council president, wore a jaw-dropping pair of tiny swim briefs, his hair in a ponytail under a cap, a whistle hanging from his neck.

"Tweeet—! I shall fulfill my duty as student council president. Ufufu☆"
"Kyaa—"

The girls squealed at Ferel's sizzling display of skin.


*

"Waa~ the sea~!!"

The vast sea spread out before their eyes.
It sparkled so brightly it was dazzling.
Herbaeken was so excited that he was about to charge into the sea with a dragon-shaped float around his waist.

"Here I go~!"
"Hmm.. swimming's not really my thing, so I'll just dip my feet in."
"Me too~ Let's leave that idiot be and just walk along the shore."

Unable to keep up with Herbaeken's energy, the two tried to slip away. But Herbaeken caught them at once.

"What are you saying after coming all this way? Come on, hurry, hurry!"
"Wh-what! (This guy's crazy strong~!!)"
"Herbaeken, stop it~!!"

Splash—!

With anguished screams, the two were plunged into the sea.

Drip... drip...

Soaked to the skin, Eunho and Kuro dripped water.
Absolutely livid, Eunho blasted Herbaeken with water like a madman, with all his might.

Splash-splash-splash-splash—!!

"HER~BAE~KEN~!!!!!!"
"Kyaaaan~~"

Watching from a tall lifeguard chair far off, Ferel tossed out a leisurely remark.

"Oh my, such youth."


***

But was it really youth?

The staple of any beach episode, watermelon splitting, was in full swing.

Whoosh— whack!

"Uwaah!!"

Herbaeken, fallen flat on his backside after dodging the stick.

Fsssh...

When he opened his eyes, the stick was planted savagely just below his crotch.

"What are you doing, Eunho~!"
"Oops~! You're the same green, so I got mixed up~ Sowwy, sowwy~"

Blindfolded, Eunho held up one hand in a half-hearted apology.

"Liar! You're wearing a blindfold!?"

Ignoring Herbaeken, Eunho swished the stick through the air.

"Aah~ where could that watermelon be~ I se~riously have no idea~"
"Uwah! Why are you swinging it in the air!?"

Eunho went "Where~?" and chased after Herbaeken in particular, while Herbaeken wailed "Uwaaan~" and ran for dear life. On the surface it looked like high school boys enjoying their youth, but what the two were feeling inside was downright murderous.

'So persistent, this flashy flirt!!!!'
'This legend nerd bastard...!!!!!'


*

The second staple of any beach episode: beach ball.

"I'm sending the ball up~!"

Kuro timidly popped the ball up into the air.

Whaam—!!!

In that instant, Eunho leapt up as if he had been waiting for it and unleashed a full-power smash at the ball. Herbaeken barely dodged it.

"Oi oi~ what are you dodging for~ You're supposed to receive it~"

Eunho said it with a sly smile, but both of their eyes were already in battle mode. At the same moment, their inner voices overlapped.

'I'll kill you!!'

"Atatatatatata—!!"

A dead-serious beach ball match, just between the two of them, had begun.
The friends playing with them just stared, with no chance to cut in.

Watching the two fight so childishly, Kuro went cold.

"What are those two doing...."


***

Before they knew it, it was dinnertime. The dining hall was buzzing with the students' chatter.

"Never landed a single hit in the end.. And I was throwing pretty seriously, too."

Still unable to shake his frustration from the beach ball earlier, Eunho grumbled and munched away at his food. Beside him, Kuro, who hadn't quite caught that, asked.

"Mm?"
"It's nothing."

Just then, Mr. Murasaki made an announcement from the middle of the dining hall.

"Everyone~ there will be a test of courage after dinner, so please come down to the lodging entrance by seven~!"
"We'll give you a taste of bone-chilling terror, gero!!!!!"
"Yes~"

Everyone was in high spirits, but Kuro alone grimaced.

"Ugh.. a test of courage.. why do we have to do that..."
"Eunho, Kuro!"

Herbaeken popped up out of nowhere.

"Did you know? There's a legend here too, they say!"
"There he goes. The legend nerd."

Still sour over what had happened earlier, Eunho shot Herbaeken a sidelong glare. Kind-hearted Kuro, not wanting Herbaeken to feel awkward, humored him.

"Eh? Here too?"
"I mean, this place is the private resort St. Saints students come to every year, right? There's no way it's unrelated!"

Herbaeken opened the book he had brought and showed it to Kuro.

"Look! The legendary mermaid! Let's sneak off to see it during the test of courage!"

The book showed a mermaid, human in the upper half and fish in the lower. Looking at it, Kuro thought.

'Heh, a mermaid, huh.. half human, half fish.. that might not be so scary? The Fox Spirit wasn't scary either.'

Just as Kuro was about to say yes,

"We're not going!!!!"

Eunho snapped.

"Because of you, Kuro gets dragged into something dangerous every single time~!! Did you forget what happened the other day!?"

At the sudden outburst, Kuro and Herbaeken's eyes went wide.
But Eunho wasn't wrong. Kuro did end up in danger time and again because of Herbaeken's stubborn pursuit of legends.

"..You're right. Then I'll go, even if it's by myself!"

Unexpectedly, Herbaeken backed down without a fuss and turned away.
At the unexpected reaction, it was Eunho, the one who had been angry, who ended up looking the more flustered.


***

The dead of night. The test-of-courage grounds

"Enter in order~"

At the starting line, Murasaki paired the students off and sent them into the woods. Eunho and Kuro stood side by side in the waiting line. Kuro looked around for Herbaeken but couldn't see him.

"Herbaeken really did go off by himself... Was it really okay for us not to go with him?"

At Kuro's worried murmur, Eunho went "Uuugh...." with the most uncomfortable face in the world.


***

At that same hour, Herbaeken sat alone on a cliff with a clear view of the rock where the mermaid was said to appear.

He looked back and forth between the illustration in the book and the rock below the cliff, but no mermaid appeared—only the waves rolled.

At last Herbaeken closed the book, set it down beside him, and sank deep into thought.

The day his dragon-slaying plan had failed.
He recalled himself cornering Lackness.

The Lackness of legend had to be evil itself.
But Kuro, the host, was far too kind, and even Lackness had bowed his head with an unexpected apology.

That contradiction, as if the legend he knew had been turned upside down, shook Herbaeken.

'It can't be. There's no way I hesitated.'

Before long, he steeled his vengeance once more.

'I have to listen once more to the voices of suffering.'

Herbaeken called to mind the bitter anguish of his ancestors, massacred a thousand years ago. Then the screams of vengeful spirits began to reach him, and formless souls came creeping to entwine his body.

[ "Avenge us. End it.." ]

The phantom voices came, as they always did.
Assimilated into that grudge, Herbaeken's eyes once again became those of an avenger filled with hatred.

"Lackness. I will. Kill you."


*

Rustle—!

"!?"

The bushes behind him stirred.

"There he is! Herbaeken!"

It was Kuro and Eunho.

"Eh. Why are you two.."
"We know full well it's dangerous, so we couldn't just leave you. Well.. we're friends.... after all."

Eunho answered awkwardly.
Herbaeken was left dumbfounded at the unexpected answer.

"Sorry about earlier."

Eunho's apology followed.
Beside him, Kuro looked at Eunho with proud eyes.

Looking at the two of them, Herbaeken felt the strength drain out of him.
He muttered in a voice too quiet for them to hear.

"...Why are you both so soft?"
"Did you say something?"
".........No, it's nothing."

A late-summer night in October..... The breeze was warm....

Eunho's consideration, trying to protect Kuro's peace, and the bright smile of Kuro, who knew nothing..... And Herbaeken, who, amid their kindness, felt an unfamiliar fear without even knowing it was friendship...

One swallows his secret for the sake of a friend's precious everyday life; another hesitates on the trigger of revenge before the kindness of the one he must kill... A precarious companionship, walking side by side once more while aiming at each other.......

Whether the genre is thriller, shounen manga, or just a plain trainwreck, even I don't know anymore. But let's call it youth anyway... since that's the mood in the air right now.....

*

"Now then— it's that rock, right? Where the mermaid shows up."

Eunho took the lead to hide his embarrassment.
The three of them looked together at the rock below the cliff.

"Yeah. But it's not coming out at all..."
"We can't just wait around forever. Hyah!!"

Eunho picked up a stone from the ground and hurled it at the rock.

Tink—!

The stone struck the rock with a small sound.

Creeeep.....

An eerie, unidentifiable hand rose up over the rock.
It crawled up bit by bit, and soon revealed itself in full.

It was a grotesquely shaped mermaid... no, fishman, whose body was a giant fish with only its arms and legs human. The exact opposite of the beautiful mermaid in the illustration.

"Pfuuu—!"

A jet of water spurted from the fishman's mouth.
The trio froze on the spot with stupefied faces.


***

"IYAAAAH!!!!!!!!!!!"

The fishman came chasing after the trio like a thing possessed.
The trio ran for their lives.

"No way, no way!! That's a mermaid!?"

Kuro wailed.

"Mermaids don't look like that!?!?"
"Uwaan~ I guess that was just an image people made up on their own~!!!"
"That's way too big a difference!!!!! That thing's a monster!!!"

Even while running for dear life, Eunho still fired jabs at Herbaeken.

"Where do we go!?"
"I don't know, just run!! Who knows what it'll do if it catches us!!"

While Kuro and Eunho ran frantically, looking only ahead, Herbaeken, who had fallen behind, tripped and vanished off-screen. But the two didn't notice a thing.


***

At that same hour. Murasaki was doing a headcount of the students.

"Let's see~ three left now, Kuro, Eunho, and Herbaeken.. Why aren't they back?"

Thud-thud-thud-thud-thud—!

Along with footsteps came the sound of something charging from somewhere.

"...Hm?"

Spotting Eunho and Kuro in the distance being chased by an unidentifiable fishman, Murasaki was aghast.

"Kids!?"

At the same time, Murasaki lost his nerve at the fishman's grotesque appearance.

'What is that thing, I don't wanna—!! B-but I have to save the kids! I'm a teacher, after all!'

He was scared, but he summoned his courage for his students.

"Y-your teacher will save you! Hyoi!"


*

'It evolved~!!!'

Kuro and Eunho were still being chased, and now only Murasaki's legs stuck out of the fishman's mouth, flailing. From inside the fishman's body echoed Murasaki's desperate scream: "Nooooo—!"

In the middle of their frantic flight, Eunho and Kuro ran into Hashida. Hashida, apparently fresh out of the bath, was passing by the lodging entrance with a yukata draped loosely over him, drying his wet hair with a towel.

"Ah, Kuro. You haven't had your bath yet, right? If you don't get in soon, it'll clo..... eh?"

Thwack!

Shota, who just happened to be passing by.

"Hoeeeh~"

Thwack!

Ferel, sexily savoring a post-bath milk.

"Fufu.. how beautiful............ NOT~!!!!"

Thwack!

And so every member of the cast was sent flying, one after another, by the fishman's charge, and everything descended into chaos.


***

Meanwhile, Herbaeken, left all on his own.

"Damn it.. Ditched again.. That makes twice. ( ▀ 益 ▀ )"

Herbaeken recalled the last festival day, when the two hadn't come looking for him for the longest time.

Back in avenger mode the instant he was abandoned, he had forgotten the feeling of youth from earlier and was now busy feeling betrayed.

"I knew it, those guys.. friends, as if...!!"

Just then, Kuro appeared from behind a tree, smiling.

"...............('⊙⌓⊙;)"

Herbaeken broke into a cold sweat, afraid Kuro had heard what he had just said.

"Ah! Kuro! Thank goodness you're safe—I got lost, and..!"

Herbaeken switched gears.

Fwip.

Without a word, Kuro pointed the way with his finger.

"Thanks, Kuro!"

Herbaeken started off ahead, but then realized he couldn't hear Kuro's footsteps behind him.

"Eh, aren't you coming with me?"

He turned around, puzzled, but there was already no one there.

"...Huh?"


***

Meanwhile, in front of the lodging, Eunho and Kuro were gasping for breath.

"Hah, hah, we finally lost it.. that monster mermaid.."

Eunho collapsed on his back, and Kuro noticed that one of their party was missing.

"Ah! Where's Herbaeken..!?"

Rustle—

At that very moment, Herbaeken emerged from the bushes ahead.

"Herbaeken!"
"Kuro, you got here first? You were over there just a moment ago... you're fast, huh?"
"...Eh?"

Kuro's face went pale in an instant.
Behind him, Eunho spoke, breaking into a cold sweat.

"What are you talking about, Herbaeken. We got chased by that monster and ran together the whole way.."
"? Then what I saw was.."

Shhhhh—...

The night wind suddenly blew cold.
All three got goosebumps at once.
If what Herbaeken had just seen wasn't Kuro.........
...........a.....ghost....?

"AAAAAAAAH————!!!!!!!!"

The screams of the three rang out in front of the lodging.
A chaotic school trip, complete with a thoroughly accomplished test of courage.

And, far off on a dark wooded hill, a boy looking down at the lodging.

"Big brother looks like he's having fun with his friends..."

The identity of the boy whose face was the spitting image of Kuro's—
Kuro's younger twin brother. It was Shiro.

"Kukuk."

Shiro said, grinning cutely.

"Maybe it's about time I went to the academy too~☆"

[End of Ep. 8]
