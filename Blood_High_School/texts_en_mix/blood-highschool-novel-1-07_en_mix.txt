▶Ep. 7 The Judge of the Fallen Kingdom

His father's old study.

Among books piled mountain-high, young Herbaeken had his favorite book of dragons spread open, gazing down at the dragons in its illustrations.

Then a huge shadow approached from behind.

"Otousan?"

His father snatched the book from Herbaeken and ripped it to shreds.
Then he roared at the terrified boy.

"Dragons are our enemy!"
"We kill them!!"
"We kill the dragon, Herbaeken!!"

***

"Hah—!"

Herbaeken woke, gasping for breath.
His whole body was drenched in sweat. The terrible nightmare of his childhood.
At the foot of the bed, a black wolf kept watch at his side, as if worried for its master.

"Kh.. this dream again."

He ran a hand down his face.
He got out of bed and set his bare feet on the floor.
The creak of old wooden floorboards.
Outside the window, dark clouds were gathering with a rumble, as if rain were on its way.

Herbaeken sat down at his desk. The wall in front of the desk was grotesque.
Photos and notes were plastered all over a large map, with countless black lines drawn between them.
At the center, where every line converged, a photo of Kuro was pinned.

Beside that photo, it read:
[ ← THIS IS LACKNESS ]

By contrast, the photo of Eunho stuck next to it bore just a single line.
[ Flashy, flirty childhood friend. In the way. ]

Next, Herbaeken opened a picture diary of the sort a lower-grade elementary schooler would use, and began his sublimation activity of venting his rage.

The picture he drew in the diary was a self-portrait, gun in hand, wearing a cold expression. To fill in the empty space, he wrote the phrase 'I'm Cursed BOY'. (Baeken: perfect www)

[Title: Why not know your 罪]

The space below, further down the diary page. Filled in with crooked handwriting.

[The.time.to.car.ry.out.my.re.venge.draws.near.]

Herbaeken drew his gun and gripped it, radiating killing intent.

"Lackness."
"Kisama, who massacred ore no people."
"I.will.ex.e.cute."


***

The first day back at school, on Kuro's walk there.

"So summer break is already over... A lot happened over summer break.."

Early September. The summer heat had yet to fade, and Kuro wiped his sweat, thinking, 'Still as hot as ever, though..'

'To think there's such a terrifying dragon power inside me....'
'Boku, being born into the Ryuo family turned out to be a really big deal...'
'I'm a little anxious, but daijoubu da yo ne.'

Kuro thought of the Fox Spirit on the night of Tanabata.

[ "From now on, too, I will be watching over you, so watashi wo shinjiro." ]

Kuro went all fuwafuwa.

'Because I have the Fox Spirit watching over me..!'
'........But how, exactly..?'

A cute little doubt popped into his head.

"Thinking about the Fox Spirit again?"
"Eunho...!"

Eunho popped up out of nowhere and joined him on the way to school.
And then he poked at Kuro, just to test him.

"Hmm~ You sure are quick to believe some guy who showed up out of nowhere."
"Mou~! Don't speak ill of that noble being!"

Kuro huffed like an angry Pomeranian.

'That noble being...?'

Special treatment unlike anything he himself got, and the loftiest of honorifics.
Eunho was flustered by Kuro's unexpected anger.

To think he would defend the Fox Spirit this fiercely. He was exasperated to see Kuro so readily trust someone he had only just met, and yet the fact that that someone was, in the end, himself left his feelings in a tangle.

He was glad Kuro liked the Fox Spirit, but here he was, the real thing, getting scolded by Kuro. Not knowing whether to laugh or cry, Eunho's face turned sulky.

"Mou, Eunho! You don't believe any of it, desho! Your reaction was so lukewarm when I first told you!"

[Flashback to that day]

"The Fox Spirit just appeared to me!"

Kuro, shouting in excitement.

"Uwaa~ maji ka yo~ sugee~."

Eunho's soulless reaction.

[Back to the present]

"Iya iya, I believe you, I swear~! I saw Lackness once, back when he fought Miko~"
"Eh?"

Eunho brushed it off lightly. Then he added, perfectly at ease.

"Anyway, even with the power of the dragon, Kuro is still Kuro, right. Whatever happens, you just take care of it in a flash with that power, same as you always have."

Feeling his anxious heart grow strangely lighter at that cool answer, Kuro looked at Eunho.

Ding-♪

Just then, a text alert chimed on Kuro's phone.

The sender was Herbaeken.

[Huge discovery! Assemble after school!]


***

The opening ceremony was short.

With the principal's speech and the cleaning done, it was 11 a.m.
With no lunch on the schedule, most of the students went home.
And at the end of the empty hallway, the Legend Research Club room.
Herbaeken sat perched on a desk with a dark expression.

Click, clack.

He loaded and unloaded the gun in his hand, over and over.
Beside him, Lubell tilted its head, observing Herbaeken.

Twitch.

Lubell's ears twitched toward the door.

Rattle—!

"Herbaeken!"

Kuro and Eunho came into the club room.

"You're here?"

Herbaeken hid the gun and greeted the two.

"Lu!"

Lubell leapt into Kuro's arms.

"Lubell, hisashiburi~"

Kuro greeted Lubell happily.

"—So, a huge discovery on the very first day back? What is it?"

Eunho asked, as if it were a bother.

"Nee, nee! Over summer break, boku went on a great expedition in the academy~"
"So you broke in, huh;"

For the first time in a while, Eunho's tsukkomi came flying.

"And guess what, I found it! A secret passage!"


***

"Here it is"

Where Herbaeken stopped was in front of an old, massive door, boarded up with planks in the shape of an X.

'One look and you can tell it's super suspicious and we're definitely not supposed to go in...'

Eunho and Kuro had the same thought.

"I saved this spot so we could all explore it together. You have no idea how long I've waited for this moment!! >o< Come on, let's go in!"
"O-oi!!"

Before they could stop him, Herbaeken tore off the planks and went into the passage, and Eunho and Kuro reluctantly followed.


***

Tap, tap

In the dark passage, the footsteps of the three echoed.
The passage led underground. Perhaps it wasn't entirely abandoned, for lights had been installed.
It was like a secret passage that someone was maintaining.

"What kind of academy is this place.."
"I've got the creeps..;"

Eunho and Kuro were full of worry and unease, but Herbaeken, in the lead, pressed on without a trace of fear.

"Oi, aren't we going in too deep?"
"But there's got to be something amazing further in~"
"Ghh;;"

Despite Eunho's attempt to stop him, Herbaeken went deeper still.

At that instant.

Clunk.

Herbaeken stepped on something, and the stone floor beneath their feet gave way.

"Eh."

The floor the three were standing on vanished.

"Uwaaaaaah!!"

With a scream, they plunged into the darkness. The chute branched this way and that, and the three tumbled off in different directions.


*

Patter.

Dirt and dust rained down.
Coming to, Eunho got up, coughing.

"What the—"

When he raised his head, a massive wall stood blocking his way.
Eunho's eyes went wide. A planned trap.
Instinctively, he sensed that Kuro was in danger.


*

At the same time, Kuro opened his eyes as well.

"Eunho? Herbaeken?"

He looked around, but no one was there.
Kuro barely managed to get up, and walked on, scanning his surroundings.
Murals began to appear along the walls of the passage.

"What is this..?"

Murals carved with the dragons of legend continued on like an exhibition.
Among them, a mural of a dragon labeled Lackness savagely burning people sent a chill through Kuro.

At the end of the path, a helpful sign appeared.
→ [ Dragon, come here. ]

Gulp.

'It's scary, but there's only one way. I have no choice but to go.'

Kuro walked on as the sign directed.


***

The end of the signs. The one who appeared there was Lackness.

A vast hall, tiered layer upon layer like a medieval opera house.
Within it stood dozens of full-length mirrors in ornate frames.
The mirrors reflected Lackness from many angles.

Between the mirrors, mannequins in scorched clothes stood like specters, and plastered all over the partition walls were pictures portraying the Black Dragon maliciously, as a villain. On the surfaces of the mirrors, words had been scrawled in red paint.

[ Why not know your 罪? ]
[ NAZE SHIRANAI KISAMANO TSUMI? ]

In case the English wasn't understood, he had kindly written out a translation as well.
This was the Exhibition Hall of Condemnation that Herbaeken had spent the whole summer break preparing.

That was when it happened.

"At last, you're alone. Lackness."

The voice of Herbaeken, who did not show himself, echoed through the entire hall.

"....So the one you have business with is me after all."

Bang!

A bullet buried itself at Lackness's feet. Lackness turned his head toward where the shot had come from, but Herbaeken had already hidden himself behind a pillar.

"Why did you destroy the kingdom?"
"...My past life."

Lackness looked around him.
These crude installations were clearly aimed at him.

Lackness's eyes trembled faintly, then sank.

"...Did I truly destroy the kingdom?"

The moment Herbaeken, hidden behind the pillar, heard those words, he couldn't contain his fury and fired once more.

Bang—! Crash—!

The bullet struck a mirror near Lackness.
Sharp fragments scattered in all directions.
Lackness's reflection showed hideously in the broken mirror.
Lackness swept his gaze around quickly, searching for his opponent's position.
Just then, a voice flew from somewhere unseen.

"....Don't play dumb!"

A voice laced with hatred rang through the hall.

"The last king of the legendary kingdom of the dragon clan, Lackness. A thousand years ago, you were the worst king of all."

Herbaeken went on, holding his voice in check.

"That kingdom was a blessed land, built up by the power of the dragon clan. So why did you bestow no power at all, slaughter the people who believed in and followed the royal house, and burn and destroy the kingdom!?"
"....."

Lackness wondered. What on earth was he?

The Black Dragon of the legend Herbaeken had first told, the strongest of all dragons. The decline of the dragon clan that the Fox Spirit had spoken of, and the Black Dragon's power, manifested and come to dwell after a thousand years. And now the record of a massacre he could not accept, as told by the adversary before him.

At last, Lackness bound his fragmented self into one.

A cruel monarch who, though he possessed the power to build up a kingdom, far from bestowing blessings, burned that kingdom with his own hands and obliterated even its records. And a being who, having forgotten the sins of his past, had shamelessly come back to life.

That this was what he was.

"......"

Lackness furrowed his brow, straining to recall the truth beyond his lost memories.

"Answer me, Lackness!!!"

Lackness flinched. And dropped his gaze.
After a long silence, he spoke carefully.

"...Regrettably, I truly have no memory of my past life."
"...Hah! Of course. That shameless attitude of yours, wiping away your own sins just like that."

Click. Behind the pillar, Herbaeken loaded his gun.

"You are the worst Black Dragon after all!!"

At that moment

"Are you a descendant of those people, Herbaeken."

At Lackness's question, Herbaeken's eyes twitched.
He froze, shocked that his identity had been exposed even though he was hidden behind the pillar.

"........Though I have no memory of my past life, if it is true that I destroyed the people with my flames, then I offer my deepest apology."

Lackness bowed his head, courteously.
At the sight, Herbaeken's eyes went wide, shaken.

Grind.

Herbaeken clenched his teeth again.

"Don't make me laugh. As if that could earn you forgiveness for everything."

He took aim once more.

"The resentment and thirst for revenge you planted in my people have carried on to this very day!!!!"

Herbaeken's emotions reached their peak.

"And it has to end with your death!!!!!!"

That was when it happened.

"Kuro!"

Eunho's shout broke the hush of the hall.
Lackness turned his head and looked at Eunho.

"......Wait, Lackness?"

Eunho approached Lackness sheepishly.
Herbaeken glared at Eunho, gritting his teeth.

"That guy...... how did he get all the way here...!?"

Eunho, who had been locked away so that he couldn't interfere with this execution, and yet had gotten out without a scratch. Not knowing Eunho's true identity, Herbaeken couldn't make sense of this absurd situation.

Lackness shot a quick glance at where Herbaeken had been.
Confirming that Herbaeken's killing intent had subsided the moment Eunho appeared, Lackness immediately returned to Kuro.

"......Are? Eunho?"

Kuro stared at Eunho blankly.

"Are? Arere? Boku... used the power again....!?"

Flustered, Kuro looked around at the wreckage surrounding him.
Eunho let out a sigh of relief and put a hand on Kuro's shoulder.

"......As long as you're safe. Let's find Herbaeken and get out of here fast."
"Y-yeah;;"

Not knowing what was going on, Kuro followed after Eunho.
Putting the complexity of the situation aside, Eunho kept watch on their surroundings and led Kuro out of the hall.

"Tch."

Herbaeken gathered his weapons and retreated into the darkness.

Eunho and Herbaeken. The one who would protect and the one who would kill—this was the beginning of their confrontation.

[End of Ep. 7]
