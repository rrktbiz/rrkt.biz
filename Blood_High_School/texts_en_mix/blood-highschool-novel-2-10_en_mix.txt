▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 10

The living room of the Ryuo house.
On the living room table, several of the Ryuo family's products lay unwrapped.

Back at the Ryuo house after meeting Aoi, Kuro thought of him with a worried expression.

'I wonder if Aoi-san is okay?'

Just then, he heard someone's footsteps approaching the living room.
It was Eunho and Miko.

"Huh, Eunho? And Miko, too?"

When Kuro asked, puzzled, Eunho spoke heavily.

"Kuro. I have something to tell you."

Eunho's voice was low.

Kuro's eyes widened at Eunho's serious air, so unlike his usual self.

And Eunho told Kuro the whole truth.

The sins of the Ryuo family. And the existence of the Kagehira family, holding out in hell, bearing the full weight of that karma. All of it.

All the while he listened, Kuro's eyes trembled faintly.


***

At that hour, Herbaeken was still face to face with the Being he had met in the cathedral. The tale of the dragon legend resumed.

< "At the far edge of the dragon kingdom's despair stood its last king, Lackness." >

Along with the Being's voice, afterimages of the dragon kingdom going to ruin unfolded. The dragon kingdom, once called a paradise, was now no different from hell. A wasteland that not even invaders would enter.

< "Lackness despaired deeply." >

***

The despairing face of the Lackness of old overlapped with the face of Kuro, sitting in the living room in the present. Having heard the whole truth from Eunho, Kuro had gone ashen.

Thrown into confusion, Kuro argued a textbook case for hope to Eunho. That they had to help, that they had to get them out somehow—naive words of the sort you would find in a schoolbook.

But Eunho broke those clumsy hopes one by one.

Feeling like a fool at the fact that he alone had known nothing, Kuro was flooded with shame. And, unlike his usual self, he took it out on Eunho, nitpicking and arguing.

Eunho faced Kuro with unwavering eyes. The stage of veiling the truth out of consideration for Kuro was over. He no longer held his words back.

Eunho added that it was all right, that he was not alone, but Kuro, reeling from the shock the truth had dealt him, could not hear Eunho's words.

Standing by, Miko broke into a cold sweat at the heavy air between the two. It was the first time she had seen Eunho, who always shielded Kuro, force him to look at reality so cruelly.

When Kuro, unable to find words to argue back, fell silent, a brief hush followed. Miko cautiously spoke up and pointed out how impossible 'salvation' was in practical terms.

Seeing Miko shake her head, Kuro soon let his own head drop limply, in despair.

< "With the power left to him, he could save no one." >

The despair of Lackness, against the backdrop of the royal palace of old, overlapped perfectly with the Kuro of now.

Kuro remembered.
The conversations he had had with Aoi.

Aoi, laughing that he was a family head too.
Why he had moved from place to place so often, praying to the land gods. Why he had so many little brothers and sisters.
Why he had asked whether there was a god who would accept even a murderer.

Every piece of the puzzle fell into place.

And, adding his own imagination, Kuro pictured Aoi coughing up blood, looking as though he would die at any moment.

"Aoi-san..."


*

Just then.

"Is that true?"

Jiji appeared at the living room door.

"The head of the Kagehira family has let us off?"
"...."

When the three gave no answer, Jiji let out a 'phew—' of relief, as if he had finally been spared. He cleared his throat, ahem, and went on.

"Yes, well, it's ancient history by now—too late to be assigning blame."

Jiji came up to the three and warned them.

"Kuro! Miko! Listen well! When Yoshiyuki was head, he needlessly paid compensation for every little thing, and thanks to that, the Ryuo family is in no position to take responsibility for another family now! If their side says they'll let it go, then that's that! Listen to your grandfather while I'm still asking nicely!"

Arms crossed, Jiji was overbearing.
The three defied Jiji with silence, and with eyes tinged with loathing.

Their positions and feelings each differed, but in their loathing for the ugliness of the Ryuo family, they were as one.

Sensing that defiant current, Jiji blustered a threat.

"You lot, don't go getting any foolish ideas! Robbing the Ryuo family of its power means making enemies of the people of the Ryuo family!"

When the three still did not lower their eyes, shocking words burst from the mouth of the flustered Jiji.

"If you don't want to end up like Yoshiyuki and his wife, keep quiet and......!!!!!!"

Silence.
All the air in the living room froze at once.
Jiji stiffened, with the look of a man who has let something slip.

Eunho broke the silence in a fierce voice.

"The ones who killed Yoshiyuki and his wife......"

Miko went on, staring at Jiji with madness in her eyes.

"were people of the Ryuo family......?"

And the expression of the one most shocked of all—Yoshiyuki's son, Kuro—changed horribly.

The ugly truth hidden behind the deaths of his father and mother.
His father, who had walked the right path for the family's future, and even his mother, who had followed that father—only now did he learn that they had been eliminated by the family.

"Hghk—!"

Clutching at his tightening heart, Kuro braced a hand against the floor.

"Kuro..? Stay with me!"

Flustered, Eunho gripped Kuro's shoulders and shook him.

At the same time, Miko tore into Jiji savagely.

"What is that supposed to mean! Explain yourself properly!"
"W-washi didn't kill them, no ja!"

Ba-dump, ba-dump.

The sounds around him grew ever more distant. Eunho's cries as he shook him hard, Miko's sharp questioning as she pressed Jiji, the cowardly voice of Jiji spluttering excuses in a panic—all of it was drowned out by Kuro's ragged breathing and the sound of his heart.

Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.

The sound of his heart grew fiercer and fiercer.
Kuro gasped for breath.

'Ah. How stupid.'

He was a nagging, worldly grandfather, but Kuro had tried hard to honor him within the fence called family. With nowhere to go but this Ryuo house, he had endured, telling himself that it couldn't be helped.

The relatives were the same. They didn't particularly cherish Kuro, but neither did they treat him badly. They were simply 'ordinary.' And that ordinariness, in this very moment, came at Kuro in the worst form in the world.

'Grandpa. So this is how awful you were. And the people of the Ryuo family, too.'

Something Miko had once said to him flashed by.

[ 'You really don't suspect a thing, do you.' ]

As Kuro's mind writhed in agony from the great shock, Lackness stirred within him. At the same time, brief memories of Lackness's past life, reaching back across eons, flashed by.

The successive dragon kings, recorded in splendor on stone tablets.
And the memories went back further still, to before those tablets even existed—his own field of vision as he first set foot upon the land. And for that very reason, a sorrow all the deeper.

The memories of Kuro and Lackness began to jumble together at random.

[ "My lord the Dragon King." ] [ "Please look kindly on me, Dragon God-sama." ]

The people who had been dying on barren land that had lost its blessing; the Kagehira family, waiting only for a peaceful death in a swamp of karma. In essence, they were horribly alike.

An abandoned land that no one could save.

And the thing most alike of all
was the 'helplessness' of Lackness, face to face with that horror.

'Aoi-san, I'm sorry. There's no way...'

Kuro's mind could hold out no longer. Within his collapsed mind, as if to fill the void, the mind of Lackness shielded Kuro. Kuro and Lackness were now resonating more intensely than ever before, melting into one.

Kuro accepted Lackness. And with that, the sensation of the eons that lay within Lackness revived. That sensation, recorded across the eons, was 'emptiness.' The emptiness in which, if everything lies sunk in despair, even the meaning of protecting it is lost.

The back of the Lackness who, a thousand years ago, had made his decision for a kingdom going to ruin overlapped once more with the Lackness of the present.

"Yurusanai. Neither the Ryuo family (the dragon clan), who have not paid for their sins, nor myself."

< "Unable to bear the despair, Lackness at last—" >

The soul of Lackness slowly rose from where it sat, as if to carry out an appointed destiny.

And he proclaimed coldly.



"There is an order to things. First, the sin-steeped dragon clan."




< "began to burn and destroy everything, one thing after another." >




It was ruin, once again.




"—!!!!"



Eunho and Miko let out soundless screams.




*

Rumble, crash—-!!

Dark clouds gathered over the Ryuo house, and lightning struck.

Patter...

The living room was strewn on every side with holed walls and broken furniture. At the center of it, Eunho and Lackness faced each other.

At the moment Lackness moved to kill Jiji, Eunho had barely managed to block his way. Behind Eunho, Jiji was frozen in terror, mouth hanging open.

"Kuh.."

Eunho was holding Lackness back with all his might. His hands, gripping Lackness, trembled violently.

Lackness stopped his attack. For the Eunho before his eyes was not someone Lackness had to kill right now.

"Move, Eunho."

At the words Lackness spat coldly, Eunho's eyes wavered. The name he had called him by was not 'silver kitsune' but 'Eunho.' That was what Kuro called him.

"...Kuro!?"

Eunho asked Kuro.

"What on earth are you thinking, Kuro."

Watching the scene, Miko was horrified.

'That's... Kuro!?'

The words that came from Lackness's mouth were clear.

"...After killing the dragon clan, the root of it all, I have to free everyone from this suffering."
"..You're going to kill everyone!? Are you serious!?"
"Yes. I'm serious. I have no hesitation whatsoever."

There was not the slightest waver in Lackness's expression.

"I (ware) have accepted this power now. My power can burn and destroy everything. I can end it in an instant. This is something only I can do."

For an instant, Eunho sensed Kuro. But within him, the hesitation and guilt particular to humans had been excised. It was as though he were imitating the mercy of an arrogant god, looking down from on high upon the suffering of lowly lives, convinced that he alone was the savior who could end that suffering.

Eunho clenched his teeth hard.

'Kuro doesn't talk like this...!! No doubt about it. Kuro has been swept away by the power!'

In that instant, a fragment flashed through Eunho's mind.

"...!"

'Destruction.' That was what Lackness had done a thousand years ago.
Kuro, plunged into the depths of despair, was now about to repeat the very same act as in his past life. In that case.

'Don't tell me the reason Lackness destroyed the kingdom was this...!!'

It was just as Eunho grasped the whole chain of cause and effect.

Crash—!

Behind Eunho came the racket of someone running, shoving furniture out of the way. Seizing that instant, Jiji was fleeing outside. Lackness's eyes flashed fiercely and followed the fleeing Jiji.

Flash.

Lackness moved. At an inhuman speed, as if skipping across space, Eunho was thrust back.

But Eunho, too, was no human—he was a Fox Spirit.
He shot forward at once and swiftly blocked Lackness's way.

"?"

Lackness's eyes narrowed, questioning Eunho's reaction speed. While Lackness was trying to work something out, Eunho shouted.

"Killing everyone...! That's wrong!!! You've just fallen into despair, that's all!! We have to struggle against fate..!!"

Lackness was silent for a moment, then answered.

"And is a wretched death like this what comes of struggling against fate?"

Miko's and Eunho's hearts dropped with a lurch.
That 'wretched death' referred to 'his own parents.'

Eunho went on, without giving up.

"Kuro. Even if everything feels hopeless, a heartfelt wish leaves so much behind. We have always been with Yoshiyuki's teachings, and we can overcome this."

Silence fell.
But the answer that came back was firm.

"...No. I can't overcome it. It was all hypocrisy. A hypocrisy that comes easily, so long as you look away from the despair on the far side of the world."

Lackness was hacking Kuro to pieces.

"No! It was the best you could do!"

Eunho shot back, but Lackness had already reached a different conclusion.

"...The best. For the me of before, perhaps it was. But the me of now knows what the true best is. For I have the power to resolve everything. That is why a power like this was bestowed. To put an end to all the despair and sorrow in the world."

Colossal blood-red flames burst from Lackness's body. His bearing was that of a twisted king, more arrogant and more chilling than anyone.

And in a tone so grave it was hard to believe this was Kuro, he proclaimed.

"Destruction alone is the one salvation that can deliver the world from hell. My mercy is to burn this world to the last, and grant it the repose of nothingness, where nothing remains."

Power had gained a will.
And thus the Black Dragon Lackness, who had destroyed the kingdom of the dragon clan a thousand years ago, at last made his true advent.

Even Eunho, a spirit, felt an instinctive fear of that power.

"......."

But the only reason he felt fear was that destructive 'power,' and nothing else.


*


Eunho changed his manner and, as if exasperated beyond belief, lit into the 'Kuro' hidden in Lackness's form.

"Don't make me laugh, you. Without Lackness's power you'd have done nothing but cry, and now that you've gotten a bit of power, you think it's fine to kill everyone?"

At Eunho's scathing, mocking words, Miko, watching, started and trembled.

"Snap out of it. That's not the best. You just want to get your own pain out of your sight, and you're making excuses!!"
"......!"

Lackness's eyes wavered faintly.

"—!..."

In spite of herself, Miko's lips moved to stop the two. The sight of them at odds came to her as a terror, as if the only warmth in the Ryuo family were dying out.


*

Feeling displeasure, Lackness unleashed blood-red flames, meaning to blow Eunho away.

But Eunho was not blown away. The human aura that had enveloped his body crumbled, and a bluish divine aura seeped out. For an instant, the form of a numinous fox wavered beyond Eunho's shadow.

Lackness's eyes wavered violently.
At last he realized. That the childhood friend before his eyes was the Fox Spirit.

".....You deceived me."
"......"

Lackness's voice was low and calm.
Within it lay disappointment and anger.

Eunho did not answer. He only glared at Lackness, aching at the betrayal Kuro must feel.

"You deceived me. Even you."

Lackness's voice strained to hold his emotions down, but it could not hide its tremor. Each time the forcibly suppressed emotion leaked out, Lackness's shoulders trembled faintly. Kuro was in there, after all.

"That's right, I deceived you!"

Eunho shouted right back. The moment his identity was exposed, he instead threw off everything and cried out desperately.

"Because I couldn't be by your side in a form like that!"

Lackness frowned at Eunho's unexpected words.

"That spirit guy you believed in so much, y'know, was nothing but an insufferable jerk with zero sense of reality, handing down blessings from on high. But y'know what, I made a choice."

Eunho's eyes blazed.

"I left behind my destiny as a spirit and came down from that damned seat of the gods just to protect you! In the same form as you!!"

At Eunho's cry, tinged with a sense of injustice, every memory of the time spent with Eunho flashed through Lackness's mind.

He was surely furious at having been deceived, and yet, paradoxically, between those fragments of memory rose the tenacious trust he had placed in Eunho. In this whirlpool of two-sided emotion, Lackness gasped for breath.

"Kuro. You. You've got the order wrong. If you want to kill everyone that badly, get rid of me first."

At Eunho's remark, Lackness glared at him.

Eunho stepped into the blood-red flames Lackness was giving off. It was a gamble that staked the entire weight of the trust built up over seventeen years.

As a spirit who had come into being through faith, to be without that faith—that trust—was no different from losing his reason for existing. If Kuro were to bring down with his own hands the one he had trusted most, then the destruction of the world that would follow would be an easy thing.

Lackness understood what Eunho meant, and slowly moved.
Yes. The order was not the dragon clan first, but 'Eunho first.'

Lackness raised his hand to remove Eunho from before his eyes.


"............"


But the hand stopped in midair.


"............"


Kuro could not attack Eunho.


"............"


It was a strange thing indeed.

Eunho, whose identity and whose very name had been lies. A being who had gazed down from on high would know despair better than anyone. And yet he had wished for trust from Kuro—that contradictory figure might have been an outright lie. But the Kuro of now could, at the same time, tell. The more he dug through his memories to find Eunho's contradiction, the clearer it was that there had not been a single moment when Eunho was not acting for his sake.

In the end, what stayed Lackness's hand was the trust that Eunho had planted in Kuro's heart over seventeen years. To harm Eunho, with whom he had spent most of his life, hurt as much as carving out a piece of himself.

'No. This doesn't matter. Not something like this.'

Lackness reminded himself once more that what he felt now was by no means the answer. The stark despair of the world and the personal trust he was feeling now had, in truth, nothing to do with each other. To escape despair was to avert one's eyes from despair. And yet why was he—

"What have you done to me?"

Lackness asked Eunho.
Lackness's blood-red flames had long since lost their force.

At the question, the corner of Eunho's mouth lifted.

"...You can't do it, can you?"

Lackness frowned.

"Of course you can't. Do you know how much I've, all this time, for you—"
"....."

Eunho choked up and could say no more. But, feeling the relief of having confirmed that all his efforts had not been in vain, he slowly approached Lackness and met his eyes. And he said firmly.

"You must not carelessly trample the heartfelt wishes carved into this land."
"....."

Then Eunho's tone turned, for a moment, sad.

"And you mustn't carelessly trample your own heart, either."
"......"
"...You mustn't accept power in that way."
"....."

And those words reached the Kuro within Lackness.

Sssss—...

Lackness let the flames go out.

*

Silence.

Lackness had grown visibly docile, as though caught in Eunho's spell. Seeing this, Eunho and Miko let the tension go.

Eunho went back to his usual self and teased Lackness.

"In other words! In this life, you can't pull any of that obnoxious god-of-destruction nonsense. Aah, Kuro. Just admit it already. So—"

Eunho dropped the light tone and whispered in the gentle tone of the Fox Spirit.

"Come down from there, Kuro."
"!"

That quiet invitation reached deep into Kuro. Just as Eunho had first come down from the seat of the gods to stay by Kuro's side, now, hoping that Kuro would abandon that lonely throne and come back to the warmth where he had been, Eunho took Kuro's hand and was pulling him down.

Sssh—

Lackness's aura dispersed.

Thud.

What was left there was only a wounded, fragile boy—Kuro—who crumpled to the floor.

*

The dark clouds that had covered the Ryuo house lifted, and a single ray of light poured down through the clouds.

Kuro spoke, on the verge of tears.

"...Jaa, dou sureba ii no...? What do I have to do, to save everyone..."

Kuro wept.

Eunho, holding Kuro up in his arms, gave no answer.

A hollow, empty laugh spread across the lips of Miko, who had been watching in a daze.

"Why.... what is this...?"

Through the broken roof, amid the fallen wreckage,
light settled upon the three of them.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 10]
