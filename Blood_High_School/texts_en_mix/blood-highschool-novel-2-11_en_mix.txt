▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 11

The past. The mountain where the Fox Spirit dwells.

"Uwaaaah~!!"

With screams, the construction site turned to pandemonium in an instant.

"How dare you trample sacred ground. You foolish humans...!!"

The voice of the enraged Fox Spirit rang out.
In that moment, Yoshiyuki stood before them.

***

After that day, Yoshiyuki came to the mountain time and again and devoutly made offerings. The Fox Spirit, having taken a half-human form, asked Yoshiyuki.

"I am grateful that you protected the mountain, but you have a purpose, do you not?"
"Me? I'm merely a pacifist, you know?"

At Yoshiyuki's coy answer, Eunho clicked his tongue.

"You sly snake of a human. Out with it."


***

A small newlywed home.
Simple, modest furniture could be seen.

There were Yoshiyuki and his wife, Tsugumi. Tsugumi, shy with strangers, bashfully greeted the Fox Spirit. The Fox Spirit blinked at the sight. Then he caught an aura that was out of the ordinary.

"...There is life in your womb. Two."
"Eh! Twins!?"

Tsugumi touched her belly in surprise.

"However.. a power that must not exist in humans dwells in them."

At those words, Yoshiyuki's expression hardened.
He lowered his head, as if he had already suspected as much.

"...Just as I thought. I have a favor to ask of you, Lord Fox Spirit."


***

Time passed, and Kuro was born.
The house was filled with a baby's laughter.

From this time on, the Fox Spirit took the human name 'Eunho,' and was called so among the humans who did not know his true nature.

While Tsugumi looked after the baby, the Fox Spirit, in the form of a grown man, helped with the housework.

Hanging the laundry, Eunho thought back.
To the deep conversation he had had with Yoshiyuki about the child's future.

At that time, the Fox Spirit had warned him.

[ "We succeeded in sealing Kuro's side.. but if a situation comes that threatens his survival, even that will be of no use. So long as the power dwells in him, it cannot be avoided." ]

Hearing that, Yoshiyuki wore an expression that said they could not rest easy yet.

The painting at the Ryuo family's dragon shrine came into view. Whether the dragon that had come to dwell after a thousand years would be a sacred being or a monster, no one could know.


*

"Spirit-sama!"

Startled by Tsugumi's sudden call in the midst of his recollection, Eunho turned around.

"I'll do that. Please watch Kuro for me!"

Eunho answered with a troubled look.

"It's fine. You're still not feeling well, Tsugumi."
"Even I want to move around once in a while, you know~"
"W-well... in that case.."

She wasn't wrong, so Eunho traded places with Tsugumi.
Tsugumi deftly settled Kuro into Eunho's arms.

"Ueh."

Eunho found baby Kuro difficult to handle, yet the look in his eyes as he gazed at him was endlessly gentle. Hanging the laundry, Tsugumi smiled at the sight of that awkward spirit.


***

Before they knew it, it was the third year of raising Kuro together.
Tsugumi and Eunho handled the child-rearing and housework.
Yoshiyuki spent busy days out earning money.

Yoshiyuki's request had been specific. 'Stay by his side' was meant in a truly practical sense. To guard Kuro from the closest place possible, he had to become his 'friend.'

And so, before the three-year-old Kuro, Eunho appeared, transformed to be the same age. Kuro hid behind his mother, shy.

"Hora, Kuro, it's Eunho da yo~ He's the same age as you now~"

Tsugumi introduced Eunho to Kuro.
Kuro stared intently at Eunho with a puzzled expression.

"Eunno.."

The Eunho in Kuro's memory had, until just yesterday, been the grown man who looked after him. But being a child, he readily accepted this magical turn of events.

"He got chicchai..?"

Just as Yoshiyuki and Eunho had expected, Kuro gradually lost his memories of the adult Eunho. 'Eunho' was a child left in their care by an acquaintance of Yoshiyuki's due to unavoidable circumstances. That was the general cover story.


***

Time passed again, to the year Kuro turned seven.
The head of the Ryuo family, Ryuo Ren, died.
Sitting across the three-person dining table, Yoshiyuki declared.

"Lord Spirit. We will be entering the Ryuo family. I've been asked to serve as head, you see."

Eunho asked back, with a look of surprise.

"But you never wanted to go there!"
"That's right, but.. there is a child there I must protect, too."


***

"It's all mine~!!! ヽ(ｏ`皿′ｏ)ﾉ"

With Kuro's cousin Miko coming into the home, the household turned to pandemonium in an instant. Having been raised until the age of seven by the villain Ryuo Ren, Miko was the very definition of a 'problem child.' Even Tsugumi, who had only ever looked after gentle Kuro, was at a loss when faced with this little terror.

"Ueeen~!! (˚ ˃̣̣̥᷄⌓˂̣̣̥᷅ )"

Eunho hurriedly wrapped the crying Kuro in his arms, aghast.


***

The Ryuo family dojo.

The three Ryuo children devoted themselves to their lessons under Yoshiyuki's guidance. Through kendo, Yoshiyuki taught them to accept victory and defeat with humility, and the courtesy of respecting one's opponent. Next they learned physical training, and the mental discipline of sitting on their knees and governing the heart.

After lessons came field trips, roaming the hills and fields.
Observing streams and small creatures, they learned how precious the world is.

Miko, having become Yoshiyuki's disciple and receiving private lessons, grew noticeably brighter. Before long, Miko would even hold tight the hand of Kuro, who lagged behind.

Eunho, like the eldest brother; Miko, reformed and sharp as a tack; Kuro, like the youngest; Tsugumi, caring devotedly for the children; and the dinner table, aglow with happiness as they welcomed Yoshiyuki home late from work. Eunho watched them with a contented heart.

But the peace did not last long.


***

When Kuro was ten.

"Uwaaaan~~ Mommy, Daddyyy~~"

The funeral of Yoshiyuki and Tsugumi.
On the altar, the memorial portraits of the two were set side by side.
Behind the crying Kuro and Miko, the relatives' whispering went on.

"They say it was an accident."
"Who's going to be head now?"
"I hear the old master's taking it up again."
"Then what about the children? Our house is muri, muri~"
"They say the old master will take them in~"
"By the way, who's that child?"
"A child left by an acquaintance of Yoshiyuki's, apparently?"

In that moment, Eunho made a deep vow in his heart.

'I have to protect them.'

***

And so Kuro and Miko were taken in by Jiji.
Eunho, now with no grounds for staying in the Ryuo house, was thrown out.

Mealtimes with Jiji and the children were bleak.
Jiji, endlessly spouting nonsense while leaving them to fend for themselves.
Kuro and Miko's walks to school were always gloomy.

But whenever they spotted Eunho waiting along the way, both their faces lit up.

Eunho came and went at the Ryuo main house all the time.
Jiji would berate him—"Why, you, what business have you coming to someone else's house day in and day out"—but Eunho played it off and held his ground.

For a Fox Spirit who had raged that his own shrine grounds were violated to go and upend the grounds of humans would not be right. And so Eunho humored Jiji and did all he could to stay near the children.

'Whatever it takes.'

Thwack.

A prospectus for new students at an elite girls' middle school was laid on the dining table.

"I'm going to a boarding school for middle school. I can't stand the sight of Jiji."

It was Miko's firm declaration.
Jiji and Miko had fought day after day, and at last it blew up.
Kuro was terribly sad to be parted from Miko.
And so the children of the Ryuo family were split apart.

The Fox Spirit was, truly, of no use.

***

"Miko, are you eating properly? Is anything giving you trouble?"

To Eunho's calls, Miko would more often than not snap, "Stop calling so much!!" and hang up. Eunho sometimes had his fox familiars from the forest keep watch over Miko.

Miko, whose strong spiritual power had let her know Eunho's true nature long ago, drove off the fox familiars hovering around her with a "Get lost!"

Before long, Kuro became a middle schooler and went to school in a high-collared uniform. For the sake of the 'ordinary life for his son' that Yoshiyuki had so wished for, Eunho gladly blended into the youth of a human boy.

But when night fell, the air changed.

In the dark room, Kuro, still longing for his parents, asleep before the Buddhist altar. Eunho quietly took Kuro's hand.

'No matter what trials come, I must never let go of this hand.'

***

The hand held in the past carried through to the present.
Kuro's fingertips twitched faintly.

The guest room of the Ryuo house, which had at least remained intact.
Kuro, lying on the bed, at last opened his eyes.
Beside him, Eunho felt that small movement and looked at Kuro.
Kuro slowly looked at Eunho.
And, his mind still hazy, he spoke.

"...Eunho."
"....I'm sorry I deceived you."

Eunho apologized quietly.
Kuro smiled.

"It's okay."

Just then, Miko, hearing the two talking, came rushing into the room.

Relieved that the two people he held most dear were by his side, Kuro let slip a drowsy smile.

"You know, I had a dream of the old days.. Dad and Mom.. Miko, Eunho.. the Ryuo family where we all lived together."

At those words, Miko's and Eunho's expressions turned sorrowful.

"I'm sorry I never understood how you felt until now, Miko."

Miko stopped short, as if surprised by the unexpected apology,
then lowered her head with a sad expression.

Kuro then opened his hand and looked at it.

".....Boku, I tried to trample everything with this power again."

At last, Kuro understood.
That Lackness and he were originally a single soul.
That the cruel self he had so denied and pushed away was, in the end, also a part of him.

Kuro closed the hand he had opened.

"Let's go. To the Kagehira family. I've decided the path I have to take."

***

A few days after everything had settled down.

The children of the Ryuo family—Kuro, Miko, and Eunho—met face to face with Aoi, the head of the Kagehira family. At Kuro's decision, Aoi looked surprised.

[ 'The Ryuo family fully accepted the Kagehira family as members of the family.' ]

[ 'That was the final wish that Yoshiyuki, in the end, had been unable to see through.' ]

In the form of Lackness, Kuro summoned every blood relative of the Ryuo family to one place and declared it unilaterally.

To the elders of the family, the sight was taken as 'the wrath of the Dragon God,' belatedly come down upon them. The terrified relatives clung to him, begging for their lives.

***

The Ryuo family changed. No, it was in the midst of changing.

The Ryuo house that Lackness had smashed up was being remodeled while they were at it, and several members of the Kagehira family had moved in first, becoming new members of the household.

Among them, Aoi's younger sister 'Yuka' bowed her head politely in greeting. She was that very girl who, only a short while ago, had faced off so menacingly against Miko and Eunho.

Every time she faced Yuka, Miko recalled that battle and broke into a cold sweat, but being the same sex, she was put fully in charge of helping Yuka settle into the family. Yuka trailed after Miko like a baby chick.

Shiro just fumbled about, bewildered by the changed atmosphere of the Ryuo house.

Kuro watched the changing Ryuo family with a grave expression.

[ 'In the end, Kuro walks Yoshiyuki's path.' ]

In the eyes of the Fox Spirit watching from behind, there was only a youthful boy who had just taken his first step as head.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 11]
