▶Ep. 6 A Tanabata Night and the Silver Fox

Summer came, and with it the day of the town festival.

"Whoa, a festival!"

Herbaeken led the way in high spirits, and Kuro and Eunho followed behind, chatting. The trio strolled through the festival, each in a yukata that suited him.

In the middle of the plaza, the majestic beat of taiko drums rang out.
Stalls stretched on in endless rows through the pleasantly bustling crowd.

Herbaeken threw himself into the goldfish-scooping game. When Kuro, unable to scoop a single one, looked disappointed, Eunho coolly held out the goldfish he had caught.

"Hora."
"You're giving it to me?"

Kuro happily took the goldfish.

Next stop.
The trio stuffed their mouths with tasty street food.
Eunho had takoyaki, Kuro a candy apple, and Herbaeken a chocolate banana.

And the next stop.
Eunho and Herbaeken each bought a mask they liked at a stall.

"Pfft, a dragon mask!"

Kuro burst out laughing at Herbaeken's dragon mask.

"Kitsune de~su!"

Wearing a fox mask, Eunho did a goofy fox impression.


Among friends fooling around in silly masks, Kuro laughed more brightly than ever.


***

"Ah~! They're selling tanzaku over there!"

At Herbaeken's shout, the trio trooped over to the booth selling tanzaku, the paper wish strips of Tanabata.

"Wooow, yellow for me!"
"Purple!"
"Red.."

Just as each had picked his favorite color, a familiar voice reached them.

"Hai, douzo~ 300 yen desu."

Kuro and Eunho froze on the spot.
The attendant who handed over the tanzaku was none other than Miko.
Only Herbaeken, who didn't know Miko, innocently accepted the paper.

"Miko!? What are you doing here!?"

To Kuro's shocked question, Miko answered as if it were nothing.

"Part-time job. If I'm going to run the Ryuo family in the future, hands-on experience is a must."
"Miko, you're always working so hard......."

Kuro gazed at Miko with eyes full of awe.
Unable to follow what was going on, Herbaeken asked Eunho.

"Who's that?"
"Kuro's relative."

And so the little incident with Miko came to a close.


***

"Minna, do you know? The legend of this town!"


"There he goes~ the legend otaku."

Eunho answered in a voice of weary resignation, but Herbaeken went on, eyes sparkling.

"Long ago, near that mountain over there, they enshrined a Fox Spirit who protected this town, but people gradually stopped visiting, and it became an abandoned shrine, they say. When they tried to tear it down for local development, the Fox Spirit attacked the people, so they still haven't been able to demolish it and the shrine remains to this day, they say."

The legend of the Fox Spirit on the hill out back. That was all there was to the story.

"Fox Spirit-sama, kawaisou. He'd have nowhere left to go...."

Kuro's heart genuinely ached.

"...."

For some reason, Eunho looked off somewhere else, feigning disinterest.


*

That was when it happened.
The festival crowd began to swell at a frightening pace.

"Whoa, hey, it's.. getting more and more crowded...."

No sooner had Eunho spoken than people surged in like a tsunami. The three were scattered in an instant.

"Uwaah, minna~~!!"
"Herbaeken!"

Herbaeken had already been swept up in the crowd and was drifting far away.

"Kuro, grab my hand!"

Luckily the remaining two had been close to each other, and they held hands tight and slipped out of the crowd.

***

The hill behind the festival grounds.

Eunho and Kuro took refuge there.
Eunho caught his ragged breath, and Kuro sat down on a nearby rock.

"We got separated from Herbaeken.. There's no signal at all."

He held his phone up to the sky, but with so many people around, there was no reception. Then, feeling something off about his foot, Kuro looked down.

"Whoa! The strap on my geta snapped!"

To make matters worse, even his footwear had given out.

"I'll go buy you a pair. Wait here. Oh, and while I'm at it I'll go find Herbaeken too~"
"Mm, arigatou~"


*

And so Eunho left, and Kuro was alone.
As he looked around, a small shrine caught his eye.

'Come to think of it, this is..'

A small shrine set atop a knee-high stone platform. Its red paint was peeling, as if it hadn't been tended in a long time, and a pair of stone foxes stood on either side in front of it.

'The Fox Spirit shrine Herbaeken was talking about?'

Before it, Kuro quietly closed his eyes, bowed his head, and prayed.

"Fox Spirit-sama, arigatou for protecting this town."

In that instant, a mystical aura filled the air, and a voice came.

"Kuro."

Startled, Kuro opened his eyes and raised his head.
Someone was perched in a tree, looking down at him.

'......Eh? Eunho?'

For a moment Kuro doubted his eyes.
A face that looked exactly like Eunho's. But he soon dismissed that mistaken impression.

The being before him was dressed in ancient divine vestments, with silver fur and a tail that split into many. Fox ears rising from his silver hair, and beneath them, swaying knotted-cord earrings. The red rouge drawn sharply along the corners of his eyes accentuated his cool gaze, lending him an unapproachable majesty.

Above all, he wore a cool, deep expression that Eunho would never make. The very air he gave off was so different, so numinous, that Kuro judged by instinct: this being was not human.

"Fox Spirit-sama!?"

At Kuro's astonished question, the Fox Spirit met his eyes steadily and answered.

"Do not be afraid. Watashi wa a dear friend of Yoshiyuki. The Fox Spirit."
"Fox Spirit-sama is.. my dad's.. friend?"

The Fox Spirit narrowed his eyes and continued his explanation.

"You were too young back then to understand, but Yoshiyuki was a once-in-a-generation onmyoji who devoted his entire life to the coexistence of monsters and humans."

The Fox Spirit looked at the shrine, remembering.

"When this place was on the verge of being torn down, Yoshiyuki protected it alongside me. That was when our bond was formed."
"So that's.. how it was...."

Kuro was simply at a loss at the sudden story. Looking at him, the Fox Spirit added quietly.

"Kuro, I have something to tell you. In Yoshiyuki's place."


***

The two found a secluded spot and sat down side by side on a rock.

"You know that you have the power of the dragon, yes?"

At the Fox Spirit's question, Kuro nodded.

"Ah, yeah. Because of it, my memory's been going blank lately...."
"That is the destiny of the Ryuo family. For the blood of the dragon clan runs through the Ryuo family."
"The dragon clan!?"

Kuro's eyes widened. The Fox Spirit's explanation continued.

"A thousand years ago, the dragon clan were originally beings born with power at their core. It is said they were monsters close to divine beasts. But for some reason, as time passed, the dragon clan lost their power and came to live no differently from humans. So much so that they forgot they were ever the dragon clan at all."

The Fox Spirit's gaze deepened.

"And now, a thousand years later, as if to make it known that the blood of the dragon clan ran in the Ryuo family, the power of the dragon came to dwell in a descendant of the Ryuo family. The name of that power is the Dark Dragon of Darkness, Lackness."

Kuro remembered the dragon mural Herbaeken had once explained to him.

"Lackness..! The one from the legend..?"
"Yes.... According to legend, Lackness is the dragon who destroyed the kingdom of the dragon clan... a dragon of colossal power."
"It can't be.."

Kuro's face went pale.

"Lackness burned everything in the kingdom, so almost no records remain. That is why no one could know why he did something so cruel."

The Fox Spirit's voice dropped low.

"But Yoshiyuki knew how terrifying that power was, and so he sealed it away the moment you were born. That is why you have been able to grow up until now as Kuro, a human. However, this seal has its limits—someday it will break, and the power of the dragon cannot help but awaken."

The Fox Spirit paused for a moment, then spoke again.

"Yoshiyuki asked me to watch over you. The colossal power that has manifested in your blood can no longer be held down, Kuro."

The Fox Spirit looked straight at Kuro.

"Because of that power, countless troubles have come upon you, and each time Lackness has protected you. But Kuro, a day will come when you too must recognize that power and protect yourself."
"...."

After a brief silence, Kuro asked in a puzzled voice.

"I understand that.. I've been protected all this time by a power I didn't even know about. But.. why can't I remember any of it?"
"! That's..."

The Fox Spirit stopped short in surprise, then answered carefully.

"Surely it is only that you are kind, Kuro, and so you naturally refuse to hurt anyone with that power. Do not fear it too much. It also protects you."
"Y-yeah... but like you said, someday I'll have to do it myself..."

Trailing off, Kuro clenched both hands tight on his knees. Watching him, the Fox Spirit smiled and reassured him.

"...Do not worry, Kuro. You can do it. And.. from now on, too, I will be watching over you."

The Fox Spirit gazed at Kuro with deep, quiet eyes.

"Watashi wo shinjiro. Believe in me."

With those words, as if time had stopped, their eyes locked in midair. To have a being as numinous as a spirit watching over him felt like a lot to carry, and yet Kuro felt a strange, ticklish flutter. He hurriedly looked away to hide his flushed face.

"Y-yeah.. Arigatou, Fox Spirit-sama."

Not noticing Kuro's state, the Fox Spirit went on seriously.

"...Lastly, Kuro, there is something I must tell you. You have a twin brother."

"Eh!? I have.. a brother?"

Kuro was stunned.

"Two were born that day. Ryuo Shiro. The power that child carries refused the seal and vanished. If he is a being with the same dragon power, he will come looking for you someday."

The night sky reflected in Kuro's startled eyes grew deeper and deeper.


***

In front of the Tanabata tree.

Countless tanzaku hanging from its branches.
The trio's wishes were written there.

[For all of us to have fun together ^^] - Kuro
[May Kuro be happy.] - Eunho
[Kill the dragon.] - Herbaeken

*
At that same hour.

A gunshot rang out from the shooting gallery stall.

Bang!

A stuffed dragon set out as a prize fell to the ground.
Smoke that should never have come out of it rose from the muzzle.

Leaving behind the stall owner, crushed at having his prizes cleaned out, Herbaeken walked off with cold eyes, a sack stuffed full of the dolls he had won.

Feeling betrayed by the two who hadn't come looking for him. . . ( ▀ 益 ▀ )
