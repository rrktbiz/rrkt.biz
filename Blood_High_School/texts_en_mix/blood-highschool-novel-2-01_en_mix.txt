▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 1

Tweet, tweet. Birdsong, heralding the morning.
This is the Ryuo family, the House of the Dragon King, heirs to the blood of dragons.

Ding—

To the chime of the prayer bell, the two brothers offered a short prayer before the memorial portraits of their parents.

"Ittekimasu~"
"Ittekimasu na no desu~"


*

"♪~"

Shiro was on his way to school with a slice of bread in his mouth.

"Shiro, what's that? You already ate breakfast."
"Hehe. I wanted to try going to school with bread in my mouth, no desu~"
"Mattaku, you're such an oddball~"

Up ahead, Eunho stood in his usual spot and greeted Kuro and Shiro. It was a peaceful walk to school.


***

'So I'm a second-year now...'

The classroom was filled to the brim with that floaty excitement unique to a new term.

"Ohayou, Herbaeken."

They exchanged their everyday classroom greetings.
And a small wish flickered in a corner of Kuro's heart.

'I really hope this year turns out ordinary.......'


***

After school, the Legend Research Club room.

Thud!

"This is every historical record my clan had."

Herbaeken set a thick stack of documents on the desk. It was every bit of material on the dragon legend that he had brought from the Herba clan. Kuro, Shiro, Eunho, Herbaeken. The four sat around the desk, surrounding the documents.

"I brought them thinking maybe there'd be a twist where you see these and your memories come back—or something."

"Ah...... that."

Eunho nodded in understanding and turned his head.

"Well? Lackness, Lumensia."

Lackness and Lumensia, who had been scanning through the documents.

Lackness, reading with perfect posture. And Lumensia, slouched and flipping carelessly through the pages, spat out his answer.

"I don't remember a single thing about any past life."

Lackness chimed in as well.

"Likewise."

Firm answers from them both.

"So that's how it is, after all......."

Herbaeken's reaction was matter-of-fact.
Eunho then summed up the situation.

"At this rate—"

Lubell was loafing on its own special cushion, paws tucked in like a little loaf of bread.

"We're not getting any information at all."

The stack of documents on the desk was piled high, but that was the result.


***

"Ah, baito time."

The clock pointed to five. Because of Herbaeken's part-time job, club activities ended earlier than usual.

Beneath the sunset sky, the shadows of the three who had seen Herbaeken off stretched long.

"Herbaeken, taihen da yo ne....... If I could, I'd tell him to stay at our place for a while. But it's Grandpa's house...... and there's no way Grandpa would allow it......."
"........."

At Kuro's worried words, Eunho groaned, "Gnnh~"

"That Jiji—he's that stingy even with you, his own flesh and blood. No way it'd work."
"......."

Herbaeken, who had come here to kill a dragon in accordance with his clan's will, only to be cast out by that very clan. Kuro knew, having heard the whole story from Eunho while Herbaeken was back at his family home. A life caught up in a clan's tragedy bound to 'dragons'—it didn't feel like someone else's problem, and it kept weighing on his mind.

On top of that, the helplessness of being unable to help a friend. If only an enemy worth defeating would show up, he could at least take it down for him.

When it came to real-world problems like this, power of the dragon or Fox Spirit or not, they were, in the end, nothing but students with no money.


***

The Ryuo house on the weekend.

Imported cars were parked in a row before the gate of the house. The car doors opened, and relatives of the Ryuo family, in formal attire, stepped out one after another. They made their way past the expensive lotus flowers blooming in the garden and into the house.

And Eunho came face to face with Miko.

"You look like you're in an even worse mood than usual today......."

Miko was giving off an ominous aura—gogogogo—.

"Atarimae desho. Kuro as head—it's absurd."

In her stormy mood, Miko walked right past Eunho.
Eunho followed after her.

Mingling with the stream of relatives, Eunho and Miko, too, headed into the house.


***

Inside a room,
before the memorial portraits of his parents on the Buddhist altar.
Kuro was squatting right in front of the photographs.

"Haah...... the day of the succession came after all......."

Kuro let out a heavy sigh.

It was the day of the succession—the day he would be seated as head by force, his wish not to take the position ignored.

"Otousan, okaasan. Do you think I can do a good job as head?"

It was while Kuro was mumbling with a troubled expression.

Watching from the threshold of the room, Shiro tilted his head, unable to understand why nii-chan was acting that way.


***

Outside the formal reception room of the Ryuo house.

Eunho, who did not share the family's blood, was waiting outside the door as if he were well used to it.

Inside the room, the relatives sat in long rows along either side. Arrayed to the left and right of the seat of honor, they were neatly dressed, and the atmosphere was solemn.

In the seat of honor sat Kuro, in a kimono.
To his right sat Jiji, and to his left, a step behind, sat Shiro.

The eldest of the Ryuo family and its current head, Jiji, began to speak.

"Ahem. And so~ I wish to announce that my grandson Kuro! shall become head of the Ryuo family, its rightful successor~ It is a little early, but I am an old man who could go any day now, after all! Ho ho."

Kuro wore an unnatural expression out of embarrassment.

"Our Kuro, no less, has the power of the dragon dwelling within him. Is that not a destiny ordained by heaven, telling him to walk the path of the head?"

The relatives nodded in agreement.
The mood was amicable.

"To cut a long story short, let us all help one another and live well this year too. ^^"


*

It was just as the succession ceremony was about to wrap up.

"Is that all?"

An old man snapped.
The relatives' eyes turned to him.

"When a power called the Dragon God has come to our family?"

He was the second eldest of the Ryuo family.
Kuro blinked.
The old man's impassioned speech went on.

"The Dragon God has surely appeared so that people will hold the Ryuo family in awe and serve it! Gather people and lead them down the right path! Be revered! That is the very road to the Ryuo family's revival!"

The old man shouted, spittle flying.

"We must go back to the way things were!! The way Ren did it!!"

'Ren... that would be.... the former head of the Ryuo family.'

Just as Kuro managed to place the name, Miko's icy voice rang out among the unsettled relatives.

"There is no such thing in the Ryuo family anymore."

Everyone's eyes turned to Miko.
Miko was glaring at the old man as if she could kill him.


***

A space for the children alone, having slipped out of the reception room.
Miko spoke irritably.

"Nani yo, 'the way things were.' That baldy."

The commotion had been settled for now, but questions remained.
Kuro cautiously broached the subject with Miko.

".....The Ryuo family of the past must really have been something, huh.."

Looking at Kuro, Miko answered.

"You know the gist of it too, don't you, Kuro? That the Ryuo family was riding high, did charity work, and had a good image."
"Y-yeah."

The Ryuo family of those days, as Kuro remembered it.
He didn't know the details, but there were so many photographs of Ren surrounded by people and children, and he had many followers. The world knew that Ren as 'the benevolent head.'

Over the adults' drinking party, flowing by like background noise, Miko spoke coldly.

"But contrary to appearances, it was rotten to the core behind the scenes."

The truth hidden behind the glory of the past.

"All that corruption—from what I hear, Yoshiyuki-sama worked hard to clean it out, starting from when he became head. Maybe because of that, the Ryuo family even went through a bankruptcy scare once."

The Ryuo family that Ren had corrupted, and Kuro's father, Yoshiyuki, who devoted himself to cleaning up the mess after Ren died.

Kuro asked without confidence.

"Um.. by corruption, you mean the stuff on the news, like tax evasion.. bribes.. da yo ne?"
"That's the basics. Beyond that, there was the 'faith business' and such."

Miko's explanation continued.

"In the past, the Ryuo family's mainstay was sorcery. But once the modern age came, growing big on sorcery alone would have been impossible, and the only business you can run with a shrine is the faith business. They used that as a foundation and rebranded into the everyday businesses we have now."

At this full-fledged talk of the Ryuo family's inner workings, Kuro looked dazed. The shrine, the restaurants, the hot springs... These were things his family had simply always had, as a matter of course, since before he was born, and so he had never once wondered how they had come to be.

"So that's how it was.. I didn't know.."

Miko looked at Kuro as though he were hopeless.

"You really don't suspect a thing, do you~ It's a dark chapter, so the adults are desperately hiding it, but this much you can figure out if you think a little."

Miko brought the conversation to a close.

"Anyway, even if it's clean now, becoming head means shouldering all of that past, too."

At those heavy words, the three so deeply entangled with the Ryuo family—
Eunho. Miko. Kuro. Their gazes crossed.

"Not that you could ever manage it, Kuro."
"Eh, that's already settled!?"
"Da-ka-ra- don't you dare put a scratch on the Ryuo family I've worked so hard to build up while you're head!!! Because I'm going to prove myself, again and again, and take that seat back!!"

Miko shook Kuro by the shoulders.
Watching from the side, Eunho put in a word with a look of weary resignation.

"Miko, you still haven't given up on being head.."
"Obviously, desho!?!? I'm giving all three of you lessons later! Even once I become head, you'll all be working hands for the Ryuo family."
"O-okay.."

Overpowered by Miko's vehemence, Kuro answered in a tiny voice.

"......"

Shiro, who had been listening quietly in the corner, sat awkwardly, unable to blend into this heavy atmosphere where the family's complicated affairs were being traded back and forth.


***

Meanwhile, two relatives who had stepped away from the drinking for a moment were whispering at the back of the yard.

"Honestly. How irreverent."
"That old man, really... how can he bring up those days?"

The two relatives clicked their tongues.

"Speaking of which, you have no idea what a relief it is that Miko didn't become head~"
"Ren nearly brought the family down—how could we raise his spitting image of a daughter to head? The power of the dragon or whatever, frankly, who cares~ Kuro is far better than having Miko as head~ He's docile."
"Isn't he~"

Eunho, who had been tidying up around the drinking party now and then, happened to overhear the entire exchange. As he was about to round the corner and go back to Kuro and the others, he came face to face with Miko behind it.

Miko's expression was not a good one.


***

Deep in the night, on a mountain path of this town.

Flap—

Caw, caw.

On a mountain path thick with fog, where the ominous cries of crows could be heard, a group was headed somewhere, as though migrating.

Someone at the head of the group noticed a small shrine and stopped. He pressed his hands together, offered a small prayer, and murmured in a low voice.

"It's been a long while, Land God. Take us in once more, if you'd be so kind."

Lined up behind him, the group, their faces covered with black cloth, offered small prayers as well. Beneath a night sky that was blacker than black, cause and effect were slowly seeping into this land.


***

< "Those who bear the ties of fate shall gather upon one land, and the play shall begin." >

A beautiful night sky, seen from inside a cathedral.
Surrounded by sacred beasts giving off a holy light—Rubellite Sphinx Cats—'That Being' sat perched on the windowsill, radiating light, casting not even a shadow.

At length, 'That Being' spoke again.

"The moment you open those eyes completely, everything you face will become hell."
"For that is what sin is. Lackness."

From here on, the horizon of battle shifts.
Not to power, but to the spirit.
The Ryuo Clan Arc: this is where it begins—.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 1]
