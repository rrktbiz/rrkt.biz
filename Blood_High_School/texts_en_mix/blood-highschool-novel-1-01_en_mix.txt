▶Ep. 1 Tea Time in the Rose Garden

[Lackness, the dragon of legend.]
[The Dark Dragon of Darkness, said to hold the power to destroy the world.]
[Now, after a thousand years, it a.wa.kens.]


***

Tock—!

The crisp knock of a bamboo fountain rang out across the garden.
Morning came to the stately old Japanese house, heralded by birds chirping over its roof.

Before the Buddhist altar set in one corner of a room.

Ding—.

To the chime of the prayer bell, the boy offered a short prayer before the memorial portraits of his parents, and then—

"Ittekimasu~"
"Itterasshai na no ja~"

He said goodbye to his grandfather at the front door and left the house.

In front of the house, a shrine carved with dragon crests stood in all its grandeur,
and around it, stone dragons guarded the entrance.
The boy never forgot to greet the housekeeper sweeping the yard.

Cherry blossoms were in full bloom along the April road to school.

'I'm Ryuo Kuro, an ordinary high school student!'

Just as he left his neighborhood behind and students in the same uniform began to come into view.

"Yo, Kuro!"

A familiar voice, calling from a distance. It was his childhood friend, Eunho.

"O-ohayou!"

Kuro walked over to Eunho, his whole body creaking with stiffness.
Watching him, Eunho smirked.

"Are~? Don't tell me you're nervous about your big high school debut?"
"A-am not!"

Actually, he was. More nervous than excited. Eunho went on with a sly grin.

"Maa, you're so plain the girls won't pay you any attention at all, so don't you worry~"

Just as Eunho said, Kuro looked 'ordinary.'
A mild face, and plain glasses that didn't stand out.
Uniform buttons done up neatly all the way to the collar.
Anyone could see he was the very picture of a model student.

Hearing that, Kuro felt oddly relieved—but Eunho's tone sounded like he was being made fun of, so he got mad anyway.

"Kora~!"
"Gomen, gomen."

Eunho pressed his palms together in apology, with a face that didn't look sorry in the least.

Dong—! Dong—!

At that moment, the academy bell rang out in the distance.

'Ah, gotta hurry! Today's the entrance ceremony at the one and only St. Saints Academy, after all!'

And so the two boys headed for the academy.


***

The entrance ceremony.
Amid the buzzing crowd of new students, Kuro sat side by side with Eunho.

Slam—!

The auditorium doors flew open. Every student's gaze turned that way.
Student council members rolled out a red carpet with practiced hands, and a single student made his entrance, striding down it.

"Kyaa~!! It's Ferel-sama~ Suteki~!"

Long, flowing hair. Tall, with striking good looks.
A customized uniform, unlike those of the regular students.
On his arms he wore the armband that marked the student council president—not one, but a matching pair, no less.

He was the student council president of St. Saints Academy, [Rius Ferel].

As Ferel passed through the center of the auditorium, Kuro was left dumbstruck by his sheer mys-te-ri-ous-ness.

Just then, Ferel glanced Kuro's way.
And then he smiled. Ufu.

Startled out of his wits, Kuro looked away.
'I'm just imagining it, right? It can't be.'

*
Stepping up onto the stage, Ferel spoke.

"Attention, everyone! Silence! We will now hear a few words from the principal."

The next moment, popping up—boing—onto the lectern at center stage, came an adorable boy.

"Minna~ Youkoso to St. Saints Academy~ Let's all have a super fun school life, ne~☆"

A boy who looked like an elementary schooler.
Dressed in a cutely customized St. Saints uniform and hugging a stuffed rabbit in one arm, he smiled with the face of an angel.
He welcomed the new students in an adorable voice.

It was the principal, [Shota].
At the shocking sight of their principal, the auditorium fell silent.

"Ka.. kawaii~!!!"

A beat later, the crowd erupted in squeals—kyaa!

"Eh—!? That's the principal?"

Kuro was bewildered.

Just then, an unfamiliar boy sitting next to Kuro spoke to him.

"You didn't know? Shota's the son of the previous principal. Got the job through pure nepotism."

Kuro faltered at being spoken to so suddenly.

"Etto.."
"Who're you?"

When Eunho asked in Kuro's place, the boy beamed and introduced himself.

"Ah, gomen. Boku wa Herbaeken. Yoroshiku ne."


***

"Bokutachi—to think we'd end up in the same class~ What a coincidence!"
"Sure is."

The hallway to the classrooms, after the entrance ceremony.
Herbaeken spoke up first, breaking the awkward air.

"Hey, do you guys know about St. Saints Academy?"
"Eh, no? Is there something to know?"

Eunho asked back, in a tone that said, Aren't all schools pretty much the same?

"This academy was built way back in '08—it's got a long, deep history, you know. Monsters you can't find anywhere in the modern world, all sorts of legends—it's an amazing academy da yo!"

As Herbaeken said, this academy was a little unusual.

A stately exterior in the Gothic style.
And inside, an oddball of a school where Western and Japanese styles were all jumbled together.

Portraits of the successive principals hung along the walls of the long hallway that stretched from the first-floor entrance hall, and here and there stood statues and murals tied to the legends.

Leaving the two receding voices behind him, Kuro stopped, as if spellbound, in front of a dragon mural painted on the hallway wall.

In the mural, dragons of every shape were painted around a giant magic circle. The dragons encircled a kingdom shining gold, like one colossal rampart.

Kuro gazed at the majestic mural for a long while.

"Sugoi yo ne?"
"Eh...."

Herbaeken, who had come up beside him at some point, began to explain.

"This mural is called the [Legend of the Dragon]. The legend of the dragon clan's kingdom, steeped in history."

Then Herbaeken pointed a finger at one part of the mural.

"And this dragon is the most famous king of all, the Black Dragon Lackness. They say he's the strongest of all the dragons! ...Though they also say he used that power to bring the history of the dragon clan to an end—"

Herbaeken's tone was meaningful, and bitter.

Beside him, Eunho's eyes sharpened on Herbaeken for an instant.

But the mood flipped in no time.

"Yappari, the Black Dragon is the most kakkoii, right? +_+ It sets a boy's heart on fire!"

Herbaeken shouted, eyes sparkling with excitement.

"—What are you, a middle schooler?!"


Eunho's eyes relaxed as he fired off a tsukkomi.

"I mean— oretachi just picked whatever school was close to home, that's all~! A legend's just a legend. No way any of it's real~"

Eunho strolled off on his way as if it were nothing.

"Eh~!? But they're real, I'm telling you~!!"

Watching the two of them bicker their way ahead, Kuro gave an awkward laugh. "Haha..."

Suddenly Herbaeken's eyes lit up as if he'd found his proof, and he shouted.

"Hora, look at that!"
"?"

Eunho and Kuro turned toward where Herbaeken was looking.

"That's one of the Seven Great Legends of St. Saints Academy! The rose garden da yo!"

A large, beautiful garden, visible beyond the hallway.

"Isn't it just an ordinary garden?"

At Kuro's question, Herbaeken went on as though he'd been waiting for it.

"This rose garden here at the academy, you see—hundreds of years ago, it was the vampires' white rose garden, they say. The vampires had a hobby of dyeing the roses with the blood they'd sucked, and that's how it became the red rose garden it is today, they say!"

His explanation finished, Herbaeken bounced around wildly.

"Let's go explore it later~!"

But Kuro, who had been staring hard at the garden, spoke up cautiously.

"Um, Herbaeken, it says 'Student Council Only' over there.."
"Eh?"

Herbaeken was flustered.

The sign in front of the garden read [Student Council Garden. No Entry for General Students].

Eunho chimed in as if this were great news.

"Aah~ zannen~. That place is student council only, so we can't go in~ Come on, let's get to class."
"Sonna~! T_T"

Herbaeken wailed. Kuro comforted him.


*
Meanwhile.

The student council members, looking down on the garden, were grotesquely entwined with one another. At their center, Ferel whispered in rapture.

"Aah, I can smell it, I can smell it. The sweet fragrance of blood."

When Ferel finished, the members surrounding him took up the rest.

"Saa, saa."
"Once again, these roses."
"With sweet blood."
"Let us dye them."


***

The first-year hallway.

The new students whispered among themselves, thrilled and puzzled by the grand procession of the great student council president.

"Eh, what's Ferel-sama doing here?"
"You don't think there's a new student who caught his eye?"
"Kyaa—"

And at last, Ferel found his target.

"Ryuo Kuro-kun?"
"Eh?"

The moment he called Kuro's name.
The hallway filled with the scent of roses, and a distinctly BL atmosphere.


***

When he came to his senses, Kuro was seated at a tea table inside an ornate gazebo, in the very center of the student-council-only rose garden.
Outside the gazebo, student council members surrounded them, each showing off their own beauty.

"...Why did you invite me to the student council's tea time?"

At Kuro's question, Ferel, seated across the tea table, propped his elbows on the table, clasped his hands together, and gave Kuro a long, smoldering look.

"That's.. because you seduced me, Kuro-kun."
"EH!?!?!?"
"Fufu, playing innocent, are we~"

At that moment, Ferel pulled one of Kuro's arms toward himself.

"I sensed it at the entrance ceremony. You are one worthy of this tea time."

Gazing at Kuro's veins, Ferel grew excited.

"A blood donation, if you please.. onegai shimasu? Haah, haah."
"What are you..!"

Ferel's breathing rapidly grew ragged.
Kuro wanted to run, but with his arm caught, he couldn't.

"This sweet scent that's been piercing my nose all this while! Aah, this is, without a doubt, a high-class dezaato!"

Ferel had long since lost the composure befitting a student council president.

"Mou, I can't hold back any longer!!!!!"

Ferel pulled Kuro to his feet, supported him by the waist, and dipped him halfway back.

The nape of Kuro's neck, exposed and defenseless.
The very moment Ferel the vampire bared his fangs to bite—

*
Thump—!

Deep in Kuro's subconscious. A colossal heartbeat, resounding there.

Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump—!

The blood coursing through his veins surged.
The blood of the dragon, manifesting within Kuro's DNA for the first time in a thousand years.

Something awoke inside Kuro, and his pupils narrowed to sharp slits, like a beast's.

It was the legend of a faraway dragon kingdom.
The king of destruction, said to be the strongest of all dragons, who with that power brought the history of the dragon clan to an end.

It was the resurrection of the Black Dragon, Lackness.

"....Get lost."

Fwoosh—!

Splat!

Blown away by Lackness's flames, Ferel tumbled gracelessly across the lawn.

"Nani?"


Ferel raised his head and looked at Lackness.

The gentle eyes had turned sharp, and the glasses were gone.
His uniform unbuttoned and disheveled.
A presence utterly unlike Kuro's.

Blood-red flames rose around Lackness.

Beyond the shimmering heat haze, wreathed in fire and ash, he gave off a chilling, aloof air of intimidation.

Paradoxically, the sight was cruelly beautiful.

'Kirei~...'

He was entranced by the sight, but only for a moment,

'Wait, these blood-red flames.. it can't be.. the dragon of legend, Lackness!?!?!?!?'

Ferel was aghast, as though he had touched something that must never be touched.

"For a mere vampire to come after me, of all beings—you must not value your life."

[Lackness / ~The World's Strongest Dark Dragon of Darkness~]

"This rose garden, your tasteless hobby. I'll burn every last bit of it."

At a wave of Lackness's hand, flames engulfed the rose vines.

Fwoosh—!

'My masterpiece, hundreds of years in the making...!!!'

Ferel screamed in anguish as he watched the roses burn.
Looking down at him, Lackness gave a final warning.

"Kiero. Never show yourself before me again."
"Kyaaah! Please excuse us!!!!!"

Ferel and the student council members fled, screaming.

As the flames died down and his own personality returned, Kuro passed out on the spot.

Slump—
Fwump...

Before he hit the ground, someone caught him.

It was his childhood friend, Eunho.

Eunho deftly scooped Kuro up into a princess carry.
Kuro's sleeping face was pure innocence.

"...Kuro."

Eunho's expression, full of resolve.

And, looking down on this from the rooftop, Herbaeken's ice-cold expression.

"...Lackness."

Both voices were dead serious, but they pointed in completely opposite directions.

"I'll protect you" / "I'll kill you"

[End of Ep. 1]
