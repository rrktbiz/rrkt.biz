▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 4

The evening of the day he had met Kuro; a Japanese-style house shrouded in deep darkness. Inside the building of the Kagehira family, there hung a heavy, formal silence in which not even the sound of breathing was permitted.

The room of its head, Aoi.

Ding-a-ling.

A text from Kuro arrived on Aoi's phone.
Reading the text, Aoi smiled warmly.
It was the face of that kind, handsome young man from earlier in the day.
But the smile did not last; in the end, he had to put it away.

Snap.

The moment he flipped his phone shut, his face turned cold.

Step, step.

Aoi walked down an unlit hallway toward a certain room.

Inside that room, curse dolls and implements of sorcery, and sacrifices awaiting death, stood ready.

Aoi looked over the whole scene and moved on.


***

Meanwhile, the conversation between Eunho and Miko went on.

"Do you seriously think Kuro is fit to be head?"
"...."

Eunho fell silent at Miko's pointed question.
Miko went on.

"Kuro never properly inherited Yoshiyuki-sama's will."
"...Kuro was too young back then. And you were unusually sharp for a child."
"Exactly. Which is why, in the end, only you and I inherited Yoshiyuki-sama's convictions in full."

Eunho added.

"But the way Kuro acts has always been just as Yoshiyuki taught."

The difficult humanities lessons Yoshiyuki had given them in childhood.
Kuro may not have been able to keep up, but the goodness and pure heart he had shown all his life were themselves proof that Yoshiyuki's teachings had not been in vain.

Hearing that, Miko paused for a moment before continuing.

"...Yoshiyuki-sama taught us all the principles of this world. About the balance of power, turning with all its parts intricately meshed."

Miko's eyes flashed with resolve.

"But what's needed to protect the Ryuo family as it is now is the 'strength' that I have."
"...."
"You know it too. The way of doing things that Yoshiyuki-sama set right—the people of the Ryuo family aren't properly following it! Jiji is lost in his worship of the 'Dragon God,' the relatives are busy lining their own pockets, and the rotten spirit of the Ryuo family has to be set straight. Someone has to do it!"
"...."
"If Kuro becomes head, far from changing how things are done, he'll just end up being pushed around by the adults. Because Kuro is 'too much of an ii ko.'"

Miko was certain.

"Even now he's a fool who can't talk back to a single word Jiji says, isn't he?"

Eunho, who had been holding back, exploded.
Unable to hide the emotion surging up in him.

"Don't treat Kuro like a fool! Kuro knows what's right and what's wrong, too! He's just reading the situation and holding it in!"

Miko's eyes wavered at Eunho's force.
But she gritted her teeth and struck back.

"That part of him—honto kirai da wa! He'll be just the same even as head!"
"That's not true! He can learn the head's work as he goes. If you and I together pass Yoshiyuki's way on to Kuro and lend him our strength-"

Bang!!!!

"Why should I!?"

Miko stood and slammed the table.

"The one who cares more than anyone about the Ryuo family that Yoshiyuki-sama built up is me. The one who understands the weight of the headship is me, too.
So why should I have to stand behind Kuro?"

Miko's gaze fell to the floor.
Her voice grew thick with tears.

"...Do you think I'm not qualified to be head, too?.....Because I'm Ren's daughter?"
"! That's not what this is about!"

Eunho hurriedly denied it at Miko's unexpected show of weakness, but Miko shot back coldly.

"....If you truly thought Kuro was fit to be head, you should have told him about the Kagehira family first—not me."
"....."

Hit where it hurt, Eunho's expression stiffened.
It was a fact he could not refute.

Even Eunho, without realizing it, had been sorting Kuro as someone to be protected,
and Miko as someone to share responsibility with.

"......"

A silence followed.
Amid the bustle of people, Miko sat back down and reined in her emotions, and Eunho spoke heavily.

"Yes, as you say, Kuro isn't strong. If he learns of all the Ryuo family's sins, he will surely suffer. So much so that I have to ask you to support him."

Eunho's eyes turned firm.

"But this is a matter that comes before any debate over who is fit to be head. I understand how you feel, but the fact that Kuro has become head can't be changed, and whichever of you two is head, we have to get through the Ryuo family's affairs together."
"....."

Eunho had a point, too.
Jiji had already appointed Kuro as head.
Miko backed down.
Seeing that, Eunho spoke.

"As I said earlier, I'm going to tell him about the Kagehira family."

Miko flinched.

"No."

Miko cut him off in one stroke.

"Whatever you do, don't tell Kuro about the Kagehira family."
"Miko! Why—"
"You wouldn't understand. The way that boy looks at me."

Eunho, who had been staring blankly, finally grasped the situation.

"I don't want to be pitied any more than I already am."
"~~~...."

Eunho dragged his hands down his face in exasperation.
'So in the end it was a matter of pride.......' he thought, but he bit his lip, knowing it was only natural.

Clatter.

Miko picked up her bag and stood.

"You two just assist. I'm more than enough to lead the Ryuo family on my own. Carrying on Yoshiyuki-sama's will, the seat of the head, even those sins—I'll shoulder all of it."
"...Miko, taking everything on by yourself like that is wrong."

Eunho let out a sigh and murmured inwardly.

'That... is not Yoshiyuki's will.'

Eunho was at a loss as to how he could convey to Miko these words he had swallowed.


*

At that same hour.
Near the tallest building in the city center, Aoi and the people of the Kagehira family were concealed in the darkness.
When Aoi reached out his hand, the night air froze in an instant.

Kiiing—!

With the sound of sorcery, the Kagehira family's commission began.

*

"........!!"

It was Eunho's keen senses that picked up that dark aura. Sensing a chill presence, Eunho shot a look toward the window.

At Eunho's sudden movement, Miko asked.

"Why so startled?"

Even at Miko's question, Eunho could not tear his eyes away.

Between the passersby, within a black shadow, an oni was rippling along at speed.

Eunho knew that oni.
That oni was.

"...That's the Kagehira family's oni!!!!"



**

The Kagehira house.
The feeble voices of the family members surrounding the sacrifice rang out as if resonating. Like a lullaby to console a soul about to be sent off.

The oni invaded the building in an instant.
To the eyes of people without spiritual power, the oni was invisible.

Thud.

The target inside the building, a man, collapsed as the oni sucked out his soul. At that moment, the sacrifice offered on the altar toppled over as well.

Atop the building, Aoi turned over the words he had said to Kuro earlier that day.

'The self I became to protect something.'

Its task complete, the oni returned to its master's side.
Behind Aoi, the form of a colossal oni wavered.

'It is the self that has accepted despair.'

**

On Eunho's side, things were moving fast.

"Miko! Don't you follow! You'll get caught in the backlash!"

Leaving Miko behind, Eunho chased after the oni's aura.
His heart pounded as if it would burst. Afterimages of Yoshiyuki and of the sacrificed Kagehira flashed through his mind.

*

But when Eunho arrived at the scene,
the oni and the Kagehira were already gone.

There was only—
the corpse of a grown man, only just dead.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 4]
