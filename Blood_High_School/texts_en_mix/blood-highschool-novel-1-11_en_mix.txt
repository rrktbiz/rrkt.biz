▶Ep. 11 Cursed BOY, Crazy BABY

In the chill air of the underground dungeon, Herbaeken was lost in thought.

On the floor, he drew the elements that symbolized the powers of the dragons.
Patterns intertwined like a five-pointed magic circle. And he thought of a new being that fit none of them: 'Lumensia.'

'A dragon with the power of blue flames. That is not a power that circulates among the dragon clan.'

The knowledge of dragons passed down among his people for generations. And everything he himself had witnessed until now. It all jumbled together and threw his mind into disarray. The fact that what he knew was not the whole picture unsettled Herbaeken's resolve.

'What is happening?'

That was when it happened.

Creeeak...

The inner door of the dungeon opened.

"Eh?"

Herbaeken stepped up to the bars and looked toward the door.

"Eunho!?"

There stood Eunho, looking sheepish.
Herbaeken casually opened the cell door and stepped out.

"What the, there's not even a lock..."
"Apparently not."

Eunho fired a tsukkomi at the dungeon's rather shoddy security.
In truth, Herbaeken had merely been playing along with Lumensia's childish game, staying inside the cell of his own accord.

Herbaeken asked again.

"There should have been guards."
"There weren't. Kuro went to settle things with Lumensia, so it looks like they all went to watch."
"!"

At Eunho's explanation, Herbaeken grew restless.

"It's finally the finals? Then we should hurry too—."

But instead of responding to that, Eunho looked him square in the face.

"Before that."

Eunho's voice sank low.

"I need to know just who you really are."

At those words, Herbaeken answered with a smile on his lips alone, his eyes not smiling.

"...Whatever could you be talking about?"


"Don't play dumb. I saw you that day. Taking aim at Lackness."

In fact, on the day of the culture festival, Eunho had witnessed Herbaeken up in the auditorium ceiling, sniping at Lackness.

Herbaeken was silent for a moment, then answered with his usual feigned innocence.

"Haha, then what about you, Eunho—who are you really? That place underground isn't something you can get out of easily. Do you have the power of a dragon too, or something?"
"...."

Eunho didn't answer.
A taut battle of nerves stretched between the two.

Herbaeken let out a short laugh.
And his voice dropped lower and lower.

"So you're saying I should go first?"

Herbaeken shifted his posture at his leisure and said.

"Then listen. To the story of a cursed boy—"


***

It was a very long time ago.
The succession ceremony for the duty handed down through generations of the Herba clan.
Before young Herbaeken's eyes lay a stone tablet of the dragon legend.

"The dragon born in a thousand-year cycle, the Black Dragon Lackness. Herbaeken, you must kill it."

At those words, young Herbaeken asked in a trembling voice.

"Why..? Weren't we the people who served the dragons..?"
"We were. But those ingrates slaughtered us all!!"

Hatred filled the voices of the Herba clan's elders.

"Our blood holds the pain and rage of our ancestors. You, who inherited the blood of the Herba clan, must accept this destiny."

Amid brainwashing that was all but forced on him, Herbaeken looked back in terror. But the elders of the clan only stared down at him menacingly.

Young Herbaeken was afraid.
He feared how he would be treated in this clan if he refused.

"Listen to the voices of your ancestors, carved into your blood, Herbaeken!"

The screams and resentment of his ancestors, forcibly carved into him, became shackles that bound him.

[ "That was my destiny—one like a curse." ]

As an adolescent, Herbaeken looked down at the torn book with the face of an avenger. Then he entered a special training facility and endured hellish training.

[ "I have lived my whole life to kill a dragon, after all." ]

Hours spent enduring electric torture, practicing marksmanship, forging himself solely to kill a dragon. And at last, he locked onto his target.

"Found you, Lackness."

Looking at a photo of Lackness, living in hiding within a host called 'Ryuo Kuro,' he sharpened the blade of his revenge.

[ "But after I enrolled in this academy, what I saw of you two was not what I expected." ]

Two people who were just ordinary high schoolers, and terribly good-hearted.

[ "I was absolutely certain Lackness would be an evil being, but..." ]

The mercy that granted rest to a doll who had lost its master. The warmth that took in an abandoned monster. Two friends who, though they sometimes bristled at him, never once left him all alone. And the sight of the dragon bowing his head in apology even with no memory.

[ "The more time passed, the less I understood." ]

His recollection finished, Herbaeken spoke, thinking of the standoff between Lumensia and Lackness.

"Whether what I'm facing is truly the 'evil' I must destroy. I need to know for certain."

At Herbaeken's steady gaze, Eunho's eyes turned serious as well.

"...All right. I'll tell you everything I know. Including what I really am."

The taut battle of nerves between the two was over.
In its place, a deal toward a new purpose was struck.


***

Meanwhile,
Lumensia was studying the mural of the dragon legend painted in the hallway. Looking over the dragons in the mural one by one, Lumensia was full of discontent.

"Ore-sama isn't here either, huh. Tch."

Step, step.

Just then, Hakase approached, his shoes clicking.

"There is no need to concern yourself. What matters is the present: 'who will stand at the top.'"
"....."

At Hakase's words, Lumensia didn't so much as stir. But Hakase, as though Lumensia's reaction made no difference to him, sank deeper into madness and murmured as if to himself.

"....When I first met you, Lumensia-sama, I was struck with admiration."

Hakase recalled his first meeting with Lumensia, when the boy was around three years old. A mysterious child said to appear in the forest. Attacking hikers and raiding their food, with the vertical-slit pupils of dragon eyes. A beast-like figure ruled by instinct alone, stripped of all reason.

"Your power is the very image of one worthy of being the strongest in the world. I believe that you will change this world."
"....."

Hakase stood beside Lumensia, keeping a proper distance, and faced the mural.

"Ara, a challenger has arrived."

At Hakase's words, Lumensia's eyes sharpened.

Step, step.

The one who appeared was Lackness.

Hakase smoothly withdrew so as not to be in the way, and there, a taut one-on-one standoff between Lumensia and Lackness took shape.


***

The finals, on the academy rooftop.

The stands were already packed with the entire student body, abuzz.
On the tournament bracket set up beside the arena, a [FORFEIT] sticker had been slapped over [Entire Student Body].

Herbaeken and Eunho squeezed in through the crowd belatedly.

"...It hasn't started yet."

Far off in the middle of the wide rooftop, Lumensia and Lackness faced each other.

Whoooosh—

A fierce wind whipped between the two.

Lumensia spoke first, with a nasty smirk.

"So you finally feel like fighting?"

The spectators gulped, waiting for Lackness's answer.

"....How much longer do you intend to disrupt the order of this place? Did you refuse the seal merely to stage this kind of pandemonium?"

At Lackness's unexpected answer, the eyes of Eunho and Herbaeken, watching, went wide.

"Twisting the school rules under the pretext of a tournament just so you can do as you please, trying to reign over the entire student body through fear... How uncivilized"
"..........Hah??"

Lumensia, too, seemed thrown; his eyebrow twitched.
Lackness went on.

"However strong you may be, there can be no such rule as everything going according to your desires. Your behavior is......."

Lackness paused for a moment, as though picking out the right word.

"'A nuisance (meiwaku)'...... is what it's called."

Lumensia and the spectators were all dumbstruck.
Eunho and Herbaeken in particular doubted their ears.

It was not the sort of thing a Black Dragon like ~Lackness, the Dark Dragon of Darkness~, destroyer of an entire kingdom, would say. It was closer to 'a very basic scolding' given to a little brother. (And by the standards of human society, at that.)

A moment later, Lumensia belatedly registered the fact that he had just received 'a scolding—or rather, a nagging,' and his face scrunched up. Lumensia's face turned bright red.

"Iiiiiiiiigh!!!! Shut up!! Don't you try to lecture my great self!!!!!!!!" (Like I don't know that! Like I don't know that!)

Lumensia flailed his whole body, expressing just how 'pissed off' he was.

"The strongest IS the rule!!!!!!"

Lumensia screamed loud enough to shake the rooftop.

In the face of that outburst, Lackness only looked at Lumensia with eyes gone cold and still.

"......Sou ka. Then I will carve it into you until you understand."

With Lackness's words, the mood turned.


*

Behind the two dragons, colossal flames in the shape of dragons rose up.
With murderous glares, the two shot forward at the same instant.
The clash was beyond the realm of humans.

Ka-boom—-!!!!!

With a tremendous impact, the rooftop floor caved in.
Lumensia attacked, and Lackness calmly dodged.
But debris from the rooftop floor flew all the way up into the stands.

"Kyaaah~!!"

Hearing the spectators' screams, Lackness seized Lumensia the instant he lost his balance and hurled him into the air. Then he swiftly gave chase.

A dizzying exchange of blows continued up in the sky.
Lumensia poured out blue flames with brute abandon.
But Lackness evaded every attack with restrained movements.

"Kuh, kuhah!"

Lumensia let out a hollow laugh in his excitement, as if overjoyed to have met the rival he had waited his whole life for.

Crack!

Then he landed atop the railing and shouted.

"Oi, Lackness. Don't you feel cheated? You're this strong, and you got locked away the moment you were born."

Lackness, too, landed lightly atop a structure on the opposite side and listened quietly to what Lumensia had to say.

"That, let me tell you, is a feeling I hate enough to die."

Lumensia thought of the past.
The terror of nearly being sealed away in darkness the moment he was born.

"But, see, these guys recognized my power."

In the laboratory, followed by Hakase and his comrades, he had made the ranks submit one after another, earning comrades and recognition. The final proof stood right before his eyes. At Lackness, the rank-one, strongest Black Dragon, Lumensia shouted.

"Now if I just take you down, there'll be nothing left that can stop me!!!!!"

Whack—!

Without the slightest waver, Lackness clobbered Lumensia.
It was a wordless rebuke: no backstory could justify the 'nuisance' he was being now.

Lumensia felt wronged.
Fury welled up at the fact that his opponent wasn't listening to him at all.

"Grr...!"

His sense of injustice bursting, tears welled a little at the corners of Lumensia's eyes.


***

Up in the sky, dark clouds spread and lightning struck as round two began.

Sssss—-.

Lumensia, huffing and grinding his teeth, tried to transform into his true form, a 'dragon.' But Lackness snatched him and stopped him at once. A fight between giant dragons was sure to lay waste to everything around them.

'Not that.'

He warned him coldly with a single look. With even his transformation blocked, Lumensia's rage boiled over, but Lackness rained down attacks as if to give him no chance to change.


*

A roar went up from the spectators watching the scene.

"Uooh, sugee—!!"
"They're fighting in midair—!!"

In the sky, Lackness and Lumensia traded blows at a speed almost too fast for the eye to follow. But as it went on, the balance of the fight began to tip to one side.

Lackness read Lumensia's monotonous attack patterns and slipped precisely through the openings. He got a firm hold on Lumensia and struck him hard in the stomach and jaw. It was a merciless, utterly one-sided beating.

"Is it just me... or is Lumensia just getting pounded?"

At the doubtful voices of the crowd, Hakase watched Lumensia with a cold expression.

"All right! Go, Kuro, win~!!!"

Amid the cheers, Herbaeken, watching the overwhelming scene, murmured.

"The difference in power is enormous."

But Eunho, watching the fight beside him, saw it differently.

"No. There's not much difference in power."

Herbaeken looked at Eunho questioningly.
Eunho went on, following the fight with his eyes.

"If, as the legend handed down in your clan says, Lumensia is not a power that circulates among the dragon clan, then that's a new power. In other words, a 'newborn power.'"

At that moment, Lumensia, who had been taking a one-sided beating, let out a scream and made his last desperate struggle.

"And if Lackness, conversely, is a power that has been born again and again, then against Lackness, who has been fighting for ages—"

Lackness condensed all his power and drove it down.

"—he can't possibly win that easily."

KABOOOM——!

[End of Ep. 11]
