▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 8

"Would the Dragon God welcome even a murderer—shite kureru?"

Aoi's words, heavy with meaning.

Kuro froze, his eyes wide.

"Aoi-san, nande sonna koto kiku no...?"

Aoi did not answer.

Half hidden by his hair, Aoi's expression was steeped in sorrow.

An expression Kuro had never seen on him.
Kuro sensed it by instinct.
That this man might be a murderer,
or that there might be a story he could not bring himself to tell.

"....."

In that tension, Kuro thought it over carefully, and at last he spoke.

"..Ano ne. According to the dragon legend, a thousand years ago the Dragon God was the king of a kingdom. But he didn't bestow the abundance that the dragon clan had bestowed for generations, and he massacred the people of the kingdom."

Aoi's eyes widened.

"For some reason the Dragon God has no memory at all of that time, so I don't know why he did such a thing, but...."

Kuro went on, shrinking into himself.
It was nothing he had done himself, and yet his voice trembled as though the fault were his own.

"He couldn't simply condemn someone who'd committed the same sin..."

That was the best answer Kuro could give.

Hearing it, Aoi gazed at the empty air straight ahead.

And answered plainly.

"......Sokka."


*

The moment silence fell.

Ding-a-ling♪

Aoi's phone rang.

"Ah, a call."

Answering the phone, Aoi replied briefly.

"Mm, ....sugu iku wa."

Click.

Aoi rose from his seat.

"Kuro-chan. Somethin' urgent's come up, so I'll be headin' off first."
"Y-yeah..."

Watching Aoi's back recede, Kuro called out.

"Aoi-san!"

Instead of answering, Aoi glanced back over his shoulder.

"Mata ne!"

Aoi answered with a gentle smile.

"...Mm, mata na."


***

Meanwhile. The central hall of the Kagehira house.
The standoff between the girl and Eunho and Miko had reached its peak.

The girl in black mourning dress drew a black handbell from her sleeve.

Clang—.

When the girl shook the bell, as if in answer to the sound, dark shadows in every corner around them squirmed, gathered in one place, and clumped together. The masses of oni, multiplying in an instant, stretched into grotesque shapes and surrounded Eunho and Miko layer upon layer.

Without a word, the girl unleashed a relentless assault. Wavering black forms shot out like tentacles, trying to swallow the two whole at once.

Miko hastily drew several talismans and cast her shrine maiden's sorcery, while Eunho fended off the onslaught with the nimble movements and barriers characteristic of a Fox Spirit. The girl watched Miko's sorcery closely, then, sensing Eunho's alien divine power, narrowed her eyes beneath the black cloth.

Tap-...

Shaking off the oni, Eunho and Miko barely managed to land in the middle of the hall.

'It seems she's been ordered to kill intruders on the spot.'

Just as Eunho exchanged a look with Miko and coolly assessed the situation, the girl rang the bell once more, and from the central door behind her, a colossal oni came crawling out with a grotesque noise.

"Hun...... gr...... yyy......."

It was an oni that was nothing but a huge gaping mouth in formless black smoke. Miko was aghast.

"Nani are!?"
"Hitokui oni (a man-eating oni). They mean to dispose of our corpses with it."

Eunho met the girl's eyes and shouted.

"Matte! We have no intention of fighting the Kagehira family!! We are—."

Before he could finish, at a gesture of the girl's hand, the black forms flew at the two. Miko gritted her teeth and defended.

"These guys don't look like they're going to listen!?"

Eunho made his decision at last.
This was no longer the time to hide it.

Fwaaaaaah—-.

"......!!"

In that instant, the aura of the Fox Spirit burst from Eunho's body. Its light wore down and shaved away the darkness of the oni. Before that sacred aura, even the girl faltered and stepped back.

"Moushiwake nai, but watashi is not a being who will die from this much. We are people of the Ryuo family. We came only to talk! Let us meet the head of the Kagehira family!"

Despite Eunho's cry, just as the girl gripped her bell again to attack, a chilling voice came from the front entrance.

"Stop."

"!!!!"

At that voice, everyone in the hall stopped as if frozen.

Where Eunho and Miko turned their heads stood Aoi—who until moments ago had been with Kuro—as head of the Kagehira family.


***

The reception room of the Kagehira house.
A Japanese-style room, closed in on all sides. Even the light coming through the window was faint; dark and suffocating, the place was like a sealed chamber for trading secrets.

The three sat facing one another across a low tea table.
The intimidating presence of a family head that Aoi gave off bore down on the air.

Eunho and Miko kept their heads lowered, avoiding his gaze.
Aoi rebuked the two sharply.

"The Ryuo lot got no manners, I see. Tailin' an evil spirit, of all things? Your lives worth that little to ya?"

Eunho answered in a low voice.

"......Moushiwake nai."

It was a seasoned manner of speech, unlike a seventeen-year-old's.
The battle just now, and the Fox Spirit that Kuro had told him about.
From that information, Aoi could guess what Eunho was.

Aoi opened a folding fan and covered his mouth.

"Sore de. What ya came to talk about—nan ya."

Eunho went on courteously.

"We know of what the Ryuo family did in the past. How deep a sin the Ryuo family committed against you."

Eunho's and Aoi's gazes locked coldly.

"....And I know that before he died, Yoshiyuki said he would take responsibility for the Kagehira family. But when I went after you following Yoshiyuki's funeral, the Kagehira family had already left that place, and I could find no trace...."

Aoi listened quietly, unmoved.

"I have thought all this time. That someday the Ryuo family must pay for that sin. We are the ones who will lead the Ryuo family from here on. Will you not give us the chance to take responsibility for it?"

When Eunho finished, Aoi closed his eyes as if to collect his thoughts. Then, very slowly, he spoke.

**

Aoi drew out a memory of the past.

< "....The day Ryuo Yoshiyuki came to the Kagehira family. He admitted every sin an' said he'd take responsibility for the Kagehira family—an' at those words, the head of the Kagehira family flew into a rage." >

The scene of that day, when Yoshiyuki came.

[ "Responsibility!? Our family was no victim! We were partners in the trade!" ]

Yoshiyuki, the former head of the Ryuo family, who had come to the Kagehira house alone.
He lay prostrate before the furious head of the Kagehira family and did not raise his head. Aoi, then barely thirteen, simply watched the scene with vacant eyes.

Yoshiyuki, who had seemed like he would be persistent, for some reason never came again after that day. The former head clicked his tongue as if he had expected as much, and gave young Aoi an order.

[ "Hmph. So that nuisance isn't coming anymore. Aoi. We're moving our base. Get ready." ]
[ "Yes." ]

Listening, Miko and Eunho cautiously exchanged looks. They sensed that it was not the Kagehira family who had killed Yoshiyuki.

Aoi's recollection continued.

Young Aoi played at being obedient before the head, while behind his back he gazed, with eyes full of longing, at the spot where Ryuo Yoshiyuki had been.

< "The head couldn't admit he'd been used, so he tried to carry on Ren's way." >
< "To win back the power he'd had a taste of, he went lookin' for a new employer, an' the Kagehira family had to leave that place." >
< "But me, young as I was, I was desperate to get outta here. A few days later, I went to the Ryuo family." >

Just before the Kagehira family relocated, young Aoi ran to the Ryuo house in secret. Before the gate of the enormous house. Spotting Jiji going inside just then, Aoi clung to him and cried out desperately.

[ "I'm a child of the Kagehira family! The head of this house said he'd take responsibility for us! Please, help us......!" ]

Seeing Aoi clinging to him, Jiji recoiled in horror and shook him off.

[ "Th-the head of the Ryuo family is dead! It has nothing to do with us anymore!" ]

Aoi froze on the spot.

[ "The head...... is dead......?" ]

Yoshiyuki, who had said he would take responsibility for the Kagehira family, was no longer of this world. Only then did young Aoi understand. That there was no salvation for him.

Listening, Eunho bit his lip.

"......Ano Jiji......!"

Beside him, Miko bit her lip too, with a wretched expression.

Aoi swiftly scanned both their faces and gathered that neither had known of this. He put the matter to rest.

"....Well, never mind. It's all in the past."

Aoi moved on to the next part.

"Shadow families get word fast, y'see. I know well enough that after Ren died, the Ryuo family cut ties with the underworld an' has been livin' quiet as a mouse. There may be some o' Ren's accomplices left in the Ryuo family, but lookin' at how things stand now, they were likely just your common opportunists."

Eunho and Miko looked convinced.

*

Aoi folded the fan that had been covering his mouth.

"I met the head of the Ryuo family, Ryuo Kuro."
"!"

Eunho's breath stopped.

"Not that the young head knew a thing."

Leaving the startled Eunho aside, Aoi went on calmly.

"If nothin' of Ryuo Ren's spirit remains in the seat of the head of the Ryuo family—the one who follows after Ryuo Yoshiyuki—then that's enough."

Aoi slowly closed his eyes.

"That is my answer."

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 8]
