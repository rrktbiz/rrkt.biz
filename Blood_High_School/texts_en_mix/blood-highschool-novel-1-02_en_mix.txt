▶Ep. 2 The Marionette in the Art Room

Tock—!

The crisp knock of a bamboo fountain.
A stately old Japanese house—this is the 'Ryuo family,' the House of the Dragon King, heirs to the blood of dragons.

"Sou ka.. Tsui ni, the dragon of legend, Lackness, has awakened no ka..."

Kuro's grandfather intoned gravely, hands clasped behind his back as he gazed out at the garden.

Behind him, in the room, sat Eunho, his usual slyness nowhere to be found, wearing a serious expression.

"....."

A heavy silence hung in the air, and then—

"....—Hooray~!!! The Dragon God has come to our family~!! Time to cash in on our ancestors' blessings!"

Wherever all that gravity had gone, Jiji was now dancing a merry jig.

Eunho was dumbfounded by the sight.

But he had to say what needed saying.

"...Hey, Jiji. Listen, though—Kuro doesn't remember a thing, so.. keep it a secret for now."
"Eh-wha?"


***

"Fwaaah~"

In the classroom, Kuro was simply yawning in peace.

"Mmm.. no matter how hard I think, I've got no memory of yesterday.. Why was I passed out at lunchtime?"

When Kuro asked drowsily, Eunho answered without missing a beat.

"Didn't you just fall asleep 'cause you were stuffed from lunch?"
"Eh—... no way.."

Da-da-da-da—!

Ominous footsteps approached. It was Herbaeken.

"Nee, minna! Kiita? The u.wa.sa. going around the whole school ☆"

Herbaeken's eyes sparkled.

"A kid from the class next door forgot something and snuck into the academy at night when nobody was around, and on the way past the art room there was this strange sound, they say. And when they peeked in, right there...... some little kid was sitting in front of a doll, talking to it, they say. 'Are you not cold? Are you not hungry?'—like that!"

Herbaeken made his pitch, face lit up with excitement.

"Wanna go check it out later? That little ghost-san might just know the secret history of this academy!"

Kuro trembled, scared stiff.
Eunho wrapped a protective arm around Kuro and said firmly.

"What are you saying with a smile on your face!? We're not going!"
"Eh~? Issho ni ikou yo~"
"We're not interested in that stuff, so go by yourself!"

At Eunho's cold response, Herbaeken went "muu—" and stuck out his lip in a pout.

"Cheh~! Wakatta yo. Later, even if it's all by myself, iku mon!"

The instant he turned his back, Herbaeken's eyes went cold.


***

Lunchtime.

"Herbaeken~ Huh?"

Eunho looked for Herbaeken, but he wasn't there.
Instead, a sticky note was stuck to Herbaeken's desk.

[Heading out, enjoy your lunch!]

Kuro came up behind him, and their eyes met.


***

"Honestly, what a weird guy."
"Da ne.."

Putting Herbaeken, who had vanished off somewhere, out of their minds, Kuro and Eunho were heading for the cafeteria.

For some reason, a huge crowd had gathered by the school store next to it.
Eunho stopped and turned to look.

"What's all the commotion?"

At the chaotic entrance to the school store, students were locked in a scramble.

"Doke~!! This bread is Shota-sama no mono da~!!!"
"Kyaaah!"

Shota's fan club shoved students aside and seized the bread.
They ran straight to Shota, who sat atop a palanquin shouldered by the fan club, and offered it up.

"Shota-sama, douzo! A yakisoba-pan just for Shota-sama desu yo!"
"Ore was first, kono yarou~!!"

Shota accepted the bread and nibbled away, nom nom.

"Hoeeh..."

The fan club's reaction to the sight was explosive.

"Kyaa, Shota-sama~ Even the way you eat is kawaii desu"
"Thirty-two years old, and the uniform still suits you~"

The two recoiled at the bizarre spectacle.

"Ugeh... nanda are."
"Principal-sensei's fan club.. I think?"
"Maji ka yo.."

Before long, the two set off for the cafeteria again.

"The school store's muri da na, from now on."
"Yeah."

Kuro and Eunho, walking off into the distance.
Shota's eyes followed the two of them.

Then he turned back and spoke to his fan club.

"Minna~ Shota ga daisuki desu ne~?"
"Haaai~!"

Shota smiled a chilling smile.

"There's something Shota really, really wants. You're all going to grant Shota's wish, right☆"


***
Ding-dong-dang-dong, ding-dong-dang-dong— ♬
An announcement rang out over the PA.

[ "Minna~ Today is a wakuwaku event~" ]

Startled by the sudden announcement, the students stopped what they were doing.

[ "You are all going to offer up the dragon to me~ Losers will all be expelled☆" ]
"!"

At the word 'dragon,' Eunho went on high alert.
A distorted voice, impossible to identify.
At that moment, a scream came through the speakers.

[ "Uwaaah~~!!" ]

Kuro's eyes went wide.

"......! That's Herbaeken's voice?"

"That guy! Where the heck is he at a time like this!"

As Eunho panicked, Kuro racked his brain in a hurry.

The sticky note Herbaeken had left before lunch, and the legend of the art room, came to mind.

"Ah! He said earlier he'd go to the art room even if he had to go alone! I'm not sure, but...... let's try there first!"

They nodded and were about to head for the art room, when—

Slam!

Rumble—

"Find the dragon~~!!"
"The dragon, for our master~~!!"

From the door on the opposite side, students lunged at the two like zombies.
Eunho swiftly held them off with a mop that had been sitting in the corner.

"Kh! They're all being controlled! Kuro, leave this to me and find the real puppet master!"
"Demo......!"

Kuro couldn't bring himself to take a step.
He couldn't leave Eunho behind.

To Kuro, Eunho shouted in a voice full of conviction.

"I'll be fine! You can do it, Kuro! Ore wo shinjiro—believe in me!"
"......!!"

Kuro clenched his fist tight.

Dash—!

Then, trusting Eunho's words, he took off running down the hallway.
Once he'd made sure Kuro was far away, Eunho murmured.

"—Sate to......"

Swish—

Eunho's hair turned silver.

"Guess it's about time I showed what I can really do."


***

Meanwhile, as Kuro sprinted down the hallway, his head was filled with confusion.

'What's going on!?'
'Why has everyone turned into zombies?'
'And what's this about a dragon......'
'Wakannai yo~~!!!'

*

Dodging students left and right, he finally arrived in front of the art room.

"Herbaeken's in here.."

Kuro opened the door with a trembling hand.

Creeeak—.

"......Herbaeken?"

Inside the pitch-dark art room.

Kuro stepped in cautiously.
There, instead of canvases, unidentifiable odds and ends and grotesque dolls lay strewn in every direction.

"You did well to make it all the way here☆"
"You're...... Principal-sensei!?"

At the center of it all stood the principal, Shota.

"Master said so. He said he wants a dragon....... Everything Master wanted, Shota has collected, zenbu, zenbu. Now, be good and become Master's trophy."

Clack.

Wooden puppets as big as Kuro himself blocked his way.

"What are you talking about......? If this is all your doing, Sensei, please stop....!"

Kuro cried out timidly, but Shota only laughed a grotesque laugh.

"Fufufu, not listening to Sensei—what a bad student. Bad students...... end up like this."

As Shota stepped aside, someone emerged from the darkness—swish. Kuro's eyes went wide in shock.

"Herbaeken!?"

It was Herbaeken, being controlled just like the other students.
Shota gave the order at once.

"Now, capture the dragon!"
"Hai."

With unfocused eyes, Herbaeken raised a sword and lunged at Kuro. Kuro grabbed a sword that had been stuck in a stand beside him and blocked Herbaeken's attack.

Clang—!

"Snap out of it, Herbaeken! Wake up!"
"...."

Despite Kuro's desperate cry, Herbaeken's eyes were soulless.

'......Dame da! My voice zenzen isn't reaching him!'

And so the clash between the two went on.
The sharp ring of blades continued.
Watching the scene, Shota sneered.

"Fufu, muda muda. This student is now Shota's perfect doll, you know☆"
"Shota will gather what Master wants, and see his smile once more."

Shota remembered.

The figure of the master who had made him—the previous principal.

[ "Shota. Your name is Shota." ]

The photograph of his son that sat on the previous principal's desk.
Longing for his dead son, the previous principal had created Shota.

'Whenever Shota brings what Master wants, Master always smiles.'
'If Shota brings him the dragon, this time for sure, Master will smile again.'


*

Clang—!!

Just then, Kuro's sword went flying and clattered across the floor.

Thud—!

Losing his balance, Kuro was pinned beneath Herbaeken.

When he gathered his wits and opened his eyes, Herbaeken's blade was right before them.

Kuro reflexively squeezed his eyes shut.

That was when it happened.

"Ku...... ro......."
"Eh?"
"Ko...ro...shi...te......."
"......!!"

Kuro's eyes wavered violently.
His friend's agonized voice pierced his heart.

Ba-dump—.

In that instant, the blood coursing through Kuro's veins began to respond.


Fwoosh—!!!


With Lackness's appearance, blood-red flames rose up.
At the same time, Herbaeken was sent flying far across the room.

"Fueeh!?"

Shota shrieked.

"Kisama....... Toying with the hearts of ningen as you please—yurusanai. Release your control this instant."

Overwhelmed by Lackness's power, Shota began to show his true colors.

Clatter.
Clatter.
Clatter.

"Hiiiii, nooooo…...."

Then his face contorted and he screamed.

"Students.. students are supposed to be good and listen to Sensei—!!!!!!"

He yanked the head off the stuffed rabbit he had been holding.
A small blade was attached at the neck.
Gripping it, Shota charged at Lackness.

Whoosh—!

Lackness's massive flames repelled Shota and sent him flying toward the wall.

"Kyeeek!!!"

Thud!

Shota, slammed into the wall with a scream.
From the impact, a wooden puppet that had been hidden behind the curtain toppled over with a clatter.

"Fueh."

Shota cradled the wooden puppet preciously in his arms.

"Master, are you all right? Master, are you all right?"

It was a wooden puppet the size of a grown man.
A wig had been placed on it, as if it were a person.

"Master, are you all right? Master, are you all right?"

Shota only repeated the same words.

Not knowing of his master's death, he had kept moving for his master's sake, all this time, all this time.

Lackness looked down on the scene with cold eyes.


"......So it was a doll gone mad."


Lackness walked up to Shota.

And spoke, calm and even.



"Doll who has lost its master. It is time you slept."



At the touch of Lackness's hand,

Shota slowly closed his eyes.



***

The next morning

"Minna, kiita? Yesterday, it wasn't just us—every student's memory got wiped, they say!"

Kuro was surprised to hear it.

"So it wasn't just us.."

Beside Kuro, Eunho thought back with a complicated expression.

Eunho, who had hauled the two of them, huffing and puffing, from where they'd collapsed in the art room.
He had slung Kuro over his back and, with one hand, dragged along the unusually heavy Herbaeken. All the while grumbling, 'Why is Herbaeken so heavy!'

'......I remember it all, though.. -_-;;'

As Eunho's bitter recollection ended, Herbaeken neatly summed up the whole enormous affair.

"Honto, what a mysteric high school da yo ne!"
"Y..yeah;;"

Kuro let out a deflated sound at Herbaeken's off-the-wall remark.

Herbaeken then reported the results of his 'legend expedition' to the two of them.

"And I went back to the art room today, but there was no little ghost-san and no doll!"

At that moment
In the clear sky above the academy, Master and Shota appeared at 50% opacity...☆

(Shota, be sure to live happily with your master up in heaven~!)

[End of Ep. 2]
