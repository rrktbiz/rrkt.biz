▶Ep. 10 Ore-sama Conquers the Academy!!

On the auditorium stage of St. Saints Academy.

The two dragons glared at each other, spewing flames.
Amid the murmuring of the audience, Lackness spoke first.

"......Ware no twin of the dragon clan, who refused to have his power sealed."
"If you know ore-sama, that makes this quick."

Lumensia twisted the corner of his mouth, let off even more flames for no good reason, and struck a villainous 'signature pose.'

"Ore-sama wa Luu-mensia. The one who will become the strongest in the world."

Lackness asked back of the arrogantly declaring Lumensia.

"The strongest in the world?"

Lumensia swaggered up to Lackness.

"This world, see—it belongs to whoever's strongest, right?"
"....."

Lumensia thrust his face right up to Lackness's and glared.

"Dakara, you're in ore-sama's way. Lackness."

The eyes they turned on each other were murderous.


*

At that moment

Slam!

The auditorium doors were flung open, and everyone's eyes turned toward them.
The student council president of St. Saints Academy. It was Ferel.

"Attention, everyone! Shizuka ni! What on earth is going on in our sacred St. Saints Academy!? We of the student council will not tolerate any further disturbance—yurushimasen yo!"

But the moment he spotted who was on stage, his attitude changed abruptly.

"Hm..? Gahk!!"

There stood Lackness, who had told him never to appear before his eyes again.

"Kyaaah~!"

Ferel fled.

"Ferel-sama~!"
"Ferel-sama ran away~!"
"What's going to happen to St. Saints Academy without the student council~!"
"Yabee~~!"

With the student council president gone, the students milled about in confusion.
Eunho pushed his way through them toward the front of the stage.
His expression was as tense and worried as could be.

"Eh~!? So Kuro was a futago.."

Just then, Herbaeken popped up behind Eunho.

"....Ah, yeah. Though this is the first time they're meeting face to face."

Eunho didn't ask where Herbaeken, who had suddenly appeared, had been, and carried on the conversation naturally.


*

As the students' murmuring went on, Lumensia grew irritated.

"Ah~ urusee na."

Just then, a firm voice rang through the auditorium.

"Everyone. Shizuka ni."

Step, step.

The click of shoe heels echoed across the stage. The murmuring stopped, and the students' eyes focused on him. He took his place at center stage, side by side with Lumensia. Then, with the bearing of a well-mannered butler, he bowed courteously.

"Watakushi wa the new principal who will lead this St. Saints Academy from now on. My name is Shitsujide Hakase."

Shota objected to his introduction.

"Fueh~! The principal is Shota na noni~!!"

Fwoosh-!

"Hoeeeh~~!"

Lumensia's flames blew Shota away.

"Shota-sama~!"

The students screamed as they watched Shota fly off.

Right after, a slideshow came up on the big screen at the center of the auditorium.
On the screen was written [St. Saints Academy Strongest Tournament].

"From this moment on, this will be a new form of St. Saints Academy, and we shall hold a tournament to determine the strongest. The strongest in this academy shall hold top-priority rights in any and all situations."

Lackness raised his head and looked at the slides. The next slide showed a drawing of the strongest stomping on a crying loser, shouting "Keh-keh-keh, go buy me bread!" From among the students, the objections began.

"Isn't that just a bully?"

Hakase flipped to the next slide and brought up the tournament bracket.

"Those who wish to seize those rights, please challenge the strongest, Lumensia-sama."

But something was off about that tournament bracket.

Next to Lumensia, seated at the top, were written the phrases [Currently the Strongest: Ore-sama] and [I'll take you all on]. The bottom, with no individual matchups, was lumped together into a single block labeled 'Entire Student Body.'

Lackness, meanwhile, dangled separately in a corner of the bracket, on a single line connected to Lumensia.

Seeing this, the students objected again.

"Isn't that bracket weird?"

Unfazed by the criticism, Lumensia, pleased with the phrase 'the strongest, Lumensia-sama,' gave a satisfied "Hmph."

"Boooo~~ Fuzakeru na~! Konna no kiitenai zo!"
"Sou da sou da~"

At the students' fierce backlash, Lumensia glared, his temper flaring. And he shot flames at the students.

Fwoom-!!

The students flew off in every direction, screaming "Uwaaah!"

"...Gyaa gyaa urusee, buta-domo...."

Twisting his face into a fierce scowl, he bellowed loud enough to shake the auditorium.

"Anyone who defies ore-sama gets expelled!!!!!"

Lumensia was being utterly unreasonable.
Hakase, who had been watching, murmured calmly.

"Yare yare, we have lost more students again....... At this rate, the academy cannot run smoothly. Anticipating as much, I have prepared some students."

Snap!

When Hakase snapped his fingers, low-level villains who looked straight out of a '2000s Japanese anime' took the stage. They were Lumensia's followers. Among them, a bratty little girl character of the type so often used in the 2000s stuck her tongue out at Lackness.

"Lumensia-sama ga ichiban tsuyoin da mon! Beeh~~da!!"

Lumensia stood at their center like a 'rank-one gang boss' leading his crew. With his crew joining him, he grew all the more full of himself.

"I'd love to finish you off right now, but my great self wants to try enjoying this thing called school, too. See you in the finals."

Having declared this one-sidedly, Lumensia headed backstage.

"Hakase, I don't like the uniforms here. They don't match ore-sama's personal color."
"Shall I change the St. Saints Academy uniform to blue?"
"......No? Only I get to wear blue? Expel anyone who copies me."

Hearing the exchange from afar, Herbaeken quietly delivered a brutal truth about Lumensia to Eunho.

"A nasty case of special snowflake syndrome...... da ne."
"......Seems like it."


***

And so Lumensia's bulldozing school life began.

On the blackboard, the name 'Lumensia' was written in a chicken scratch that probably only he could read. Lumensia stood on the platform of the Class 1-B classroom as a transfer student. He wasn't wearing the uniform, but the same street clothes from his first appearance.

Beside him, Murasaki, as homeroom teacher, introduced Lumensia.

"Eeh-tto, our atarashii transfer student—.."
"It's Luu-mensia-sama."

Lumensia cut Murasaki off to make his own pitch.

"Your seat is.. eh-tto, ...eh?"

Before Murasaki could even assign a seat, Lumensia strode boldly to the back of the classroom, glared at the student sitting there, and gave an order.

"Oi. Doke. The very back seat by the window is ore-sama's reserved seat."

The terrified student vacated the seat, and Lumensia enjoyed his special spot. The students stared at Lumensia 'playing king' with a collective "Whoa..." Murasaki, in a cold sweat, resumed the lesson.

"Th-then let's begin class.."

Eunho and Herbaeken, watching the scene, wore the same expression as the other students.
Lackness only blinked, expressionless, and looked at Lumensia.


***

When lunchtime came, the hallway to the cafeteria was crowded with students.

"I'm hungry~"
"Our turn, mada kana~?"

As the school rules required, the students lined up in front of the cafeteria and waited their turn.

That was when it happened.

"Whoa, whoa?"
"Kyaa! Why are you pushing~?"

Lumensia appeared, leading his crew, shoving students aside.

"Doke! Ore-sama eats first! Ore-sama wa tsuyoi kara!!!"

It was an outrageous argument, but no one could stop him.
Standing at the serving counter, Lumensia heaped up a mountain of meat.
The lunch lady, watching, cried out in dismay.

"Ah, hey, student! If you take that much, there won't be any left for the next person..!!"

Lumensia glared.

"Haaan-!? What's that got to do with me!?"

Then, looking at his tray, Lumensia began to tremble.

"Haah? Nanda, this lunch.... There's no 'kimchi'!? Ore-sama can't do without something spicy!!!"

He turned around and gave Hakase an order.

"Hakase. Change the lunch supplier. To a spicy one."
"Yes, Your Highness."

The students watching from behind whispered, dumbfounded.

"He could just bring his own lunch. What's his deal."
"Tell me about it."

Among those students, the trio watched Lumensia.


***

A St. Saints Academy hallway.

Lumensia's crew began to march down the narrow hallway lined up side by side. Because of this, the other students had to press themselves flat against the walls to get by. One big student who couldn't take it anymore blocked the crew's way.

"Oi!!!! Marching side by side in a hallway this narrow is going way too far!! I don't know about any tournament, but I'll smash a pipsqueak like you to bits!"

*

Fssss....

The opponent collapsed to the floor, scorched by Lumensia's flames.
Lumensia's crew, watching, snickered.

"Sasuga Lumensia-sama~ So wild~♥"
"Kusu kusu! Weaklings should stay squashed, aru!"

The students gathered around, whispering in fear.

"No one can beat Lumensia after all~"

Just then, Herbaeken squeezed his way through the students.

"Eh~? Nani nani? What's going on? I can't see~ uwah!"

Pushed by the crowd, Herbaeken lost his balance and was flung forward.

Crash—!

Herbaeken collided with Lumensia, and they went down together.

"Uwaah! Gomen nasai!"

Herbaeken hurriedly got up.
The students around them held their breath and watched.

"He made Lumensia lose face!!"
"Eek! That guy's a hundred percent gonna get murdered!!"

Lumensia's brow twisted.
Irritated, he raised his head.

"What bastard dares do this to my great self..!!"

But the moment he came face to face with Herbaeken, Lumensia was seized by a strange sense of déjà vu.

"......"
"......"
"Oi. Haven't we met somewhere before?"

Lumensia tossed out.
Herbaeken blinked.

"Eh? No, this is the first time I've seen you."

Glaring at Herbaeken, who somehow rubbed him the wrong way, Lumensia pressed a hand to his head.

"Ah.. so annoying. That eye color, that hair color. I don't like them... Hakase."
"Yes."


*

Clang!

"Eh?"

Herbaeken, locked in an underground dungeon.
Hakase proclaimed a new school rule to Herbaeken.

"So that you do not run into Lumensia-sama, please attend school here from now on."
"Sonna~!!!"

Herbaeken rattled the bars and wailed.


***

A little later, a shocking rumor began to circulate in the classroom.

"Herbaeken got locked in the underground dungeon!?"

Hearing the rumor, Eunho's expression turned serious.

"That guy got totally marked by Lumensia, that's why!!"
"Rumor has it he got so lonely down there that he went crazy!!"
"Ehh~!? Scary~!"
"I'm transferring T_T"

The students murmured in fear.

Order at St. Saints Academy had collapsed, and the place had become nothing but Lumensia's giant playground.

Lackness, who had been watching it all, finally spoke.

"....The silver kitsune."
"...?"
"I'll leave Herbaeken to you."

Lackness walked toward the classroom door.
And declared, with resolute eyes.

"I will deal with Lumensia."

[End of Ep. 10]
