▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 3

A clear sky.
Kuro sat on a swing in the park, lost in thought.
And he recalled Miko's words.

[ "Becoming head means shouldering all of that past, too." ]

Then, the Fox Spirit's words.

[ "As you sit in the seat of the head of the Ryuo family, a moment will come when that trust wavers, all too fragile. And when that happens, you may lose that trust, and change." ]

Kuro fidgeted with his hands.

'The head of the Ryuo family. Dad pulled it off properly, but....'

A hazy memory flickered through Kuro's mind.
Yoshiyuki, Kuro's father and once the head of the Ryuo family, had been perfect.

He was by no means an approachable father.
But in Kuro's childhood, the Yoshiyuki who sat Miko, Eunho, and Kuro down before him and taught them about the world had been gentler than anyone.

"I'm not amazing like Dad."

Just as he was turning over the weight of the headship in his mind, the Fox Spirit's words suddenly came back to him.

[ "When a trial too hard to bear comes to you, Kuro, I will be with you. Because I am always watching over you." ]

Those words laid all of Kuro's anxieties to rest.

'I-I wonder if he's watching even now..?'

Feeling bashful for no reason, Kuro glanced around.

Just then, a rough-looking bunch drew closer and closer to him.


***

"U-um, this is a problem. If you keep doing that, the Black Dragon inside me will go on a rampage.."

Surrounded by delinquents, Kuro stood at attention, squirming and breaking into a cold sweat. The delinquents sneered.

"Kuhehe! This guy got eighth-grader syndrome or what~"
"Is that your 'lore' desu kaa~!?"

Despite Kuro's warning, they didn't stop.

"Keh-keh! Mou ii kara saa—"
"Hand over everything you've got www"


*

Pshuuuu—-

The situation was resolved in an instant.
Having dealt with the delinquents, Lackness returned to Kuro.

"Arigatou, Lackness..."

The delinquents scurried off, and Kuro set off on his way again.

Ever since that day, when he had first spoken with Lackness during the fight against Lumensia, Kuro no longer lost consciousness when he turned into Lackness. He could keep his memories, and could even converse with him if he wished. Of course, it was awkward between them, and Lackness was a being of so few words that Kuro didn't go out of his way to talk to him; but theirs was a relationship in which power was silently lent whenever it was needed.

'Boku, maybe I look like a pushover—I've gotten picked on every now and then since I was little. Eunho protected me every time, but..'
'.....'
'...Now that I have Lackness, Eunho doesn't need to protect me anymore, huh? Being able to protect myself, maybe that's a good thing..?'

Before long, Kuro's expression grew complicated.

'..Still, I want to use Lackness's power as little as possible.. Becoming something that isn't me.. I don't like it.'
'......'
'I-I won't look like a pushover~~!'

Kuro was making an effort.
With the face of an angry Pomeranian...

'Aah, my head's a mess~!'

Kuro ruffled his own hair.
He had a lot on his mind, which wasn't like him.


*

Swish—

Just then, someone appeared behind Kuro.

"Naa. Shounen."

At the honeyed voice, Kuro turned around.
And froze, entranced.

The man who had called to him was a young man so handsome, so beautiful, it could blind you.

He wore a black kimono, his long hair loosely tied and draped over one shoulder. A cool yet refined, curious air. One look was enough to tell he was unusual.

"The way—oshiete kurehen?"

'Uwaah...! He's incredibly good-looking..! A model!?'

When Kuro stood there in a daze for a good while, forgetting to answer the question, the man met his eyes and spoke again.

"Shounen?"
"Ah!"

Finally snapping out of it, Kuro hurriedly gave directions.

"Etto— you just go that way."
"Ookini~"

The man gave a light bow of thanks and looked around. Then, all of a sudden, ignoring the perfectly good path, he strode without hesitation straight into the bushes.

Fwump—!

"That's not the path!?"


***

"Honma akan na. Went an' showed ya an embarrassin' side o' me wa~"

The man said, with a laid-back look on his face.

"Jibun, I only just moved here, so the roads 'round these parts—yoo wakaran wa."

Kuro stared at the man, shocked.

'That was way beyond just not knowing the roads;; Despite his looks, he's a total airhead!?'

Curious about the accent amid all this, Kuro asked cautiously.

"Where are you from?"
"Hometown's Osaka, but I've been wanderin' all over for work ya de~^^"


***

"Aa, koko ya de."

The man's destination was in front of a Korean restaurant.

"As thanks—issho ni, dou ya?"
"!"


*

When they stepped inside the restaurant, Lumensia was there.
At the sight of him eating red food with bowls stacked high, Kuro marveled inwardly.

'Lumensia.. you really do love spicy food....'

Before long, a feast was laid out on Kuro's table.
Kuro spoke up cautiously.

"...Do you like Korean food?"
"Saa na, jibun mo don't rightly know, so I'm tryin' out all sorts o' things."
"Eh?"

The man went on, working his chopsticks.

"Jibun, I'm an adoptee from Korea ya de. I was so little I don't remember much—yoo oboetehen kedo. Somehow, I got this odd kinda fondness for Korea... aru chuu ka."

Hearing the private history of a man he had only just met, Kuro didn't know how to respond and just opened and closed his mouth.

'I might've just heard something huge..'

The man paused his meal and looked steadily at Kuro.

"What were ya standin' there for, kid? Looked like ya had a lot on your mind."

Kuro was flustered by the question that came out of nowhere.

"Ah, I, eetto."

'Aah~~~ where do I even start!?'

Complicated words flashed through his head: dragon, Fox Spirit, head of the family, the family's hidden affairs. He couldn't begin to explain, so after agonizing over it, Kuro answered.

"It's a long story... never mind about me, ii desu!!"
"Ehh~ that's cold~ Tell me, c'mon, you're hurtin' my feelin's~"
"Eh!?"

Kuro couldn't hide his bewilderment at the man's persistent offensive.


***

Quite some time passed; Lumensia was gone, and only a heap of empty bowls remained at his seat.

And at Kuro's table, a long story had already been exchanged, and the two had introduced themselves.

The man's name was 'Kagehira Aoi.'

Aoi, catching his breath as if satisfied, summed up what he had just heard.

"Hmm~ so to sum up, Kuro-chan's the head of a dragon-clan family who inherited the power of a dragon called 'Lackness'—an' that's got ya frettin' over all sorts o' things, huh.."

Aoi cried out as if something had just occurred to him.

"Ah, so the one who went an' boko boko'd those punks earlier was the Dragon God yattan ka!"

'He was watching!'

Kuro's face turned red.

"Y-you believe me..? A wild story like this..."

"Yep. Datte, uchi's a family of sorcerers, so I'm kinda used to that sorta thing, chuu ka~"

Aoi said it as if it were nothing, and pulled out a voodoo doll to show him.

"We sell these, too~"
"Eh!? I see...!!"


*

A brief silence fell.
Aoi gulped down water with a pensive expression.
Kuro was on pins and needles.

'Aah, I must've put Aoi-san in an awkward spot after all, with a story like mine~!'

Just then, Aoi spoke, with a serious expression.

"...Ano sa, Kuro-chan. Jibun's a family head, too, ya de."

Kuro's eyes widened.

'Onaji! A young head, same as me!'

"So I reckon I get how ya feel, Kuro-chan.. a little, anyhow..."

Aoi gave a faint smile and opened up about his own past.

"Uchi no family—as luck would have it, the elders all went an' died young, y'see. 'Round when I was fourteen, I had no choice but to take over as head."

It was a harsh story, one Kuro could not even imagine.

"The head of uchi no family, y'see, has to inherit a great sorcery that's gotta be carried out generation after generation, an' become the one who decides things for the whole family. An' the scale of it's pretty big, so—"

Aoi's gaze deepened.

"Takin' that seat scared me, an' it weighed on me. But now, bein' cold-blooded for the family's sake comes natural, I s'pose? I learned that to protect somethin', ya need all sorts o' faces."

Aoi gave a bitter smile.

"Uchi, y'see—whatever face it was, I accepted it 'cause it was for the best. For the family. An' for me."

The heavy air lasted only a moment; Aoi shifted the mood and laughed playfully.

"—tte, Kuro-chan, I know it's rough right now, but ganbatte, tte koto ya de☆"
"......"

Before Kuro could answer, Aoi seemed eager to wrap up the conversation quickly, so Kuro went along with it.

***

The two left the restaurant.

"Korean food is nakanaka oishii desu ne."
"Se ya na~"

'Aoi-san.. ...is this where we part ways..?'

Kuro dawdled conspicuously, as if reluctant to part from Aoi, and Aoi, picking up on that, spoke first.

"Ano sa, Kuro-chan. Jibun, I don't know this neighborhood, y'see. Mind if I ask ya 'bout it now an' then?"
"!"

Kuro beamed and answered as if he had been waiting for it.

"Mochiron desu!"

A brief flurry of exchanging email addresses passed.

"...Ja, mata aou na, Kuro-chan. For the date today, ookini ya de."

Aoi waved and walked away.
Kuro watched his retreating back for a long time.
Watching Aoi recede into the distance, Kuro fell into thought.

'Aoi-san.. he was.. kind of a mysterious person.'

Though they had only just met, he had felt at ease.
It was thanks to Aoi, the older of the two, being considerate of him the whole time.
He felt a little bad about that consideration, and yet, at the same time, it felt nice.

At the mere fact that he could share a bond with someone as a fellow young head, Kuro was as happy as if he had gained an ally of his own.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 3]
