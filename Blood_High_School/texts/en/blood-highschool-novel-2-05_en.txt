▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 5

After homeroom in Class 2-B.

"Eunho and Kuro are skipping club today for prior engagements, right."
"Yeah"

At Herbaeken's question, Kuro looked at Eunho.

"Eh, you have a prior engagement too, Eunho? What kind? With who?"
"!"

Eunho's shoulders visibly twitched at the unexpected question, but he soon answered nonchalantly.

"Ah, I'm gonna discuss the future of the Ryuo family with Miko~"
".....Mmm, you two have been meeting a lot lately."

Then a question suddenly struck Kuro.

"..? If it's Ryuo family business, shouldn't I be in on it too? I'm the head, after all...."
"It's fine~ It's nothing much... and you've got plans today anyway, don't you, Kuro?"

Eunho deftly changed the subject.

"I do, but.."
"Well, I'm off then!"

Kuro looked as though he had more to say, but Eunho quickly slipped out of the classroom.


***

Silence settled among those left behind.
Herbaeken turned to Shiro and asked.

"—So it'll be just us for club today. That okay?"
"...."

Shiro thought for a moment.

If he went home now, it was obvious he would be left alone with Grandpa and get picked on. In a house with neither his brother nor Eunho, there would be no one to shield him from the nagging.

"I'll kill some time at club before I go home, I will...."
"?"

Shiro, as he said it, looked drained just from imagining it. Herbaeken, not knowing the circumstances, was simply puzzled.


***

The Legend Research Club room.

Sprawled on the club room sofa, Shiro was petting and playing with Lubell. At the desk, leafing through documents, Herbaeken tossed out a remark.

"I wonder if there's a lot going on with the Ryuo family? Those two seem busy in all sorts of ways."
"Hmm~..."

Shiro spoke, searching his memory.

"Big brother's been busy lately with head stuff, and playing with a handsome big-brother guy. Eunho talks secrets in whispers on the phone with Miko~"
"Huh~ the Ryuo family must have it rough~"

Herbaeken chimed in lightly on the surface, but he was quickly sizing up the situation.

'A split?'

Shiro, meanwhile, hearing Herbaeken's answer, sank into deep thought again.

His big brother, looking at their parents' memorial portraits with a sad expression.
Everyone was kind to Shiro, but there were the business studies he struggled to understand, and the complicated family affairs.

Shiro found this heavy atmosphere uncomfortable, and no fun.

"....I don't really get the Ryuo family stuff, I don't."

It was said in a tone of 'none of my business,' and yet it was a mumble tinged with a faint sense of being left out.

Herbaeken glanced at Shiro, then looked away again.

'It's not my place to get involved.'

At present, Herbaeken was working part-time at a business affiliated with the Ryuo family, on Kuro's introduction, to make a living. His ostensible mission was to help Kuro and Eunho get Shiro used to the academy, and, out of private intellectual curiosity, to observe the traits of the dragon clan he kept close at hand. That, and no further.

For him, who had already been burned once by the people of the Herba clan, the insistence that comrades must always be of one heart and one mind no longer held any sway.

Having only just escaped his own harsh fate, Herbaeken defined 'comrade' and 'friend' coldly; and yet, taking a page from the tenacity with which Kuro and Eunho somehow dragged Shiro (Lumensia) along, he was lending his strength to helping Shiro adjust.

Herbaeken had already heard the unvarnished inside story of the Ryuo family from Eunho. That Shiro could not adjust to the Ryuo family was only to be expected.

Shiro had only just come out into the world and was devoted solely to the fun in front of him; for him to be suddenly dragged in and made to share the weight of the family's destiny was, even to an outsider like himself, an impossibility. Not that this was a matter he could carelessly weigh in on.

If there was any kinship between Shiro and himself, it was the fact that both, having been used as tools and then discarded, had to somehow 'hold out' in the new environment they had been thrown into. ......In any case, he stuck consistently to an attitude of 'Hold out on your own, and do it well.'


***

He turned his attention back to the analytical materials he had brought from his family home.

'I believed the materials my predecessors left behind were everything, but there is far too much about the dragon legend that I don't know. What bothers me most are the points that differ from the past. A dragon-clan power that doesn't appear in the legend: Lumensia.'

Herbaeken's eyes moved to copies of other ancient murals.

'And why is it that the "power of cataclysm" the dragon clan once had no longer exists? The power of fire that Lackness holds. My hypothesis that "that power," said to have destroyed the kingdom with its flames, is a part of the "power of cataclysm"—'

In that instant, Herbaeken's gaze stopped on one part of a mural copy.

It showed dragons in human form holding something aloft, as if in reverence. But the very part they were holding up had been torn away.

The moment he registered that incongruity,
Herbaeken's eyes went wide.


***

"Kenny!!!"

Herbaeken looked up, startled by Shiro's urgent cry.

"Something's wrong with Lubell!"

In Shiro's hands lay Lubell, limp.


***

"Lu~!"

When they arrived at the holy site where they had first found Lubell, Lubell soon perked up and returned to normal.

"It just ran low on sacred power."
"I'm so glad it's feeling better, I am~!!"

Herbaeken spoke with a troubled look.

"By rights, a sacred beast like Lubell should be out in the sunlight, receiving the aura of the gods. Truth is, I always meant to send it back someday, so I did train it to be able to survive here... but Lubell itself regards the Legend Research Club as the pack it belongs to, and wouldn't leave."

Herbaeken stroked Lubell with a bittersweet expression.

"Lubell, don't push yourself. You can leave anytime."
"Lu~"

Lubell only purred, happy at Herbaeken's touch.


*

Just then, an unfamiliar voice came from somewhere.

< "O you who thirst for truth, open the door of this place." >

Herbaeken looked around.

"...Don't you hear a voice coming from somewhere?"
"Eh? I don't hear anything, I don't~"

Shiro tilted his head.

"From the direction of the cathedral..."

The voice was coming from the cathedral.
Herbaeken headed there as if bewitched.

< "This one shall light your path, and guide you, that you may find true peace of heart." >

Herbaeken went in through the open doors of the cathedral.


*

Inside the cathedral, at the center of the altar, someone stood smiling.

Herbaeken was gripped by an inexplicable tension.
Shiro, on the other hand, only blinked and stared at the figure without the slightest tension.

'That Being' spoke to Herbaeken.

"If this one's voice has reached you, then you are ready to listen."

Something flashed in Herbaeken's mind.

'An aloofness far removed from humans, a mystical air. That form of address and manner of speech—could that be....!'

"You are..."

Herbaeken pointed a finger at 'That Being' and cried out, as if delivering the line 'There is always only one truth!' from [Detective Conan].

"A silver- or white-haired design, a non-human being who contemplates the multiverse or the timelines and has come to shake the protagonist's destiny, or else something like an apostle of God—!?"

It was a scene in which a legend nerd's knowledge and subculture meta-commentary were all jumbled together.

Silence.........

The Being blinked a few times, then answered.

"Ah, not quite."

His deduction having missed, Herbaeken quietly lowered his hand and switched gears as if nothing had happened.

"....Eh? Then what are you."

The Being's expression turned quite serious, and its eyes narrowed as it spoke.

"You have begun to see what you could not see while blinded by revenge, and to hear what you could not hear."
"!"
"And led by that, you came here. That is explanation enough."

Herbaeken furrowed his brow, desperately trying to guess what this being was.
The Being continued.

"Descendant of the Herba clan."
"!!"

The Being spoke as if it had already seen right through Herbaeken.
(Inwardly, Herbaeken thought, 'Isn't my deduction right after all? =.=')

"And.. Lumensia of the dragon clan."
"...."

Shiro, on the other hand, was entirely unbothered.
Unable to focus on the conversation, he looked around, taking in the sights of the cathedral. Then he noticed Lubell at his feet and scooped it up in his arms. Herbaeken sensed something off about Shiro's behavior.

"....Shiro?"
"Fueh?"
"Can't you see it? That.."
"I, I can see it, but.. did it say something to me?"

Unable to make any sense of the situation, Herbaeken frowned, his eyes darting about.

Just then, the Being spoke.

"It is all right. A common occurrence."

Shiro looked back and forth between Herbaeken and the Being, nervously reading the room, then gave an off-the-mark answer.

"I-I'll try my best to c-concentrate, I will~!"

Setting Shiro's answer aside, Herbaeken calmly put his thoughts in order.

An insight that was suspicious, and yet one he had no choice but to accept.
The being before him was acting like a guide leading the way. Feeling keenly, amid this one-sided imbalance of information, that control of the board lay with the other party, Herbaeken answered with a smile, without letting his guard down.

"...Maybe it's because I've got dragon-clan folk and a Fox Spirit around me. I've gotten a little immune."

At Herbaeken's words, the Being's expression softened.

Fwaaah—

The Being now cast off its human form and began to change into a form like light.

"If you have accepted me, shall we move on to the heart of the matter. O last descendant of the fallen kingdom."

Herbaeken got goosebumps at that sacred aura and broke into a cold sweat.

But deeper in his heart than the fear,
'A legend I've never seen or heard of—discovered!! I've totally hit the jackpot!!!!'
a thrilling shiver, as a legend nerd, was sweeping through him.

"I shall tell you the truth of the legend you thirst for."

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 5]
