▶Ep. 12 Settling the Pecking Order of the Dragon Brothers

"Damn it......"

A groan-laced curse escaped his lips.
It was Lumensia, miserably embedded in the floor, sprawled out spread-eagled.

"Ooh! Kuro won~!"

Cheers erupted from the stands.
Shadows fell over the head of Lumensia, driven into the floor.
They were his underlings. The underlings looked down at Lumensia as if he were pathetic.

"Lord Lumensia, why are you so weak? Ruri's super disappointed~."
"How shameful.... BAD."
"Ehh!? So the wish that once you became the strongest in the world you'd turn the whole world into a Panda Kingdom—that's not coming true!?"

The underlings each tossed out a line in turn, and the last one didn't say a word, expressing himself in mime instead. Lumensia, lying on the floor watching, was at a loss.

"What are they even saying...."

Just then, Hakase approached.
His eyes, which had always followed Lumensia with devotion, had at some point gone ice cold.

"Just one more step... What a shame.. Was it too much to ask, against Lackness, after all?"

Hakase went on.

"We have gained a great deal thanks to you all this while, so shall we bring this little game of comrades to a close. You are no longer needed."

Lumensia bolted upright at the betrayal.
And shouted at them.

"What did you say~!!?? Hey!! Cough it all back up, every one of you!!!"

Hakase's gang scampered off.


*

"Grrr...!!"

Just then, sensing someone behind him, Lumensia turned his head.
It was Lackness.

"I do not wish to harm one of my own kind, if it can be helped. Admit your defeat and follow the rules of this place, and I will spare your life."

Lumensia shot Lackness a glare, looking like he had bitten into something foul.

"As if I would? I'm transferring schools, so there~!?"

At those words, Lackness gave off a tremendous killing intent, his pupils narrowing to vertical slits.

"......Then I have no choice but to finish you."

Just as Lackness, without a moment's hesitation, was about to carry out that final decision,

[ "Stop it! Lackness! Don't kill him!" ]

Lackness stopped short.
That voice was Kuro, within him.
Lackness spoke to Kuro coolly.

'Kuro, I warn you. That is the wrong decision.'
'Until now, no matter what enemy came at you, I killed no one, for your sake. Because I know you do not want that.'
'But Lumensia is different from the ones before.'
'If we let him go with that power, he may gather a following and cause far greater chaos than now.'
'Someday we will have to fight him again. We must end it before then.'

At Lackness's words, Kuro fell into thought.

[ "Everything you say is right. But." ]

'What are you hesitating for? You mean to let that one go?'

[ "....No, I won't let him go. I'll try talking to him a bit more." ]

In the end, Lackness yielded to Kuro.

".....I see. Very well. But if I judge that you are in danger, I will kill him without hesitation."


***

Ssss....

Kuro returned to his own self.

"...?"

Lumensia looked at Kuro with eyes that said "What the..?"

Kuro spoke with a resolute face.

"Shiro. Come back to the Ryuo family."
"!"

Lumensia changed into Shiro.
Shiro readily showed himself at the sound of his name being called.

"But in return, don't use the power (Lumensia) recklessly."

Kuro's firm proposal made Shiro stiffen.
Then he threw a tantrum like a little child.

"I don't want that! Why do you keep rejecting my Lumensia!? That's the same as rejecting Shiro!! Shiro's mom and dad... and big brother too—I hate you all!!"

Kuro spoke sharply to the tantrum-throwing Shiro.

"That's only because our power is too strong and too dangerous, so Dad sealed it for our sake!"
"Don't know, don't know! I don't know anything about that!"
"Our power is 'a power for protecting ourselves'! So...... you have to throw away the way you've been doing things!!"

At that, Shiro thought something over by himself, then mumbled in a very small voice.

"....Without my Lumensia, Shiro is.. useless."
"...Shiro?"

When Kuro studied Shiro's sunken expression, Shiro began to babble.

"P-playing at being friends is what powerless idiots do...!!! When you have power this big, you can be as selfish as you want!!"

Those words sounded so cruel to Kuro that his heart ached.

That was when it happened.

"That's enough now."

Herbaeken walked up and intervened.

"You lost. So all the rights belong to Kuro."

Shiro scrunched up his face as though he didn't like that one bit.

"Yeah, yeah~! Quit being a sore loser and admit defeat~!!!"
"Follow the rules~!"
"Coward~ Boooo~!"

Hearing the crowd's jeers, Shiro grew miserable and started throwing another absurd tantrum.

"I-I'm transferring schools! Then I don't have to follow the rules!!! I'll go to another academy and find comrades who want my power!!"

As Shiro tried to make a dash for it, Eunho sidled up slyly and planted a little vision in his head.

"Hee~ you think that'll work~? Every time you try, Kuro will come find you, beat you to a pulp, and let everyone know you're actually suuuper weak. Then nobody'll be on your side."

In the vision Eunho planted, Lumensia was once again miserably defeated, and the friends around him were turning their backs, going "Lumensia's so lame~ let's go~" The color drained from Shiro's face. It seemed he hadn't thought that far.

"Right, Kuro?"
"!"

Kuro clumsily played along.

"That's right! I'll beat you to a pulp!"
"Fueh!! Σ(°□°)"

It was a threat that carried no menace whatsoever, but it worked on Shiro.

Flop.

"Muuuuuu.......(இ﹏இ`｡)"

'It worked...!!!'

Shiro fell to his knees in despair, and the trio, their tension draining all at once, secretly breathed sighs of relief.


*

A little later,

Clap!

Eunho clapped his hands, partly to wrap things up.

"All right! Then Shiro's going back to the Ryuo family, and~ you say to everyone, 'I'm sorry for being a nuisance.'"

Shiro slowly opened his mouth.
But in that instant, he switched back to Lumensia and yelled.

"My great self does not apologize—!!!!!!"

Thud—!!

Pressed down by Lackness's power, his head driven into the floor, Lumensia mumbled.

"I'm showwy......."
"Waaaah~!!!!"

With the roar of the crowd, the settling of the dragon brothers' pecking order was complete.


***

Afterward,
Shiro returned to the Ryuo family. Grandpa fell over in shock at the sight of his returned grandson, and Miko, hearing the news, came to the Ryuo house and scanned Shiro up and down.

Peace returned to the academy.
Shota was reinstated as principal, and Ferel came back too.

Of course, whenever Lumensia acted up, Lackness beat the tar out of him. (Lackness was the designated bully in charge of Lumensia.) After each round of discipline, Lumensia would settle down again.

As a bonus, they had Shiro join the Legend Research Club too.
Shiro was simply delighted that Lubell was cute.


*

Gazing out the classroom window with Eunho, Kuro spoke up worriedly.

"Was it okay to settle it like that? I want Shiro to really understand that his way was wrong...... but in the end, I'm only holding him back with force too......"
"Don't overthink it~~ It was the best you could do, I'm telling you?"

At Eunho's reassurance, Kuro's expression eased a little.
Just then, Herbaeken came into view outside the window.


*

"Herbaeken! Welcome back! Was everything okay back home?"
"Yeah."

Coming into the classroom and taking his seat, Herbaeken brought something up seriously.

"Kuro, I've got an urgent favor to ask. Could I get a part-time job somewhere with the Ryuo family?"
"That's fine, but...... a part-time job, all of a sudden?"
"I cut ties with my family, so I need to earn my living expenses."
"EH!?!?"

Eunho and Kuro were aghast.
Herbaeken waved it off and answered as if it were no big deal.

"It's fine, it's fine. I did steal a few things that should fetch some money. But I still need to prepare for what comes after, so-"
"Steal!? No, more importantly, do you have any idea how rough that road is..!? It's cold..!! And you go hungry..!!"

Eunho shook Herbaeken by the shoulders. (Kuro gave him a look that said, 'Eunho, how do you know so much about it..?')

Herbaeken spoke calmly.

"But we couldn't bridge our differences. I explained everything, but...... I was told to 'get out.'"
"....."

(In the flashback, the people of his clan had roared, 'You think an apology undoes it?? Just kill it already~!!!')

Eunho tactfully kept his mouth shut.
Herbaeken continued.

"I still want to stay here. I haven't learned all the legends of this academy yet, and there's a new legend, too—"

Herbaeken turned in his seat to look at Shiro at the back of the classroom.

"It's kind of on my mind."

Shiro, in the middle of playing with classmates, was sulking in a corner as if asking to be noticed, apparently displeased that not everything was revolving around him.

"...Looks like he's still adjusting."

At Herbaeken's words, Eunho sighed.

"We've forbidden him from using his power, but..."

He went on with a complicated expression.

"From what I've heard, Shiro's hardly ever been out in the world. What Hakase and his lot wanted was Lumensia's power, after all. All this time he was just used for fighting, never went to school.... and he's got no common sense."
"In other words, he's like a wild animal?"
"Yeah. We've gotta teach him everything from one to ten...... sigh...."

At Eunho's tired sigh, Herbaeken went 'Hmm....' in thought, then stood up from his seat and said breezily.

"Leave it to me."

Kuro was flustered.

"Eh! I couldn't ask you to do that......!"
"What's a little thing like this between friends."
"......!"

Hearing that, Kuro and Eunho exchanged round-eyed looks.

Click.

Herbaeken loaded.

Filled with gratitude toward the two friends who had so willingly let themselves be dragged along through his self-indulgent Legend Research Club activities, and stuck with him all the while.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

It happened in an instant.
He fired at Shiro, so that the shots just barely missed.

At the sudden gunfire, Shiro switched to Lumensia (survival mode) and, with a stupefied look, sank to the floor as his legs gave out. Herbaeken walked up to Lumensia with a refreshing smile.

"Haha. That's payback for locking me in the dungeon!"

Lumensia, belatedly furious, lashed out.

"Yyy-you bastaaard—!!!!!!!!!"

It was the very moment blue flames were about to explode with a fwoosh.

"Now, now, Shiro. Using the power of the dragon is forbidden, isn't it?"

Herbaeken said coldly. He deliberately called the Lumensia before him 'Shiro,' crushing the existence of 'Lumensia.' Then he whispered heavily into Lumensia's ear.

"I'm not kind like Kuro and the others."
"UWAAAAAAAH—--!!!!!!!!!!"

And so, at St. Saints Academy, a new legend was unfolding☆
