▶The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 6

A busy downtown district.

Among the bustling passersby, Miko and Eunho, in street clothes, were leaning against a wall on a stakeout.

Miko spoke.

"First, the head of the □□ family out in the provinces. Next, the △△ family, and then even the ○○ family......."

On a TV set up on the street, news of the related murder cases was playing.

"Several people connected to those families have died. And now it's reached the area where we live... I confirmed everything with Grandfather. Every one of them is a family that was tied up in what the Ryuo family did in the past."

Miko recalled the time, not long ago, when she had gone to see Grandpa.

She had cornered Grandpa and dug out every piece of information related to the Kagehira family. And she relayed to Eunho the shocking words Grandpa had spat out then, trembling with terror.

"The Kagehira family... he said they're 'taking revenge for those days.' Grandfather and the people of the Ryuo family are all scared out of their wits."
"...."

Eunho only listened, looking straight ahead, and gave no answer. Her explanation finished, Miko murmured with a bitter expression.

"...So the last one will be the Ryuo family after all, I wonder."
"...."

Silence. Once again, Eunho did not answer.
In this heavy silence, it was Miko who could not bear the tension, and let out a sigh.

"Haah..."

Miko gripped one of her own arms tight.

"If you go after the Kagehira family, you're sure to be hit by the backlash. Even first-rate onmyoji don't pursue them. It's a rule of this trade."

At Miko's trembling voice, Eunho turned his head.

"...Shall I go alone after all?"

Miko glanced at Eunho, then faced forward again.

"No, I have to go too. If heaven's punishment is coming to me anyway, I'll go and receive it in person."

Miko went on, with a resolute expression.

"Because I'm the daughter of Ren, the root of it all."

Eunho simply looked at Miko, expressionless.

Flash-!

That was when it happened.
Eunho sensed something, and his eyes sharpened.

"...Found it. We're following."

No sooner had he spoken than the two shot off into the crowd.
To tail the Kagehira family's oni and find where they were based.

Meanwhile,
the Being in the cathedral began to tell Herbaeken the story of the dragon clan.

< "Once upon a time, there was a land unblessed." >
< "It was a place where the countless lights of life were gradually going out... a land of death." >

Trees withered black, and the land cracked dry, without a drop of moisture.
In the fields, humans and livestock, nothing but skin and bone, lay collapsed, breathing faintly.

< "Those of that land prayed toward the heavens." >

[ "We beseech you, bestow your blessing upon this land." ]

< "In that moment, a dragon descended from the heavens." >
< "For their sake, the dragon breathed life into the land." >
< "It made the barren land fertile, and bestowed the blessings of nature." >

Water flowed through the cracks in the earth,
and the dying trees put out green leaves and covered the land.

< "Naturally, in that place, the dragon came to be worshipped as a 'god.'" >
< "As time passed, the dragons that had been in the heavens began to descend, one after another, and settled in that land." >
< "Abundant, peaceful, a paradise blessed beyond compare." >

The climate was good, and food overflowed on every side.
It was their own El Dorado, arising in this small land.
Before long, the dragons, having taken human form, stood atop a high platform. The humans exalted the dragon clan as gods and offered them endless praise.

< "Seeing this, the dragon clan thought." >

[ "The ones who can command cataclysms and move the people to rapture are we, the dragon clan...!" ]
[ "In other words, the gods are we, the dragon clan!" ]

< "The dragon clan forgot all memory of the heavens, and believed themselves to be gods." >
< "A monarchy was established, and the history of the dragon kingdom began." >

The dragon clan acted according to their own warped creed. They built stout walls for themselves alone, and constructed a system of power that no one could encroach upon. They recorded the successive dragon kings on stone tablets and extolled their reigns as the works of gods.

< "For a long time, the kingdom prospered." >
< "But suddenly," >
< "tragedy struck." >

The once-fertile land cracked, and the livestock they raised lay strewn about. Plants no longer grew, and the people, worn down by hunger, collapsed one after another, wretched figures of skin and bone.

Hidden away in his high castle, the dragon king stared at both his hands in despair.

[ "Why.. why has the power vanished..?" ]

< "The dragon clan lost the power that had been called the power of the gods." >

"!"

Cut to the present,
where something flashed through Herbaeken as he listened to the tale.

The power that had been called the power of the gods—

'That's... the power of cataclysm.'

The Being did not react to Herbaeken's agitation.
And it took up the story once more.

The desperate cries of the people, pounding on the tightly shut castle gates, rang out.

[ "Please, give us rain..!!" ]
[ "Please, bring back the abundance..!!" ]
[ "We believed.. we believed in the royal house alone. So why..!!!!!" ]

< "The people's hearts crumbled, and the monarchy declined. The land of blessing became a land of death, and was filled with chaos." >



***


Meanwhile.
Eunho and Miko were hot on the oni's heels through the streets.

Step, step.

The longer they tailed the oni, the closer the ominous aura drew. To ordinary people, the air would have felt merely eerie, but to these two, with their exceptional spiritual power, it was different. They could feel a killing intent that was gloomy and stickily, skin-crawlingly thick.

Having left the city center behind, the two soon found themselves deep in a forest.

At last, they stood before the huge house the oni had entered.
Miko and Eunho watched it for a while, then slowly stepped up before the house.

On the nameplate hung beside the main gate, the surname 'Kagehira' was written, unexpectedly, right out in the open.

Miko and Eunho exchanged puzzled looks at such boldness.

Looking again, this traditional Japanese house of dark timber was filled with a gloomy aura, yet on the outside it looked like nothing more than an ordinary building.

There was no barrier and no security of any kind.
But that very laxness, if anything, gave the impression that this was an ordinary residence.

The two held their breath and went inside.

Past the entryway, as they stepped onto the first floor of the building, a wide hall with a soaring open ceiling came into view.

On either side of the hall, spiral staircases led up to the second floor.

Miko swiftly scanned the interior of the house.
The interior, too, was all dark timber, and though lights were certainly lit here and there, the atmosphere was uncanny, as though even that light were being swallowed up. It was chilling.

Slam.

Step, step, step, step..

Just then, people appeared in a line from the second-floor corridor.

They were all dressed alike in black mourning kimono, their faces covered with black cloth. Lined up in single file on both staircases, the mourning-clad group pressed their hands together in prayer and bowed their heads slightly, as if by prior arrangement.

At the uncanny sight, Eunho and Miko tensed all over.

Creeeak-.

The central door at the front of the first floor opened, and a girl with bobbed hair, who looked about their age, appeared. The girl, too, wore a black mourning kimono, and her face was covered with black cloth.

Tap, tap.

The girl approached with demure steps.

She stopped at a fixed distance from Miko and Eunho and quietly observed the two.

"......"

One of those praying on the stairs sent her the faintest of questioning looks. As though sensing that look, the girl began, slowly, to move, as if following a set sequence.

The girl of the Kagehira family.
Miko, and Eunho.

A taut standoff took shape.
It was the beginning of an unavoidable battle.

[End of The Ryuo Clan Arc, Ep. 6]
